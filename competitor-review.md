repeated unless something new was found. Every price and claim has its source
and the date it was checked (all 3 October 2026 unless said). **Unverified**
marks anything taken from a search snippet, a third party, or a page that

## 2. Siyavula

### 2.1 What they offer

- **Practice (adaptive questions with instant marking) is for Maths
  (Gr 8-12) and Physical Sciences (Gr 10-12) only** ([siyavula.com](https://www.siyavula.com/), verified).
  There is **no Practice, question bank or auto-marked exercise for IT or CAT.**
- **Textbooks:** free IT and CAT books for **Grades 10, 11 and 12**. Each grade
  has a theory book (also readable online), a practical book (ePub only),
  a teacher's guide (ePub) and data files (zip). Sponsored by MTN; written
  by The Training Room Online
  ([IT Gr 10](https://www.siyavula.com/read/za/information-technology/grade-10),
  [CAT Gr 12](https://www.siyavula.com/read/za/computer-applications-technology/grade-12), verified in the browser).
- **Licence is unclear.** The IT Grade 10 page footer says Creative Commons
  Attribution. The CAT Grade 12 page footer says a closed copyright licence,
  all rights reserved. A search snippet says the IT books are also closed
  copyright. **Treat the IT/CAT books as free to read, not free to reuse,
  until checked.**
- **Data-free:** the site says browsing costs nothing on MTN and Cell C in
  South Africa ([siyavula.com](https://www.siyavula.com/), verified). Books can be downloaded as ePub for offline reading.
- **Teacher tools and pricing** ([siyavula.com/pricing/teachers](https://www.siyavula.com/pricing/teachers), verified in the browser):
  - Learners: **free**.
  - Access (free for verified teachers): question bank, interactive exercises, mastery practice, basic class admin.
  - Essentials: **from R2,050/yr per teacher + subject** (set work, question/mastery/assessment reports, up to 5 extra groups).
  - Advanced: **from R6,199/yr per teacher + subject** (adds school activity report, unlimited groups, priority support, training).
  - All ex VAT; 2 months free if paid yearly; price capped at 9 or more teachers; the calculator is already for 2027.
  - The page describes the plans as bringing Maths and Science practice into the classroom. The pricing page's menu lists CAT and IT, but only as textbooks.
- **AI features:** none found on the site or in searches.

### 2.2 What a lesson looks like (IT Grade 11, Chapter 2 "Software")

Read online in the browser:
[2.1 Types of operating systems](https://www.siyavula.com/read/za/information-technology/grade-11/software/02-software).

- The chapter opens with a list of units, learning outcomes, a short
  introduction story (Android's rise), a "Did you know" box and a "New words"
  glossary box.
- A recap section is followed by a **revision activity** of six short written
  questions. The first reads: "Name the TWO broad categories of software."
- Each unit is prose with tables and figures, then a numbered activity
  (for example a crossword, or a work-shadowing scenario asking what an
  interpreter is and how it differs from a compiler).
- There is a described animation and a **QR code to a YouTube video**.
- **Answers are not on the page and nothing is marked.** Activities are
  print-style questions for a teacher to mark. Model answers are in the
  teacher's guide (ePub). No input boxes, no instant feedback.

### 2.3 How the IT books cover programming

- **Delphi only. No Java.** The practical books are CAPS (DBE) books.
- **Grade 10 practical book** (ISBN 978-1-928388-50-0, 2019): starts with
  algorithms and flowcharts, then Delphi: the IDE, components and
  properties, first projects (search snippet, **unverified**).
- **Grade 12 practical book** (ISBN 978-1-928388-56-2, 2019): four chapters:
  programming fundamentals, object-oriented programming, two-dimensional
  arrays, databases and SQL. It assumes a computer with the Delphi IDE.
  Data files for pupils and solution folders for teachers (search snippet, **unverified**).
- **Grade 11 and 12 theory books** cover database management and design
  (Gr 11 ch. 8-9; Gr 12 ch. 1-2) and compilers/interpreters (Gr 11 ch. 2),
  but no code (verified, chapter lists in the browser).
- **Depth and exercises:** the practical books are ePub downloads only, so
  their exercises could not be read online without downloading a file.
  **Not checked.** From the structure: exercises are worked on in Delphi
  on the pupil's own machine; solutions sit in the teacher's data files.
  **Nothing is marked automatically.**
- **CAT:** the same pattern. Theory online (Gr 12: 9 chapters, the last one
  being the information-management PAT); practical book, teacher's guide and
  data files as downloads.

## 6. BestLessons: unique selling points and weaknesses

Written by the main session, 3 October 2026, from sections 1-4 above,
[market-research.md](market-research.md), [costs-research.md](costs-research.md)
and [platform.md](platform.md).

### Unique selling points

1. **Instant marking of IT and CAT work - nobody else does it.** Siyavula's
   Practice covers only Maths and Science; Brainline's teachers and a tutor mark
   later; textbooks print answers in a teacher's guide. BestLessons marks a
   written theory answer, a piece of code or an SQL query in seconds, with the
   reason.
2. **Code runs in the browser.** Pascal and Java consoles and SQL runners need
   no install, no Delphi licence, no JDK set-up - a school lab, a home laptop or
   a borrowed machine all work. Every competitor needs software installed.
3. **CAPS and IEB in one place.** A pupil picks their board; Pascal for CAPS,
   Java for IEB, theory for both. Siyavula is Delphi/CAPS only; Brainline is
   IEB/Java only.
4. **Written by a practising IT teacher and textbook author**, to the current
   syllabus and exam analyses (caps-*.md, ieb-*.md) - not a generic coding
   course retrofitted to SA.
5. **Price.** R349 a subject a year is about a tutor hour and a twentieth of
   Brainline. The lessons themselves are free, so a parent can see the whole
   course before paying.
6. **A teacher's tool as well as a pupil's.** Groups, invitations, class
   results, a year plan per CAPS or ISASA calendar, PAT pre-checks against the
   IEB rubric. Siyavula has no IT/CAT dashboards.
7. **Built for 30-minute class periods**, with the extras pupils actually use:
   games, word practice from the glossary, video questions, achievements, a
   Harvard reference builder, read-aloud and a dyslexia font.
8. **Safe AI for minors.** Pupil text is treated as data, off-topic or
   "ignore your instructions" answers are refused and scored 0, every call is
   costed; no chat with a general AI.

### Weaknesses

1. **One person.** Content, code, support and server all depend on Chris. A
   school buying a licence will ask what happens if he is ill or stops. (Fix:
   documented deploy and backups exist; a named second admin and a support
   address help.)
2. **No human teacher behind the pupil.** Brainline and tutors give a person;
   BestLessons gives the pupil's own teacher (school route) or nobody. AI
   marking is good but will sometimes be wrong, and a parent paying will
   complain.
3. **CAT is not ready.** The CAT course is a plan; its practical paper (Word,
   Excel, Access - 25% of the mark) cannot be marked online, and there is no
   CAT product to sell yet.
4. **Pascal, not Delphi.** CAPS schools teach Delphi (a GUI IDE); the console is
   Free Pascal. Close, but a CAPS teacher may see it as "not our language" -
   say clearly what transfers and what does not.
5. **No brand, no reviews, no track record.** Siyavula, The Answer Series and
   Brainline are known names; BestLessons has one school. Results from De La
   Salle (with consent) are the first proof.
6. **Online only.** No offline mode, and data costs matter to many CAPS
   families; Siyavula is zero-rated on MTN and Cell C. (Worth asking networks
   about zero-rating later.)
7. **Single server, one country.** One VPS (about 150 pupils online at once
   before an upgrade). An outage on exam eve would hurt. Upgrades are cheap
   (costs-research.md section 4), but they have to be done before they are
   needed.
8. **Costs in dollars.** AI is billed in US dollars; a weak rand raises costs
   (about 15% per 15% move) while prices are in rand. The per-plan AI budget
   limits the damage.
9. **No PAT or exam service.** Brainline sets and marks PATs and registers IEB
   exams; BestLessons only pre-checks a PAT part. It supplements school; it is
   not a school.
10. **The free tier is generous.** Everything except AI marking is free, so the
    paid offer must make AI marking feel essential - mainly theory answers and
    exam season. The free trial and the Grade 12 exam pass carry that.

### What this means

- **Position:** "the IT/CAT practice and marking that schools, textbooks and
  tutors don't give you, for the price of a tutor hour" - a supplement to
  school, not a replacement.
- **Lead with IT** (ready now); sell CAT once its theory courses are live.
- **Schools first for trust**: a school licence brings a teacher who vouches
  for it and solves weakness 2 (a human behind the pupil).
