# Market research: pricing BestLessons

Research for pricing BestLessons (bestlessons.co.za): IT and CAT, Grades 10-12, CAPS and IEB.
Done 3 October 2026. All prices were read on 3 October 2026 unless a row says otherwise.

**How to read this file**

- Prices are in rand and include VAT unless marked "ex VAT".
- Exchange rates: **US$1 = R16.20** (September 2026 average about R16.19, end of month about R16.20, [exchangerates.org.uk](https://www.exchangerates.org.uk/USD-ZAR-spot-exchange-rates-history-2026.html)). **£1 = R21.74** (September 2026 average, [exchangerates.org.uk](https://www.exchangerates.org.uk/ZAR-GBP-exchange-rate-history.html)).
- **Verified** means the price was read on the publisher's, retailer's or provider's own page.
- **Unverified** means it came from a search-result snippet, a third-party review or a news article, or from a PDF whose table layout was hard to read. Check these before you quote them.

### 1.4 Are e-book licences annual?

Mostly they are time-limited, and the length varies:

- Snapplify Study Opportunities e-books: 465 days.
- Focus CAT ePDF: 425 days ("1-year licence").
- Funworks Delphi e-book on Unisa Ebooks: 1,065 days (about 3 years).
- Funworks "Digital Solutions" for teachers: one academic year, January to December, renewed every year.

In practice, an e-book is a one-year rental at roughly 40-80% of the print price.

- **IEB schools (Java):** Funworks *Exploring IT: Java Programming* plus *Exploring IT: Theory* for each grade, and Study Opportunities *Computers, part of your life* for CAT. This is shown on the Brainline Gr 10 booklist, which gives both hardcopy and e-book ISBNs ([Brainline booklist](https://www.brainline.com/booklist/comprehensive-booklist/grade-10-comprehensive-booklist), verified), and on the Curro Choice and Teneo IEB Gr 10 lists at DC Books (verified). - **IEB yearly textbook spend for IT:** about **R755 at publisher prices, up to about R1,000 at retail** (Theory + Java).
- **Public CAPS schools (Delphi):** the usual books are Study Opportunities *IT is gr8!* or Funworks *Exploring IT Delphi* for IT, and Study Opportunities *Computers, part of your life* (also in Afrikaans as *Rekenaars, deel van jou lewe*) for CAT. ## 4. Pricing patterns

**Common models**

1. **Per book per year (rental e-book).** Textbook publishers use this: R70-R345 per book, licence 425-1,065 days.
2. **Per learner per year, all subjects.** Used by The Answer Series (R299-R599), Khanmigo (R713) and Codecademy (R2,300+).
3. **Monthly subscription.** Used by Paper Video (R99-R299/month list), Codecademy and Khanmigo. The yearly plan is usually priced at 10 months (a "2 months free" style discount): Siyavula says "2 months free", Khanmigo's $44 is 11 months, and Codecademy is about 20% off.
4. **Per teacher + subject (school pays).** Used by Siyavula: R2,050-R6,199 per year ex VAT. With 30 pupils that is about R68-R207 per pupil.
5. **Per user platform licence (school pays).** Used by Snapplify Engage Enterprise: R250 per user per year.
6. **Per school per subject (unlimited pupils).** Used by Educake, Century and Seneca in the UK.
7. **Per subject per year, taught.** Used by online schools: R5,000-R10,000 (Brainline, Curro Choice, UCT extra subject).

**How SA parents and schools pay**

- **School fees are paid either yearly (EFT, with a discount) or monthly by debit order.**
  - St Stithians: yearly EFT by 11 January, or 10 monthly debit orders ([fee schedule](https://www.stithian.com/uploads/files/Admissions/2026-Fees-_Admissions.pdf), verified).
  - Curro: 12 monthly debit orders, or 5% off for paying 12 months ahead and 2.5% off for 6 months ahead ([Curro Foreshore fee letter](https://www.curro.co.za/media/rgolrb0c/curro-foreshore-2026-high-school-fee-letter-finalv3-for-web.pdf), verified).
  - UCT Online: monthly, or 15% off for paying a year up front (verified).
- **Typical discount for paying yearly: 5-20%.**
- **Extra charges appear as once-a-year line items billed in February or March.** Examples: workbooks R65-R660, stationery packs about R700, and a "book fee" of about R140/month at Curro Foreshore (read from the Curro fee letters; the table layout is jumbled, so treat the book fee as unverified). A school buying BestLessons could bill it to parents the same way, as a small yearly "digital resource" line.
- **Sibling discounts are standard:** UCT 5%, Curro up to 10%. Family plans are rare in SA edtech; Brilliant has one.
- **Card payment and monthly cancellation suit consumer edtech.** Debit orders suit school billing. That parents prefer monthly is an inference from the fee letters (every school offers monthly). No survey data was found, so this is **unverified**.

## 6. What this means for pricing

BestLessons gives away all lessons and self-marked questions free. The paid part is AI marking and analysis. So the paid tier competes with "what a parent or school pays for help beyond the textbook", not with the textbook itself.

### 6.1 Reference points found

- The free floor is real: Siyavula IT books, DBE CAT guide, java-teacher.com, Khan Academy, CS50.
- An e-book for one subject for one year costs **R70-R345**.
- Print textbooks for one subject for one year cost **R275-R1,000**: CAPS at the low end, IEB Java at retail at the high end.
- An all-subjects e-book bundle (The Answer Series) costs **R299-R599 per learner per year**.
- An AI tutor (Khanmigo) costs about **R713 per learner per year**, and about **R570 per pupil per year** for districts (2023 price).
- Coding platforms (Codecademy, Brilliant, Coursera) cost **R2,300-R3,900 per year**.
- A taught online subject (Brainline, Curro Choice) costs **R5,000-R10,000 per year**.
- Tutoring costs **R300-R500 per hour**, or about R16,000 per year at one hour a week.

### 6.2 Suggested anchors: public (CAPS) school families

| Unit | Cheapest | Typical | Premium | Reasoning |

| **One course** (e.g. Pascal Gr 11 or IT Theory Gr 12), AI marking, per year | R99 | R149 | R249 | Equal to or below one IT e-book (R73-R166) or one print book (R200-R350) |
| **One course, monthly** | R19 | R29 | R49 | About one-eighth of the yearly price. Paper Video's list price was R99/month and up, for whole subjects |

Public schools have small budgets, so price per class or teacher as well (e.g. R2,000-R3,000 per teacher per subject per year, in line with Siyavula Essentials at R2,050) |

### 6.4 Other points

- **Offer a yearly price at about 10 times the monthly price** ("2 months free"). Every SA school and edtech price list seen offers a yearly discount of 5-20%.
- **Add a sibling discount of 5-10%.** It is standard in SA school billing. A family plan is less common.
- **Matric (Gr 12) can carry a higher price** than Gr 10-11, because Brainline charges R7,850 for Gr 12 against R6,500 for Gr 10-11. An exam-season pass (for example 3 months, about R99-R199) would catch late buyers.
- **Do the e-book comparison in the copy.** "Less than the e-book, and it marks your code" is a claim the numbers support for CAPS families.
