Course plan · eng10 · eng11 · eng12 · school year 2027

English Home Language 10-12

Three courses, one per grade, each serving two board streams: lessons are shared, or tagged for CAPS or IEB pupils only. A pupil's path is about 100 lessons of about 30 minutes, around three a week, with their class's set texts filtered in. Grades 10 and 11 use the Grade 12 exam format and mark distribution for each board; only the text lengths scale down.
