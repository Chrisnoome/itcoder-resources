# Grade 12 English Home Language (`eng12`): the lesson list - PLAN

**Grade 12 · school year 2027 · status: PLAN (11 October 2026).** No lessons written yet.

One course for both boards. Every pupil sees the `shared` lessons. A CAPS pupil also sees the
`caps` lessons, an IEB pupil the `ieb` lessons. On top of that, each pupil sees only the set
texts chosen for them in the "my set works" setting (a per-pupil choice, like
`pupils.syllabus`; not built yet).

The year follows the DBE Grade 12 ATP (2023/24, kept for 2026 by S19/2025). Grade 12 has 30
teaching weeks:
- Term 1 has 11 weeks.
- Terms 2 and 3 have 7 teaching weeks each. Term 2 ends with the mid-year exam, Term 3 with
  the trials. The IEB prelims sit where the trials sit.
- Term 4 has 4 weeks of past-paper work, then the finals.

The skills strands are taught once for everyone, and each board's exam format is drilled in its
own stream. There is a unit for every 2027 set text (seven units, all the same shape), plus a
per-poem set for each board's anthology. IEB pupils also get film study (the 4th genre), the
CAT, the SBA extended writing and prelim practice.

## Conventions for every lesson (stated once)

- **About 30 minutes** per lesson. Each lesson has at least one **gated guided try-it**, and
  progress is gated (`'sequential' => true`).
- **Margin extras** in every lesson:
  - a Ratel (language) or Quill (writing) drawing, with the joke as its caption;
  - an aside ("Examiner's eye" or "Layout rule");
  - a factoid.

  In literature lessons, Ratel helps with evidence and Quill helps with the essay. Each work
  has its own look (eng-art-style.md).
- **Marking:** Jev marks first, against the board's rubric, and Claude marks when Jev is unsure.
  - CAPS: App A-D.
  - IEB: D18 (transactional), the 7++ to 1 literary-essay bands, and the generic SOLO
    descriptors for short answers.
  - Summary word counts are checked by code, so the cut-off rule is exact (see open point 5
    for the IEB rule).
- **Kids' media world.** Our own comprehension, summary, visual-literacy and language texts
  are set in teen media: group chats, short-video apps, game streams, memes and made-up apps
  and brands. There are no newspapers, no TV news and no real brands.
  - **The tension, noted once:** the real papers use op-eds and real ad campaigns (C §4,
    I §3). Our texts copy the register, length and question types of those papers, but not
    their sources.
- **Practice papers** are written by us in the Nov 2023-2025 pattern of each board. The real
  past papers are the teacher's to hand out; we don't host them.
- **Orals:** the course coaches the skill and sets practice tasks. The pupil records
  themselves, and the teacher marks the formal oral. (Whether the platform can take an audio
  upload is still to be checked.)

### Where each set-text unit runs

All seven units have the same 14-lesson shape for the pupil:
- U1 opener;
- U2 context;
- U3-U7 five reading guides;
- U8-U9 characters;
- U10 themes;
- U11 motifs and language;
- U12-U13 board practice;
- U14 final unit task.

*Hamlet* carries both boards' versions of U12-U14 (17 entries in all).

|---|---|---|---|

## Open points

1. **The CAPS Nov 2027 list is not confirmed.** E11/2024 covers exams only up to June 2027. A new
   DBE circular is needed. This plan assumes the same five texts and 12 poems carry over.
2. **The IEB SBA is changing.** The 2026 user group says SBA Sections A and B are "replaced by
   writing process task", with revised rubrics. The revised SAG is not in the sources (the PDF
   is still the March 2021 version). ieb-extended-1/2 and the coversheet-dependent tasks may
   need to be reshaped once the IEB publishes it.
3. **The IEB CAT 2027 theme is not known.** It comes in the March delivery. ieb-cat is
   written to the 2026 poetry-skills format and is generic to any theme. If the 2027 CAT is
   not a poetry task, the lesson has to be rewritten.
4. **The first person in IEB essays.** The P2 2024 report discourages it, but the UG
   literature-essay session endorses the "authorial first person", and the SAG allows
   both. Proposal: teach the third person as the default and say the first person is allowed.
   Chris to decide.
5. **The IEB summary cut-off.** The SAG D20 says to stop reading 10 words over. The memos
   penalise from 94 words and stop reading at 100. The user group proposes dropping half
   marks. The word-count code needs one rule. Proposal: follow the memos.
6. **CAPS Task 8 can be an exam or a test,** at the province's choice. Our practice is the
   full P1 + P2 papers, which covers both.
7. **CAPS Task 9 can be the assignment or contextual + essay.** Both routes are supported:
   caps-lit-assignment, and novel U14. We don't know which route the school takes, so a
   teacher setting may be needed.
8. **The films are the school's choice.** We need an "our films" setting, like "my set
   works", so that film-06 to film-09 can name the films.
9. **Poems we couldn't read.** The anthologies (*Imagined Worlds*, *Clusters*) are not in
   the sources. The poems with generic titles here ("speaker, tone and the turn") get their
   real focus when the lesson is written from the anthology:
   - Dickinson, Schreiner, Press;
   - Kassam, Dowling, Gray, Mann, Karibo, Afrika.

   Also confirm which Dickinson poem *Imagined Worlds* calls 'The Shipwreck'.
10. **Copyright checks before showing anything in full:**
    - recent public domain in SA, still in copyright elsewhere: Huxley, Plath, Pound, Plomer,
      Dylan Thomas;
    - translations: Jonker's (in copyright).
11. **"My set works" doesn't exist yet.** The per-pupil text choice (like `pupils.syllabus`)
    has to be built before units can be filtered.
12. **Audio upload for orals** is still to be checked (from the course plan).
13. **The Term 2 load is heavy:** 29 lessons for a CAPS pupil and 33 for an IEB pupil in 7
    teaching weeks. If that is too much, move the novel's U6-U7 into Term 3 W1. Task 9 would
    then fall a week later.
