## Looked for but not found

- **Gr 12 Examination Guidelines newer than 2021.** The DBE index still lists only "2021 Examination Guidelines". No 2022–2026 Home Language edition was found on DBE or WCED. Note that the 2021 guideline still refers to Circular E39 of 2016 for literature; use E11 of 2024 instead.
- **ATP for 2024, 2025 or 2026.** None exists. Circulars S15 of 2024 and S19 of 2025 keep the 2023/24 ATPs for 2025 and 2026.
- **2020 revised ATP for English HL FET.** Not found as a separate file. The 2021 National Recovery ATPs (downloaded) replaced it.
- **DBE-hosted copy of Circular E11 of 2024.** It is not on the DBE National Circulars page. The WCED annexure copy was used instead (it is the DBE circular itself).
- **Covering pages of Circular E8 of 2022.** WCED hosts only its Annexure A (the lists).
- **DBE Gr 10–11 English HL exam exemplar papers.** The 2013, 2017 and 2019 Grade 11 exemplar pages have no English HL papers. The only Gr 10–11 HL exemplars are the 2025 literature-assignment exemplars (in Assessment/).
- **2024 CAPS amendments for languages (a new "Section 4").** None exists. The only 2024 CAPS amendments are for CAT and IT (Section 3). The current Programme of Assessment is the abridged Section 4 (Gr 10–11 2019; Gr 12 2020) as changed by the weightings in S33 of 2022 and S8 of 2023.
