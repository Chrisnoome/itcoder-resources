# English Home Language Grade 10 (`eng10`): the lesson list - PLAN

**Grade 10 · school year 2027 · status: PLAN (11 October 2026).** No lessons are
written yet.

Grade 10 opens the FET phase, so the course starts with orientation: each board's exam
format, the four strands, and how marking works. After that it follows the DBE Grade 10
ATP week by week (CAPS ref §2) and builds towards the ten Grade 10-11 formal tasks (CAPS
ref §1.7). IEB pupils follow the same order, with IEB-only lessons where their papers
differ. Both boards practise in the **Grade 12 paper format** at Grade 10 lengths:
- CAPS: P1 70, P2 80 (poetry + novel + drama), P3 100, orals 50.
- IEB: P1 100, P2 100 (Shakespeare essay + novel essay + transactional), SBA and orals.

Literature comes from a **teacher-selected library**. Each class gets one Shakespeare
play, one novel and the poetry set, and every set-text unit has the same shape, so the
units are interchangeable. The **literary essay** is new in Grade 10 (IEB SAG). It is
built up in seven steps across the year, from the single analytical paragraph in Term 1
to the full timed essay in Term 4. There are 153 entries in the course. A CAPS
pupil sees 99 lessons and an IEB pupil sees 102 lessons, of about 30 minutes
each.

[CAPS/caps-ehl-reference.md](CAPS/caps-ehl-reference.md) ("CAPS ref"),

## Rules that apply to every lesson

These are stated once here and not repeated per lesson.
- **Gated practice and margin extras.** Every lesson has at least one gated guided
  try-it, and margin extras: a Ratel (language) or Quill (writing) drawing with the joke
  as its caption, an aside ("Examiner's eye" / "Layout rule"), and a factoid.
- **Art.** Language lessons use L1 Tracing overlay. Writing lessons use W1 Drafting
  table. Each literature work and poem has its own look. The Grade 10 accent is cobalt
  `#2747d6`, and Quill's crest has four quills.
- **Marking.** Jev marks first, with Claude when Jev is unsure, using each board's
  rubrics:
  - CAPS: App A (poetry essay), App B (literary essay), App C (essay) and App D
    (transactional), with memo-style points for short answers.
  - IEB: D18 (transactional), D19 and the memo bands 7++ to 1 (literary essay), D20
    (summary), and the SOLO generic descriptors for short answers.
  - Summary word counts are checked by code, so each board's cut-off rule is applied
    exactly:
    - CAPS: stop reading at the last sentence within 90 words.
    - IEB: penalise from 94 words and stop reading at 100.
- **Kids' media world.** Non-literary practice texts, scenes and prompts are set in
  teen media: group chats, short-video apps, games, streams, memes and made-up apps.
  There are no newspapers, no TV news and no real brands.
  - *The tension:* the real papers use op-eds, feature articles and real ad campaigns
    (CAPS ref §4; IEB ref §3). Our texts copy their register, length and question
    types, but set them in teen media. Format names such as "newspaper article" (CAPS
    TT list) and "editorial" (IEB) are taught as formats, written for a made-up school
    or app publication.
- **Quoting.** Public-domain texts are shown in full. The library below has public-domain
  texts only. If a protected text is added later, pupils read their own copy and lessons
  quote only short extracts.
- **Gr 10 lengths:**
  - comprehension 500-600 words;
  - summary source 280 words, summary 80-90 words;
  - CAPS essays 300-350 words, literary essay 300-350 words, poetry essay 150-200
    words, transactional 180-200 words;
  - listening text about 200 words, or a 2-minute clip.
  - The IEB sets no Grade 10 output lengths. The IEB lengths used here are proposals
    (see Open points):
    - literary essay about 400 words;
    - transactional body 200-250 words;
    - extended writing 400-500 words;
    - film task 600-800 words.

## Texts: the starter library

their class's texts. Teachers can also **request** a text that isn't in the library,
which notifies Chris. De La Salle's texts are still to come from Chris.

**Copyright rule.** South Africa protects a work for the author's life + 50 years,
counted to the end of the calendar year of death. All six library texts are public
domain in SA. The notes flag where another country's term is longer, which matters only
if the platform hosts the full texts outside SA (see Open points).

## The set-text unit shape (the same for every text)

| Step | Lessons | Stream |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Opener and context (period, author, setting) | 1 | shared |
| 2. Reading guides, by act or chapter group | 5 | shared |
| 3. Characters | 1 | shared |
| 4. Themes | 1 | shared |
| 5. Motifs, imagery and language | 1 | shared |
| 6. CAPS board practice: (a) contextual questions, two extracts (25); (b) literary essay, 300-350 words (App B, 25) | 2 | caps |
| 6. IEB board practice: (a) unpacking a critic's quotation; (b) literary essay of about 400 words against that quotation (D19 bands) | 2 | ieb |
| 7. Unit task: a timed, board-neutral task. Part 1 is a knowledge check (sequence, who-said-it, context). Part 2 is one analytical paragraph marked by Jev. | 1 | shared |

Each text has **14 entries**, and a pupil sees **12**. The Shakespeare unit is split:
Part 1 (opener and Acts 1-3) in Term 1, and Part 2 (the rest) in Term 2. The novel unit
runs through Term 3.

## The lesson list

- **Stream:** `shared` = everyone; `caps` / `ieb` = that board only.
- **Text:** `—` = no set text; a `text:` tag = only pupils whose class has that text.
- **Covers:** "T1 W2-3" is the ATP term and week. "CAPS ref §" and "SAG §" point to the
  digests.
- **Prepares for:**
  - CAPS: Task n, or the paper and question (P1 Q1 ...).
  - IEB: the SBA section, oral task or paper question.

## Open points

1. **CAPS P2 in Grade 10.** The real Gr 10 end-of-year P2 is poetry plus a drama
   contextual and a drama essay. There is no novel (CAPS ref §1.6-1.7). Our Grade 12
   format adds a novel, as Chris decided. A school writing the provincial Gr 10 paper
   would need the drama-only version, so `caps-p2-final` could offer both. To confirm.
2. **The mid-year P2 has no novel for either board**, because the novel is taught in
   Term 3:
   - CAPS mid-year uses the Gr 10 ATP P2 (seen 20, unseen 10, drama contextual 25,
     drama essay 25).
   - IEB mid-year P2 is the Shakespeare essay plus transactional writing (70 marks), or
     the teacher adds a second Shakespeare topic.
   - To confirm.
3. **The IEB sets no Grade 10 lengths or SBA** (IEB ref §1.9). These are proposals
   scaled from Grade 12:
   - literary essay about 400 words (Gr 12: about 600);
   - transactional body 200-250 words (Gr 12: 250-300);
   - extended writing 400-500 words (Gr 12: 500-600);
   - film study: one film, 600-800 words (Gr 12: two films, 1000-1200).
   - Chris or an IEB school should confirm them.
4. **The IEB SBA is changing in 2026.** Extended writing (Sections A and B) is to be
   replaced by a "writing process task" (IEB ref §0, §4). The `ieb-extended-*` lessons
   are written as process writing so they can carry over, but the revised SAG is still
   needed.
5. **The CAT in Grade 10.** The CAT is a Grade 12 cluster task, with its theme sent in
   March. `ieb-cat-poetry` copies the 2026 poetry format as practice only, and the theme
   will change each year.
6. **Critics' quotations.** The IEB lessons need real, checked critics' quotations on
   each text, or statements clearly labelled as ours. Don't invent attributions.
7. **US copyright for *Animal Farm*.** It is public domain in SA but protected in the
   USA until 2040. If the platform or its CDN serves from the USA, show extracts only,
   or check where it is hosted. The same applies to the "could add" texts by Shaw and
   Steinbeck.
8. **Orals evidence.** The pupil records and the teacher marks. The platform still
   needs an audio upload (plan, "Marking").
9. **De La Salle's texts.** They are not yet received. Each one needs a status check and
   a unit in the 14-entry shape. *A Thousand Splendid Suns* is in the "could add" list
   and has no unit yet.
10. **The 40:60 per-paper split for Gr 10-11** is not in the sources (CAPS ref §0). The
    `caps-exam-map` lesson explains the task list and the papers, and leaves the
    percentage out until the split is confirmed.
11. **Path length.** A typical CAPS pupil sees 99 lessons and a typical IEB pupil
    sees 102. The IEB path is longer because of film study and the CAT. If it needs
    trimming, `ieb-cat-speech` could merge into `prepared-speech`.
