# Grade 9 social media art style: neon notifications meet the slot machine

Chris, 9 October 2026, from the board (https://claude.ai/artifact/SwqRT3UQ7giz4bxHjd6Czo):
**"combination of 1 and 2"** - the neon notification look and the slot-machine metaphor.

- **Ground:** a deep purple-black `#151026` screen, phones drawn as rounded slabs `#221a3d`.
- **Neon:** hot pink `#ff4fa3` and cyan `#3ee8ff` glow lines; red notification badges
  `#ff3b3b` with white counts ("99+"). Hearts, thumbs-up and bells as the recurring icons.
- **The slot machine** is the central metaphor, drawn in the same neon: the like button and
  "pull to refresh" as a lever, feeds as spinning reels, notifications as coins dropping.
  Every lesson's opener uses it; persuasive-design figures label each feature as a machine
  part (lever = pull to refresh, reels = infinite scroll, jackpot light = likes).
- **Type:** Bungee for labels and badges (short words only), the platform font for prose.
- One accent per figure: pink for what grabs attention, cyan for what the pupil controls.
- No real app logos or brand names in drawings; the film is named in prose only.
