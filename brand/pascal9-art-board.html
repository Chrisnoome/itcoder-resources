Station Kestrel: pick an art style

The same station, the same oxygen-counter screen and the same mission card, drawn four ways. Choose a style, a mascot, and whether the "screen they need" pictures follow the style or stay one plain terminal look.

ABlueprint and amber CRT

Engineering drawings on blueprint grid for the station and its systems; amber-on-black terminals for every program screen. Calm, technical, reads well on a projector. Pairs with realistic coloured-pencil or painted scenes from ComfyUI for the crew and Earth views.

Mascot: the station's maintenance robot

One light on the robot shows the mission's status: amber while the system is down, green when the pupil's program passes. Drawn in whichever style is chosen.

Bolt

A floating drone, one big eye-light and two little rotors. Curious, cheerful, gets in the way.

Widget

A boxy wheeled helper with one long arm and an antenna light. Practical, a bit grumpy, always carrying a tool.

Tiko

A six-legged hull crawler that walks on the outside of the station, magnets on its feet. Quick and twitchy, like a gecko.

Kip

A small humanoid in a work suit with a chest light; waves, points, holds up signs. The easiest to draw doing things.

The "screen they need" pictures

Every mission shows the terminal the crew wants before the pupil writes a line. These are drawn from real fpc runs of the model answers, with the Crt colours kept.

Option 1: one terminal look for the whole course (black, light text, Crt colours), so it matches what pupils see in the Run box. Option 2: the terminal dressed in the chosen style, as in each panel above.
