# CAT course art style: marker, highlighter and Clicky

Chris, 5 October 2026: "the CAT course is going to need its own distinctive art style. we want the
same simple clarity of diagrams, etc but a different feel and personality." From nine styles on the
board (https://claude.ai/artifact/RjE1xuHudP9idVjUypYNFF) he chose **marker and highlighter, with
Clicky the mouse pointer as the margin character** (the "1 + 9" board).

The other courses keep the blue pen (content-voice-and-pedagogy.md §5b). Only CAT uses this.

## The look

- **Line:** black felt-tip, `#1b1b1b`, `stroke-width` about 3-3.5 at 680 wide, round caps and joins.
  Boxes are drawn a little off-square (corners 1-2 px out), as if by hand.
- **Highlighter, not fills:** colour goes on as a highlighter swipe behind text or a shape, slightly
  skewed, never as a tinted box. Yellow `#ffe94d` for "look here" and what moves or changes; pink
  `#ff9ecf` for what is fixed or the answer; blue `#8fd3ff` for a third group. At most two
  highlighter colours in one diagram where possible.
- **Sticky notes:** a key value, a setting or a list sits on a yellow sticky note (`#fff27a`,
  rotated 2-5°, a soft shadow, sometimes a strip of clear tape). Use them sparingly: one per figure.
- **Type:** labels in **Patrick Hand**; headings, big numbers and the doodle captions in **Permanent
  Marker**. Never set long text in Permanent Marker.
- **Paper:** figures sit on white; the page around them can stay as it is.

## Clicky

The CAT mascot: a white mouse-pointer arrow with two dot eyes and a mouth, drawn in the same felt-tip,
with a highlighter swipe behind him. Shape (60 x 92): `M0 0 L0 78 L19 62 L33 92 L48 85 L34 56 L60 55 Z`.

- **Where:** in the margin doodles (one-line joke captions, as in §5b), and now and then in a
  figure to point at the one thing that matters, with a short speech bubble. Never more than once
  per figure, and never where he hides part of the diagram.
- **Poses** (on the board): pointing (arm out, finger-dot), cheering (arms up, open mouth),
  puzzled ("?" beside him), clinging on (to a `$`, a lock, a folder). Add poses as jokes need them.
- **Voice:** short, cheerful, never sarcastic about the pupil.

## Models

The board has the same subjects drawn in this style: absolute references (`=A2*$E$1` copied down),
a file path as nested folders, a mail merge (one letter + one list -> one letter per row) and a
column chart with the answer highlighted. Draw new CAT figures to match them.
