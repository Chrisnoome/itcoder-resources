Style: [cat-art-style.md](cat-art-style.md). **How it works:** a CAT lesson
names the IT drawing (`Doodle ('ram-fridge', ...)`); once `cat-ram-fridge.svg`
exists in `public/assets/doodles/`, every CAT lesson shows it instead
(`DesignArtSvg()`, lib/design.php). So a redraw needs no lesson edit - only
check that its caption still fits the new picture.

### 5d. Placement plan

For whoever places the drawings once they exist. Every call below is exact PHP, ready to paste. File: `AIPascalCourse/content/<course>/<lesson>.php`. Find the section by `<span class="block-anchor" id="<anchor>"></span>`. Four kinds of edit:

- **new** - the section has no doodle yet. Directly after the anchor span line, insert:
  ```
  HTML
      . Doodle (...)
      . <<<'HTML'
  ```
- **second** - the section has a doodle already. Insert the call as a new line `    . Doodle (...)` directly after the existing `. Doodle (...)` line.
- **replace** - swap the named old call in that section (the whole `Doodle ('old', '...')`, caption included) for the new one.
- **figure** - a `DesignFigure` at the end of the section's prose block: after the block's last `HTML` line, insert `    . DesignFigure (...)` before the `,`.

A call shows nothing until its SVG exists (Doodle() returns '' for a missing drawing), so a call can go in before the drawing - but place them when drawn, so the captions get checked against the pictures. Calls marked *exists* can go in now.
