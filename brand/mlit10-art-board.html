Maths Lit Grade 10: pick a look

There are two layers. The course frame stays the same all year: lesson openers, the year map, margin doodles, emblems, and the hadeda. Each batch theme changes the scenes and documents for that scenario. Pick one frame below, then theme A or B for each batch. The real-looking documents (bills, slips, plans, maps) stay plain and realistic inside every theme, because pupils have to read them the way they will in the exam.

The course frame: four options

The twelve batch themes

A is the theme from the plan, and B is an alternative. Each was checked against the other courses (tuck-shop board game, clubweb comic, pascal9 pixel, sql9 coloured pencil, socialmedia neon, safety case file, CAT marker) so that no look is reused.

How it gets drawn. The frame, doodles and most themes are SVG drawn by a script (tools/mlit10/make_art.py), as for tuckshop and clubweb. Themes tagged ComfyUI need realistic pictures from the queue. The course icon, badges and the nine rank emblems (10c to R200) go through ComfyUI in the frame's style.

Graph engine. Graphs that pupils draw use the engine's own plain grid in every batch, so the engine needs no theming per batch.
