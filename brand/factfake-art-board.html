Fact or fake: try again

New story, in the pupils' own world: the class runs the school's fact-check account. Every day something goes viral in the Grade 8 group chats and on the video apps - a "free data" offer, a clip of a shark in a flooded mall, a voice note about school being cancelled, an AI picture of the principal. Before it spreads any further, the class checks it: search well, find who made it, check the picture, spot the AI, and work out why it spread. All apps and accounts are made up. The mascots are Echo and Lens, as you chose.

Six styles

A The group chat

Everything happens in a made-up chat app: message bubbles, "forwarded many times" labels, voice notes, link previews, reactions - and the class's fact-check replies in green. Every lesson opens on the day's viral message. The world they actually live in.

B The video feed

Vertical phone screens from a made-up short-video app: captions, like and share counts, sounds - and the class's fact-check sticker slapped over the fake ones. Bright pink and mint on black. Each lesson is a clip to check, side by side with the checked version.

C Quest mode

Fact-checking as a game: each rumour is a boss with a health bar, every check is a quest-log tick that knocks it down, and XP pops up. Pixel type, dark purple, lime and gold. Each lesson unlocks a new move (search, source, reverse image, AI scan).

D Memes and stickers

Meme formats and a sticker pack: two-panel memes for the right and wrong way, reaction stickers (SUS, LEGIT, CAP), chunky comic lettering, warm yellows and oranges. Funny and very shareable - the irony being that the memes teach you to check.

E The live stream

The class streams its fact-checks live: the clip in the middle, the checker's webcam box, a chat column full of opinions (some helpful, some wrong) and pop-up alerts when a new skill unlocks. Deep purple with neon accents. Shows how a crowd reacts to a rumour.

F Lens scanner

The phone camera becomes a scanner: corner brackets, a scan line, clues circled on the picture and a checklist that fills in. Black with cyan and magenta. Lens (the mascot) is the scanner - and it doubles as the look for spotting AI fakes.

Your mascots: Echo and Lens

Echo

The parrot who shares everything the second it arrives - forwards, reposts, voice notes - without reading past the first line. Always first, often wrong.

Lens

The magnifying glass that looks closer: who posted it, when, where the picture first appeared, what the AI got wrong. Slower than Echo - and right.

Running joke: Echo shares, Lens checks, and the lesson is in the gap between them. "Pause before you share" is Echo's arc across the six lessons.

All made up: the apps, accounts, names and posts are invented and drawn by us - never copied from real posts or real people, and no real brands.
