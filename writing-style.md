# Writing style for lesson prose - the base rules

For every word a pupil reads. [content-voice-and-pedagogy.md](content-voice-and-pedagogy.md)
builds on these. Calibrated against Chris's own notes (`word documents/hw_*.docx`)
- match those, not an essay.

## The rules

- Plain, conversational English for South African teenagers; direct address
  ("you").
- **Short sentences, often one per paragraph.** Two clauses joined by a comma?
  Try a full stop.
- **Bulleted lists for anything enumerable** - Chris lists far more than most.
- **Concrete, everyday physical analogies** ("with 2 cores you could fit two
  brains inside your head!").
- Exclamation marks for the surprising fact, not for enthusiasm.
- **`Warning:` and `Note:` callouts** (`<div class="callout">`) for a
  terminology confusion or common mistake.
- **A quote at the top of each lesson** (`<blockquote class="quote">` with
  `<cite>`); bank: `word documents/_ALL_QUOTES.docx` (see
  content-voice-and-pedagogy.md §6 for its data trap).
- Hyphens, not em dashes.
- Local examples: load shedding, Springboks, rands, Afrikaans, Johannesburg.
  Pupils speak English and some Afrikaans - not isiZulu examples.
- No jargon without unpacking it at once ("X stands for Y").
- Never talk down; trust the material.
- Say "pupil", never "learner".

## What to avoid

- **Writerly constructions** that admire their own structure ("that word does
  a lot of hiding", "here is the part that surprises people"). Say the thing.
- **Banned words:** "genuine", "genuinely", "for real", "binds"/"binds
  tighter", "reach for" (meaning use), "lean on" (meaning use/depend on). Say
  "use"; state verified output plainly once ("Compiled and run, this
  prints..."). Literal "reach" (a loop reaches 0) is fine. When removing one,
  fix the article ("a genuine Integer" -> "an Integer") and match across line
  wraps.
- **Never name the SAGs** (or the syllabus) anywhere a pupil sees it in a
  lesson - prompts, html, intro, explain. State the fact without the citation.
  Lesson-file doc comments may cite it. **Exception:** the Pascal course page
  lists each lesson's SAGs coverage (`content/pascal/sags.php`, shown by
  `course.php`).

## Standing cautions

- **Numbers go stale** (GPU, token, data-centre prices) - search for current SA
  pricing, or use comparisons that last.
- **Never invent YouTube IDs** (platform.md decision 10).
