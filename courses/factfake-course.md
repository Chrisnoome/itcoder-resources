## The story

The class is Ridgeview High's **fact-check team**, run by Ms Pillay. Every lesson, something goes
viral in the Grade 8 group chat, and the team checks it.

The original plan was a newsroom. Chris turned that down: *"kids dont read newspapers or watch tv
news"*.

The mascots:
- **Echo** the parrot, who shares;
- **Lens** the magnifying glass, who checks.

| # | Id | Title | The viral thing | Gated |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `search` | Searching well | a voice note: "school is cancelled tomorrow" | `q1Site`, `s1Trust` |
| 2 | `source` | Who made it? | "FREE 50GB DATA" with a lookalike link | `o2Sideways`, `m2Links` |
| 3 | `photos` | Photos that lie | a shark in a flooded mall (an edited photo from 2018) | `m3HowItLies`, `o3Reverse` |
| 4 | `aifakes` | AI fakes | an AI picture of the principal with a lotto trophy | `hs4Clues` (find the clues), `q4Voice` |
| 5 | `spread` | Why fakes spread | "share to 10 people or your account closes" | `s5Warning`, `o5Sift` |
| 6 | `verdict` | The verdict (final) | "R500 for tablets": a cut quote, an old photo and a fake site | `m6Checks`, `q6Verdict`, and the written `w6Verdict` (6 marks, Jev) |

The course teaches **SIFT** (Stop, Investigate the source, Find better coverage, Trace to the
original), Mike Caulfield's method, and reading sideways (lateral reading: Wineburg and McGrew,
2017).
