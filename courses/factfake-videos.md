# Grade 8 Fact or fake - video plans for the video chat

Chris, 10 October 2026: "vide requirements to the video queue". The course is `factfake`
(`AIPascalCourse/content/factfake/`; plan [factfake-course.md](factfake-course.md)).

There are **6 videos, one per lesson, about 35 minutes in all.** Each **Goes** line gives the
place in the lesson. Add a `// VIDEO factfake-NN.n` comment there when you start the video.

## Rules

- **Method and voice:** follow [tutorial-videos.md](tutorial-videos.md).
- **Thumbnails** come from a new `thumbs-factfake.json`:
  - background `#0b0f14`, accent `#3df2ff`, second colour `#1f9d63`;
  - tag `FACT OR FAKE · CHECK N`.
- **The look** is [../brand/factfake-art-style.md](../brand/factfake-art-style.md): the made-up
  group chat for each rumour and the Lens scanner for each check. Echo and Lens appear in the
  hooks.
- **Real searches on screen, made safe.**
  - Record in the VM, in a normal browser, so pupils see real search pages, real reverse image
    search and real "Sponsored" labels.
  - Search only harmless things: our own made-up images, uploaded from the course, and safe
    public topics.
  - Never show real people's posts, real scams' links or real fake news.
  - Blur anything personal.
- **Never show a marked answer.** Use other examples:
  - video 2: a different lookalike;
  - video 4: a different AI picture;
  - video 6: a different case from the R500 tablets.
- Nothing goes in a video that is not in the lesson text.

## What each video covers

Each video:
1. **Hook:** the viral message in the chat.
2. **The check:** done on screen, in the Lens scanner look.
3. **The verdict:** posted kindly in the chat.
4. **Sign-off.**

What each one must show:
- **01.1:** key words, then "quotes", -minus and site:gov.za; reading a results page (the
  Sponsored label, the address, the date); going to the source.
- **02.1:** who made it and why. Read sideways with three tabs. Read a lookalike address letter
  by letter. Show why a tick and followers are not proof.
- **03.1:** wrong time, wrong place, edited and cropped. Then a real reverse image search on one
  of our own made-up pictures, finding the oldest copy.
- **04.1:**
  - the AI clues, zooming in on the hands, writing, matching things and the background;
  - cloned voices and the secret-word idea;
  - why checking the source still beats looking.
- **05.1:** feelings plus hurry, how feeds reward reactions, SIFT, and correcting kindly.
- **06.1:** gather the evidence, write the verdict (verdict, the truth, evidence, what to do),
  calm and kind.
