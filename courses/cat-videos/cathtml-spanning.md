## cathtml-13.1 Spanning rows and columns (about 7 min)

**Goes:** the comment `// VIDEO cathtml-13.1`, after the select question in `#plan`.
**The pupil can afterwards:** make cells span columns and rows, plan the cells to type on paper, and fix rows that stick out.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · HTML`, title "colspan or *rowspan*?"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:40).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. In Word Mr Botha merges cells. In HTML a cell spans.
   > Board: two cells melting into one; Clicky with a tape measure.
2. **colspan (0:40-2:00).** A title across 3 columns is the whole row; cells plus spans = columns.
   > Screen: colspan 3 on the specials title.
3. **rowspan (2:00-3:30).** Tea two rows tall; the row below leaves out its first cell.
   > Screen: rowspan 2 on Tea, then the extra Tea deleted - the row snaps back.
4. **Plan on paper (3:30-5:00).** Draw the grid, box the merged cells, write the spans, count the cells to type: 1, 3, 3, 2, 1.
   > Board: the function menu plan, counts in highlighter.
5. **Mistakes (5:00-6:30).** A cell left under a rowspan, a span too small, colspane, a span on the tr.
   > Screen: the specials fix-it.
6. **Sign-off.**
