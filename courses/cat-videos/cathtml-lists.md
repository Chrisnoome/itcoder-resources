**CAT VM `itcoder-cat`** (Notepad++ and Edge). Board scenes in the CAT marker

## cathtml-07.1 Bulleted and numbered lists (about 7 min)

**Goes:** the comment `// VIDEO cathtml-07.1`, after the typed question in `#types`.
**The pupil can afterwards:** make bulleted and numbered lists, choose their bullets and numbers with type, and spot the four usual list mistakes.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · HTML`, title "*Lists* that people read"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:40).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. A customer looked for rolls on Mr Botha's page, gave up, and phoned. People scan web pages - lists make scanning easy.
   > Board: a long paragraph with Clicky lost in it; then the same words as a list.
2. **ul and li (0:40-2:00).** Ul round the list, li round each item; the browser adds the bullets and new lines; indent the li lines.
   > Screen: Notepad++, type the bread list, save, refresh in Edge.
3. **ol (2:00-3:00).** Ol for steps and rankings; never type the numbers; add a step and the numbers move.
   > Screen: the cake-order steps; insert a step in the middle.
4. **type (3:00-5:00).** Ul: disc, circle, square. ol: 1, A, a, I, i - capitals matter; on the ul or ol, once.
   > Screen: change the type and refresh each time.
   > Board: the eight types in a grid, highlighter on I and i.
5. **Mistakes (5:00-6:30).** Li outside the list, the wrong closing tag, a missing </li>, numbers typed by hand.
   > Screen: the fix-it file broken and repaired step by step.
6. **Sign-off.**
