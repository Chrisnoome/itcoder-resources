# catword lesson 14: Sections, columns and cover pages - videos

Lesson: `content/catword/sections.php` (Grade 11). Screen recordings in the
CAT VM (Word 365), starting from Camp report.docx (G:\My Drive\CAT\Word).
Never show the title bar's account; paint out the Add-ins group. Board
scenes in the CAT marker style with Clicky; yellow highlighter on what is
talked about. Both boards teach all of it in Grade 11.

## catword-14.1 Section breaks and a landscape page (about 7 min)

**Goes:** after the quiz `q14Deleted`, before section `#columns` (the
comment `// VIDEO catword-14.1`).
**The pupil can afterwards:** say what a section holds, choose the right
section break, make one section landscape, and explain what happens when a
section break is deleted.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · WORD`, title "One page *sideways*"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:40).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. The camp report has
   a cover, a diary in columns, a cost table too wide for an upright page,
   and a page of conclusions: four layouts in one document.
   > Board: four pages in a row, one lying on its side, Clicky with
   > scissors between them ("section breaks").
2. **What a section holds (0:40-2:00).** Margins, orientation, size,
   columns, headers and footers, page numbering, line numbers, page borders.
   The four breaks: Next Page, Continuous, Even Page, Odd Page (chapters on
   right-hand pages).
3. **Insert a break (2:00-3:30).** Cursor at the start of Costs; Layout >
   Breaks > Next Page; Show/Hide shows Section Break (Next Page). Status bar
   > right-click > Section.
   > Screen: the clicks above.
4. **One landscape section (3:30-5:30).** Another Next Page break after the
   table; click in the costs; Layout > Orientation > Landscape - only that
   section. Page Setup's Apply to: This section, This point forward, Whole
   document. Zoom out to see three pages side by side.
5. **Deleting a break (5:30-6:40).** The settings live in the break at a
   section's end: delete it, and the pages before turn landscape. Ctrl+Z.
   > Board: a train of carriages, the coupling at the back of each one
   > holding its luggage.
6. **Sign-off.**

## catword-14.2 Columns and a cover page (about 7 min)

**Goes:** after the written question `w14Sections`, before the scenario (the
comment `// VIDEO catword-14.2`).
**The pupil can afterwards:** put part of a document in columns with the
Columns box, use a column break, and add and fill in a cover page.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · WORD`, title "Columns, a break, and a *cover*"

### Scenes

1. **Columns for one part (0:00-2:30).** Select the diary paragraphs;
   Layout > Columns > More Columns...: Presets Two, Width and spacing,
   Equal column width, Line between, Apply to Selected text - Word adds the
   Continuous breaks.
   > Screen: the clicks above; yellow highlighter on Spacing and Line
   > between.
2. **A column break (2:30-3:40).** Day 3 should start the second column:
   Ctrl+Shift+Enter (or Layout > Breaks > Column) - never a row of Enters.
3. **A cover page (3:40-5:30).** Insert > Cover Page > a design: in front of
   page 1. Click [Document title] and type Grade 11 camp report; the date
   picker's calendar. Blank Page; Remove Current Cover Page.
4. **Content controls (5:30-6:40).** The boxes are content controls; many
   are linked to the document's properties - the title typed once shows
   wherever the Title box is.
   > Board: two boxes joined by a string, Clicky writing in one, the other
   > filling itself.
5. **Sign-off.**
