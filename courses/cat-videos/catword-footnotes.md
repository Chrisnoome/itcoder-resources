# catword lesson 16: Footnotes, captions and an index - videos

Lesson: `content/catword/footnotes.php` (Grade 11). Screen recordings in the
CAT VM (Word 365), starting from Saving water report.docx (G:\My Drive\CAT\Word).
Never show the title bar's account; paint out the Add-ins group. Board
scenes in the CAT marker style with Clicky; yellow highlighter on what is
talked about. Equations, Search and a table of authorities are IEB: say so
in one line where they come.

## catword-16.1 Footnotes and captions (about 7 min)

**Goes:** after the quiz `q16Renumber`, before section `#figures` (the
comment `// VIDEO catword-16.1`).
**The pupil can afterwards:** insert and delete footnotes and endnotes, and
give tables and pictures numbered captions.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · WORD`, title "A note at the *bottom* of the page"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:40).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. Before Ctrl+F,
   books had notes at the bottom of the page and an index at the back -
   and printed reports still do.
   > Board: an old book open, Clicky lifting a footnote with tweezers.
2. **Footnotes (0:40-3:00).** After "2024": References > Insert Footnote
   (Alt+Ctrl+F); type the note; add another before it - they renumber.
   Insert Endnote (Alt+Ctrl+D). Delete the number in the text to delete the
   note. Next Footnote; the Footnotes launcher.
   > Screen: the clicks above.
3. **Captions (3:00-6:00).** Select the table; References > Insert Caption:
   Label Table, Position Above, type ": Water used per day" after Table 1.
   Figures below pictures; New Label (Map). The number is a field (F9);
   the Caption style.
   > Board: tables in a row, numbers 1, 2, 3 sliding along as one is added.
4. **Sign-off.**

## catword-16.2 A table of figures and an index (about 8 min)

**Goes:** after the written question `w16Footnote`, before the IEB section
(the comment `// VIDEO catword-16.2`).
**The pupil can afterwards:** insert a table of figures, customise a table
of contents, mark index entries and insert and update an index.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · WORD`, title "An *index* in two steps"

### Scenes

1. **Table of figures (0:00-2:00).** Under List of tables: References >
   Insert Table of Figures, Caption label Table, OK. Update Table / F9.
   Custom Table of Contents: Show levels, Tab leader.
2. **Lists Word builds (2:00-2:40).** Contents from headings, figures from
   captions, bibliography from citations, index from marked entries.
   > Board: four machines with four inputs - headings, captions,
   > citations, marked words.
3. **Mark entries (2:40-5:00).** Select Rainwater tanks; Alt+Shift+X; Main
   entry, Subentry; Mark All. The XE codes with Show/Hide on - and off
   again.
   > Screen: the clicks above.
4. **Insert and update (5:00-6:30).** Click under Index: References > Insert
   Index, columns, right-aligned numbers, OK. Mark three more words: Update
   Index.
5. **IEB tools (6:30-7:40).** Insert > Equation (Area of Circle; Alt+= and
   the Equation tab); right-click > Search (Smart Lookup); Mark Citation and
   a table of authorities for legal references.
6. **Sign-off.**
