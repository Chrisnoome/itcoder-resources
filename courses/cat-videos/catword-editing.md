# catword lesson 2: Moving around, selecting and editing - videos

Lesson: `content/catword/editing.php` (Grade 10). Screen recordings in the
CAT VM (Word 365), starting from the starter file Trip letter.docx
(G:\My Drive\CAT\Word). Never show the title bar's account. Board scenes in
the CAT marker style with Clicky; yellow highlighter on what is talked about.
Show the keys pressed on screen (a key caption).

## catword-02.1 Selecting, undo, cut, copy and paste (about 8 min)

**Goes:** after the written question `w2CutCopy` in section `#clipboard`
(the comment `// VIDEO catword-02.1`).
**The pupil can afterwards:** move the insertion point with the keyboard,
select with the mouse and the keyboard, delete, undo and redo, and move or
repeat text with cut, copy and paste.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · WORD`, title "Select, *cut*, paste"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:40).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. Ms Naidoo's letter
   has three mistakes. On a typewriter: start again. In Word: seconds each.
   > Board: the letter with three red circles; Clicky with an eraser.
2. **Moving (0:40-1:40).** Arrows, Ctrl+arrows, Home/End, Ctrl+Home/End,
   Page Up/Down. Scrolling moves the view, not the insertion point.
   > Screen: each key once, with a key caption.
3. **Selecting with the mouse (1:40-3:20).** Drag; double-click a word;
   triple-click a paragraph; Ctrl+click a sentence; click in the left margin
   for a line; click then Shift+click. Typing replaces a selection.
   > Screen: each one on the letter; highlight the pointer turning into a right-pointing arrow in the margin.
4. **With the keyboard (3:20-4:20).** Shift+arrows, Shift+End, Shift+Home,
   Ctrl+Shift+Right, Ctrl+A. Delete the second "Bring a packed lunch".
5. **Undo and Redo (4:20-5:10).** Delete the R85 paragraph by mistake;
   Ctrl+Z; Ctrl+Y; the curved arrow at the top.
   > Board: Clicky rewinding a tape.
6. **Cut, copy, paste (5:10-7:20).** The clipboard. Move "Please sign..."
   above Ms Naidoo's name: select it (left margin), Cut, click, Ctrl+V. Copy
   a line to a second place. Drag a selection. The right-click menu.
   > Board: the clipboard as a tray between two places on the page.
7. **Sign-off.**

## catword-02.2 Find and replace, symbols and AutoCorrect (about 6 min)

**Goes:** after the quiz `q2WholeWords` in section `#replace` (the comment
`// VIDEO catword-02.2`).
**The pupil can afterwards:** find a word, replace every one at once with
Replace All, avoid replacing inside longer words, insert a symbol and undo
an AutoCorrect change.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · WORD`, title "Replace *every* mistake"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:30).** Saturday three times - and it should be Friday.
2. **Find (0:30-1:20).** Ctrl+F, the Navigation pane, every result highlighted.
3. **Replace (1:20-3:00).** Ctrl+H or Home > Editing > Replace: Find what
   Saturday, Replace with Friday, Replace All, Word says how many, Close.
   Find Next and Replace, one by one.
4. **Whole words (3:00-3:50).** Replace "Fri" with "Friday": Fridayday.
   Ctrl+Z. More >> > Find whole words only.
   > Board: the condogenate joke (cat -> dog).
5. **Symbols (3:50-5:00).** cafe -> café: Insert > Symbol > More Symbols >
   é > Insert > Close.
6. **AutoCorrect (5:00-5:40).** teh -> the; (c) -> ©, and Ctrl+Z keeps (c);
   straight quotes to curly; 08:00 -- 14:00 becomes a dash.
7. **Sign-off.**
