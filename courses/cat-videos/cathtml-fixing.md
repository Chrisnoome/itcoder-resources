## cathtml-14.1 Fixing a broken page, step by step (about 8 min)

**Goes:** the comment `// VIDEO cathtml-14.1`, after the written question in `#nesting`.
**The pupil can afterwards:** work through a broken file with a method - two windows, comments kept, one part at a time, the tag sheet - and recognise the usual mistakes.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · HTML`, title "Fix *any* broken page"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:40).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. A test file is broken on purpose. Here is how to fix one without panic.
   > Board: Clicky as a detective with a magnifying glass over a page.
2. **The method (0:40-2:30).** Read every part; Notepad++ and the browser side by side; find the comment markers and keep them; one part, save, refresh; same name, same folder.
   > Screen: test-bakery.html opened in both windows (G:\My Drive\CAT\HTML\botha).
3. **Notepad++ helps (2:30-3:30).** Colours, matching-tag highlight, line numbers.
   > Screen: click an opening tag - its partner lights up; a misspelt tag stays a different colour. Check both in the VM's Notepad++ first.
4. **The tag sheet (3:30-4:30).** Spelling, which tag an attribute belongs to, the values; <br> and <br/> are the same.
   > Board: a tag sheet with highlighter on colspan under td.
5. **The usual suspects (4:30-7:00).** Titel, a tag with no name, rolls.jpg for rolls.png, li with no list, a total row that does not span - fixed one by one.
   > Screen: the six parts of the test file fixed.
6. **Closing and nesting (7:00-7:40).** The last check, top to bottom.
   > Board: crossed tags uncrossed.
7. **Sign-off.**
