## cathtml-06.1 HTML in ten minutes: a quick start (about 9 min)

**Goes:** the comment `// VIDEO cathtml-06.1`, after the figure in `#recap`.
**The pupil can afterwards:** make and fix a simple page with the Grade 10 tags, save it and see it in a browser; line up headings and paragraphs with align; leave comments that the browser skips.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · HTML`, title "HTML in *ten* minutes"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:40).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. Thabo is in Grade 11 and Mr Botha wants more from his website. If you are starting HTML this year, this video is your quick start.
   > Board: Clicky holding a crumpled to-do list: list, pictures, cakes page, links.
2. **A page is plain text (0:40-2:30).** HTML marks up text; tags in angle brackets, opening and closing; Notepad++, save as .html, double-click to open; after every change save (Ctrl+S) and refresh (F5).
   > Screen: Notepad++ and Edge side by side in the CAT VM; type a heading, Ctrl+S, F5.
3. **The skeleton (2:30-4:00).** Html, head with the title, body; nested: the tag opened first closes last.
   > Board: the skeleton drawn as boxes inside boxes; highlighter on the title.
4. **The body's tags (4:00-6:00).** H1-h6, p, br and hr (no closing tag), b, i, u; bgcolor, font color/face/size, hr width/size; color spelt the American way.
   > Screen: each tag added and refreshed; colour typed as colour does nothing.
5. **Align and comments (6:00-8:30).** Align="left|center|right" on headings and paragraphs, one at a time; CAPS: the center tag. Comments <!-- -->: notes, test markers to keep, hiding a line; an unclosed comment hides the rest.
   > Screen: centre the heading, right-align the hours, comment out the special and bring it back.
6. **Sign-off.**
