## catdb-14.1 From a question to a report (about 8 min)

**Goes:** after the study block.
**The pupil can afterwards:** take a question apart (records, fields, order,
totals); build the query then the report; (CAPS) change a report's record
source; export to Excel, PDF and text; start a Word Merge; check an answer.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · DATABASES`, title "Start from the *question*"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:40).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. Two questions from the
   principal on a Monday morning.
2. **Taking it apart (0:40-2:00).** Which records, which fields, what order, what totals.
   > Board: the question split into four sticky notes.
3. **Query first (2:00-3:20).** qryOwing: >0, Descending; count the records.
4. **CAPS: changing the source (3:20-5:00).** rptStudents in Design View;
   Property Sheet (Report) > Data > Record Source > qryOwing; #Name? when a field is missing.
5. **Sending it out (5:00-6:50).** Excel; PDF or XPS; Text File; Word Merge;
   More > Word; copy and paste.
6. **Reading the answer (6:50-7:40).** Count, spot-check, sense-check, add groups up.
7. **Sign-off.**
