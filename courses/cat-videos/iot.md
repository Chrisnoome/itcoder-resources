## cat11-16.1 What makes a thing smart (about 6 min)

**Goes:** after section `#ipo` (the comment `// VIDEO cat11-16.1`).
**The pupil can afterwards:** say what the IoT is, name what is inside a smart device, and explain any IoT device as input (sensing), processing (analysis) and output (feedback or automatic action).
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · INTERNET`, title "The freezer sent a *message*."

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:50).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. 02:14 on a
   Saturday, load shedding. Mr Botha's phone buzzes: "Freezer 2: -6 °C and
   rising." Nobody typed it.
   > Board: a dark bakery, a freezer with a small box inside, a message flying to a phone on a bedside table.
2. **What the IoT is (0:50-2:00).** Computers, then people, now things. A
   thing that reports, can be controlled from far away, and acts by itself.
   "Smart" in a name. Inside: sensors, a small embedded computer, a
   connection. Often no screen - you use an app.
   > Board: three sticky notes - Report, Control from far, Act by itself; highlighter on "smart".
3. **Input, processing, output (2:00-3:30).** The IPO model in IoT words:
   sensing, analysis, feedback or automatic action - and the connection
   carrying readings out and commands back.
   > Board: the iot-parts drawing, built up left to right; highlighter on each of the three IoT words as it is named.
4. **Worked example: the geyser controller (3:30-5:20).** The five steps:
   38 °C and the units used; 04:30 and the rule; switch on, off at 60 °C;
   the app shows Lerato; Holiday mode from the taxi. Remote monitoring.
   > Board: a geyser with a thermometer; a clock at 04:30; a phone with a graph; a taxi with Lerato tapping "Holiday mode".
5. **Sign-off.**

## cat11-16.2 Smart homes, farms and fleets - the good and the risky (about 9 min)

**Goes:** after section `#goodBad` (the comment `// VIDEO cat11-16.2`).
**The pupil can afterwards:** give examples of the IoT in homes, meters, health, transport and farming; name the technologies behind it; give benefits and risks; and set up a smart device safely.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · INTERNET`, title "Find the *S* in IoT."

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:30).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. Count the things in
   a South African home that are online without anyone typing on them.
   > Board: a house drawn in outline; Clicky ticking off a meter, a camera, a geyser, a watch.
2. **Smart homes (0:30-2:00).** Electricity: geyser controllers, smart
   plugs, inverters, smart lights. Security: cameras with alerts, app-armed
   alarms, doorbells and gates. Convenience: voice assistants, robot
   vacuums.
   > Board: the iot-geyser drawing; the hey-assistant drawing.
3. **Smart meters (2:00-3:20).** Keypad meter vs smart meter. The table: for
   the municipality and for the household; the water leak at 03:00; the
   privacy side.
   > Board: two meters side by side; the two-column table filling in.
4. **Health (3:20-4:30).** Wearables - steps, heart rate, irregular
   heartbeat, fall detection; medical devices - glucose monitors, pacemakers,
   blood-pressure monitors. Watched from far away.
   > Board: the smartwatch drawing; Gogo falls, the watch phones Lerato.
5. **Road and farm (4:30-5:50).** Fleet tracking on a map; accident-prevention
   sensors (parking, tired-driver camera, automatic braking, crash
   detection). Soil moisture, irrigation, livestock collars against stock
   theft.
   > Board: a map with truck dots; a cow with a GPS collar walking off the farm at night, an alert on the farmer's phone.
6. **The technologies behind it (5:50-6:50).** Big data (too much for one
   computer), AI (the camera that knows a person from a dog), notifications
   and commands back.
   > Board: three arrows into a cloud labelled Big data, AI, Notifications; highlighter on "push notification".
7. **Benefits and risks (6:50-8:30).** The table. Why IoT devices get hacked:
   default passwords, no updates, nobody thinks of them as computers. Mirai.
   Using smart devices safely - the six steps.
   > Board: the people-no-s-iot drawing; a password sticker "admin" being crossed out and replaced.
8. **Sign-off.**
