# catword lesson 22: Line and page breaks - videos

Lesson: `content/catword/pagination.php` (Grade 12). Screen recordings in
the CAT VM (Word 365), starting from Water report.docx (G:\My
Drive\CAT\Word): Thabo's PAT report on saving water, with a heading
stranded at the foot of page 1, empty lines pushed in front of it and a
typed page break before Recommendations. Never show the title bar's
account. Board scenes in the CAT marker style with Clicky; yellow
highlighter on what is talked about. The Accessibility Checker and
Translate are IEB Grade 12 - say so once. Never "in the exam".

## catword-22.1 Keeping text together (about 8 min)

**Goes:** after the written question `wBreakBefore`, before section
`#widow` (the comment `// VIDEO catword-22.1`).
**The pupil can afterwards:** say why Enters and typed page breaks fail,
and use Keep with next, Keep lines together, Page break before and
Widow/Orphan control - and know that the heading styles keep with next.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · WORD`, title "The heading that got *left behind*"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:40).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. A heading alone at
   the bottom of a page, its text on the next. Thabo presses Enter six times
   - then adds one sentence above, and it all goes wrong again.
   > Board: a heading waving goodbye from the bottom of one page to its
   > paragraph on the next; Clicky holding the Enter key like a crowbar.
2. **Where Word ends a page (0:40-1:40).** Pagination is worked out again
   after every change; Enters and Ctrl+Enter stay put while the text moves.
   > Screen: two pages side by side (View > Multiple Pages); type a
   > sentence on page 1 and watch the break move.
3. **Keep with next (1:40-3:20).** Click in How much water we use; Home >
   Paragraph launcher > Line and Page Breaks tab > Keep with next > OK. The
   heading jumps to page 2 with its text. Captions and "the following:"
   lines too.
   > Screen: each click; yellow highlighter on the tick box.
4. **Keep lines together (3:20-4:20).** The bank-details paragraph split
   over two pages; tick Keep lines together and the whole paragraph moves.
   Not on every paragraph.
5. **Page break before (4:20-5:40).** Show/Hide on: delete the typed Page
   Break before Recommendations; click in the heading; Page break before.
   The small black square in the margin. It moves with the heading and
   leaves no empty page.
   > Board: a train carriage coupled to its engine (Keep with next) beside
   > one that always starts a new train (Page break before).
6. **Widows and orphans (5:40-6:40).** The last line alone at the top (a
   widow), the first line alone at the bottom (an orphan); Widow/Orphan
   control is on already in Normal.
   > Board: one lonely line on a big empty page, Clicky patting it.
7. **The heading styles do it (6:40-7:30).** Apply Heading 1 to a heading
   made bold by hand - it keeps with next by itself. Modify Heading 1 to
   add Page break before for every chapter.
8. **Sign-off.**

## catword-22.2 Language and accessibility (about 6 min)

**Goes:** after the IEB section "Translate", before the scenario (the
comment `// VIDEO catword-22.2`).
**The pupil can afterwards:** set the proofing language of a whole document
to English (South Africa), and - IEB - run the Accessibility Checker, add
alt text, and translate a selection or a document.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · WORD`, title "Colour, not *color*"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:30).** Thabo's own "colour" is underlined in red; the
   pasted American paragraph's "color" is not.
   > Board: a red wavy line under "colour" and Clicky looking confused.
2. **The proofing language (0:30-2:00).** The language is stored with the
   text. Ctrl+A; Review > Language > Set Proofing Language (or the status
   bar); English (South Africa); OK. Do not check spelling or grammar for a
   list of plant names.
   > Screen: each click; yellow highlighter on English (South Africa).
3. **IEB: who cannot see the page (2:00-2:40).** A screen reader reads the
   document aloud; a picture without alt text is silent.
   > Board: a pair of headphones on a page, the photo a grey box with a
   > question mark.
4. **IEB: Check Accessibility (2:40-4:00).** Review > Check Accessibility:
   Errors (Missing alternative text), Warnings; click one; right-click the
   photo > View Alt Text; one sentence; the status bar "Good to go".
   > Screen: the Accessibility pane.
5. **IEB: Translate (4:00-5:20).** Review > Translate > Translate Selection
   (the Translator pane, Insert) and Translate Document (a new copy). Ask a
   person who speaks the language to check it. Not the same as the proofing
   language.
6. **Sign-off.**
