# catword lesson 5: Bullets, numbering and tabs - videos

Lesson: `content/catword/lists.php` (Grade 10). Screen recordings in the
CAT VM (Word 365), starting from Scones.docx (G:\My Drive\CAT\Word). Never
show the title bar's account. Board scenes in the CAT marker style with
Clicky; yellow highlighter on what is talked about.

## catword-05.1 Bulleted and numbered lists (about 7 min)

**Goes:** after the quiz `q5BulletOrNumber` in section `#numbering` (the
comment `// VIDEO catword-05.1`).
**The pupil can afterwards:** make bulleted and numbered lists, choose and
define a bullet (symbol, picture, size and colour), sort a list, and use
list levels.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · WORD`, title "Lists that *line up*"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:40).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. Gogo Dlamini's
   scone recipe, typed line after line - hard to follow.
   > Board: Gogo's handwritten card; Clicky with a tray of scones.
2. **Bullets (0:40-2:30).** Select, Bullets. The Bullet Library: the tick.
   Define New Bullet: Symbol, Picture, Font (size and colour). Enter, and
   Enter on an empty bullet to end. The hanging indent.
3. **Sort (2:30-3:30).** Sort Text: Paragraphs, Text, Ascending; Descending.
4. **Numbering (3:30-6:00).** Numbering; the Numbering Library; Define New
   Number Format (font, size, colour); delete a step and watch the numbers.
   Tab and Shift+Tab for levels. Restart at 1 / Continue Numbering.
5. **Sign-off.**

## catword-05.2 Tab stops (about 6 min)

**Goes:** after the simulation `simTabs` in section `#tabs` (the comment
`// VIDEO catword-05.2`).
**The pupil can afterwards:** say why spaces do not line up, set left,
centre, right and decimal tab stops with leaders in the Tabs dialog box and
on the ruler, and remove one.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · WORD`, title "Prices in a *straight line*"

### Scenes

1. **Spaces fail (0:00-1:00).** Line up the prices with spaces; change the
   font; the column falls apart.
   > Board: "iiii" and "mmmm" - the same count, different widths.
2. **Tab and tab stops (1:00-2:00).** Show/Hide arrows; the standard stops
   every 1.27 cm.
3. **The Tabs dialog box (2:00-4:00).** Select the price lines; Paragraph
   launcher > Tabs...; 10 cm, Right, leader 2, OK. A decimal tab for R5.50
   and R12.00.
4. **The ruler (4:00-5:20).** The tab selector, a click on the ruler, drag
   a stop off to remove it.
5. **Sign-off.**
