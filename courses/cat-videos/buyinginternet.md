the small print, and comparing two packages step by step. Board in the CAT
marker style with Clicky (brand/cat-art-style.md); yellow highlighter on any

## cat12-11.1 Capped, uncapped, throttled, shaped (about 7 min)

**Goes:** after section `#fairUse` - the comment after `rBuyUncapped`.
**The pupil can afterwards:** explain bandwidth and broadband, capped and uncapped, the fair usage policy, and tell throttling from shaping.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · NETWORKS`, title "Uncapped is *not* unlimited"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:40).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. Gogo's "uncapped" LTE was fine for three weeks - then it crawled. Mr Botha's advert says "uncapped, unshaped, unthrottled", and he has no idea what that means. Nobody cheated them. They just didn't read the small print.
   > Board: an advert with a tiny star and tiny print; Clicky with a magnifying glass. Yellow highlighter on the star.
2. **Bandwidth (0:40-1:50).** How much data a connection can carry each second, in Mbps - the width of the pipe. 100 Mbps is ten times as wide as 10. Shared by everyone at home, and by all the ISP's customers - slow at 20:00.
   > Board: the bandwidth-pipes figure; then the rush-hour doodle; highlighter on "per second".
3. **Broadband (1:50-2:30).** A fast connection that is always on and carries a lot at once - fibre, LTE, 5G, satellite. Before it, dial-up: 56 kbps, and nobody could use the phone. Bandwidth is an amount; broadband is a kind of connection.
   > Board: the two-column bandwidth / broadband table; highlighter on "amount" and "kind".
4. **Capped and uncapped (2:30-3:40).** Capped: a fixed amount a month, then it stops. Uncapped: no fixed limit. A bundle is capped data bought in advance (Grade 11); out-of-bundle rates are dear. Thabo's hour of HD a day: 3 x 30 = 90 GB - a 10 GB bundle lasts three days.
   > Board: a jug labelled 20 GB emptying; then the worked example written line by line; highlighter on "90 GB".
5. **The fair usage policy (3:40-4:30).** The rules in the small print of an "uncapped" package: "after 300 GB, 2 Mbps until the next month". That is what happened to Gogo.
   > Board: the advert's small print enlarged; highlighter on "300 GB" and "2 Mbps".
6. **Throttling and shaping (4:30-6:10).** Throttling slows the whole connection, usually after the limit - because of how much you used. Shaping slows only some kinds of traffic - torrents, big downloads - at busy times, because the network is busy. Why ISPs do it, and the other side. Unshaped, unthrottled fibre.
   > Board: the shaping-vs-throttling figure, one half at a time; then the Learn / Memorise table; highlighter on "whole" and "some kinds".
7. **Is uncapped unlimited? (6:10-6:40).** Not always - only uncapped AND unthrottled is.
   > Board: "uncapped = unlimited?" with a big marker question mark; highlighter on "AND".
8. **Sign-off.**

## cat12-11.2 Comparing two Internet packages (about 7 min)

**Goes:** after section `#compare` - the comment after `wBuyGogoPackage`.
**The pupil can afterwards:** check coverage, read a package, work out the cost for a year, and recommend a connection with reasons from the situation.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · NETWORKS`, title "LTE or fibre? *Do the sums*"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:30).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. Fibre has reached Thabo's street. Two adverts on the fridge: R449 or R649 a month. The cheaper one is not always cheaper.
   > Board: the two adverts as fridge magnets; Clicky holding a calculator.
2. **The connections side by side (0:30-1:50).** Fibre, an LTE or 5G router, mobile data, satellite - what each needs, and its best and worst point. Starlink's licence in South Africa. The fibre company owns the line; you buy from an ISP.
   > Board: the wan-lte-adsl-fibre figure; then the four-row table, one row at a time; highlighter on "Limitations".
3. **Coverage (1:50-2:50).** Outside: the coverage map with the exact address - but check the real bars inside. Gogo's one bar in the kitchen: a windowsill or an outdoor antenna. Inside: walls weaken Wi-Fi - an access point or mesh.
   > Board: the signal-tank doodle; a house with three rooms and Wi-Fi rings that fade; highlighter on "inside".
4. **What a package must tell you (2:50-3:40).** The kind, the speed ("up to"), the data, the price with VAT, once-off costs, the contract, the small print.
   > Board: the seven-item list as a checklist; Clicky ticking each line.
5. **Step by step (3:40-6:00).** Available? Need (four people, about 400 GB). Speed. Small print: 400 GB passes the 300 GB limit - throttled for the last week. Cost for a year: R449 x 12 = R5 388; R649 x 12 + R999 = R8 787 - R3 399 more. Power: a UPS for either. Decide.
   > Board: both adverts side by side; each step circled in turn; the sums written in marker; highlighter on "300 GB", "R5 388" and "R8 787".
6. **The answer that earns the marks (6:00-6:50).** "Fibre, because it is faster" earns little; the choice plus reasons from the situation earns the marks. When LTE would win.
   > Board: the two answers written one under the other; the weak one crossed out; highlighter on the scenario facts in the strong one.
7. **Sign-off.**
