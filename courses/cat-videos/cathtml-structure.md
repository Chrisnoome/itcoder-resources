Lesson: `AIPascalCourse/content/cathtml/structure.php`. Screen recording in

scenes in the CAT marker style with Clicky; yellow highlighter on words

## cathtml-02.1 The skeleton of a web page (about 7 min)

**Goes:** after the written question in section `#headings` (the comment
`// VIDEO cathtml-02.1`).
**The pupil can afterwards:** write the four document tags in the right
order, say what each one holds, nest tags properly, and use h1 and h2.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · HTML`, title "The *skeleton* of a page"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:30).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. Mr Botha's page and
   the biggest news site in the country have the same skeleton: four pairs
   of tags, always in the same order.
   > Board: an X-ray of a web page showing a skeleton of four tag pairs.
2. **The parcel (0:30-2:00).** html is the whole parcel; head is the label -
   nothing in it shows on the page; title is in the head - the tab, the
   bookmark name, the search result; body is what is in the parcel.
   > Board: a parcel with a label (head, title) and contents (body), each labelled with its tags.
   > Screen: Notepad++ with the skeleton; Edge beside it - highlighter on the tab, then on the page.
3. **Nesting (2:00-3:30).** The lunchbox in the school bag: the last opened is
   the first closed. Right and wrong head/title lines. The order of the four
   pairs. Indenting with Tab in Notepad++; clicking a tag lights up its
   partner.
   > Board: a lunchbox going into a bag and coming out first.
   > Screen: click on `<head>` in Notepad++ - its partner lights up.
4. **The browser forgives (3:30-4:30).** Move the title into the body: the tab
   still shows it. The code is still wrong - another browser may not guess.
   > Screen: cut and paste the title line into the body, save, refresh.
5. **Headings (4:30-6:30).** h1 to h6, biggest to smallest; one h1; h2 for
   each part; do not skip levels; not just to make words big (search engines
   and screen readers use them). Title vs h1: tab vs page.
   > Screen: type h1 to h6 lines, save, refresh; then Mr Botha's page with an h1 and two h2s.
6. **Sign-off.** In the lesson: fix the price list's skeleton, then give
   Mr Botha's page its headings.
