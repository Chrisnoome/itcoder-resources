Lesson: `content/cattheory12/iebp2.php` (IEB only - the whole lesson is an
IEB board section). Two videos: one box, one idea; and the scenario and the
stimulus. No screen recordings - all board. Board in the CAT marker style
with Clicky (brand/cat-art-style.md); yellow highlighter on any words on

## cat12-28.1 One box, one idea (about 7 min)

**Goes:** after section `#boxes` - the comment after `mw28Boxes`.
**The pupil can afterwards:** describe how Paper II is built and answered, fill labelled boxes for full marks, and explain a term in one full sentence.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · PAPER II`, title "\"Yes.\" is worth *nothing*"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:30).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. A box that says "Reason 1:", a box that says "Reason 2:". Lerato wrote both reasons in the first box. One mark.
   > Board: two labelled boxes, the first crammed, the second empty; the exam-bare-yes drawing beside them.
2. **The paper (0:30-1:50).** 150 marks scaled to 100, nine questions: Section A 25, Section B 75 (Questions 3 to 7), Section C 50 - one scenario across Questions 8 and 9. Answered in the 32-page paper; the marks grid on the cover. The cover's rules: watch the verb, general terms, one mark per fact, full sentences.
   > Board: the paper drawn as a booklet; highlighter on "full sentences".
3. **Section A (1:50-3:30).** Multiple choice with bold negatives and combinations; the missing term; matching with about twice as many options; explain the term - one mark, one full sentence, what it is and what it is for. WAN: expand AND explain - two boxes.
   > Board: the four formats as four small panels; the WAN Expansion / Explanation boxes filled in.
4. **One box, one idea (3:30-5:30).** Each box is a mark; a different idea in each; fill what the label asks; never leave a box empty. The memo: full-sentence model answers, OR, "any TWO", different reasons per box, no half marks, nothing off for spelling.
   > Board: Lerato's boxes from the lesson; each marked with a tick or a cross; highlighter on the labels.
5. **Full sentences, general terms (5:30-6:40).** "Backup." against "Make a backup on an external drive, so ..."; a spreadsheet, not Excel.
   > Board: one-word answers rewritten as sentences; highlighter on "so".
6. **Sign-off.**

## cat12-28.2 Section C - the scenario and the stimulus (about 7 min)

**Goes:** after section `#scenario` - the comment after `q28Option`.
**The pupil can afterwards:** use the command words' answer frames, answer an IPO item from the scenario, and use a package table or advert for a choice with its motivation.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · PAPER II`, title "Read the *article* twice"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:30).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. Gogo Dlamini only sends WhatsApps. Which Internet package - and why? The "why" is a mark of its own.
   > Board: the three-package table; Clicky pointing at Lite.
2. **Answer frames (0:30-2:20).** Define + example; the difference - the same point, one box per side (client and server); expand and explain; motivate - either side with a fitting reason; complete the table; write the function in full.
   > Board: the frames drawn as empty boxes, then filled one by one.
3. **Section B's specials (2:20-4:00).** Option A or Option B with the device's name - two marks. The IPO item: the bakery till's input, processing and output, from the scenario.
   > Board: two photo frames labelled A and B; then three boxes Input / Processing / Output filled for the till.
4. **One scenario (4:00-5:40).** Questions 8 and 9 are one story, from a news extract or a business description. Read it twice - once for the story, once with a pen. Use the stimulus: quote the figure, say what it means for the people.
   > Board: the bakery article from the lesson's practice; underlining with the highlighter on "WhatsApp", "e-bike", "tap", "cloud".
5. **The package table (5:40-6:30).** Package and Motivation; Lite because she uses little data; "cheaper" alone earns no motivation mark.
   > Board: the table row filled in; a cross through "cheaper".
6. **Sign-off.**
