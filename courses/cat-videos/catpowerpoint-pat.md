Lesson: `content/catpowerpoint/pat.php`. Screen recordings in the **CAT VM
`itcoder-cat`** (Word, Excel and PowerPoint 365), board scenes in the CAT
marker style with Clicky. Thabo's PAT on phones for school work, as in CAT
Theory 10 lesson 36 (cattheory10 reports).

## catpowerpoint-06.1 From a report to a presentation (about 8 min)

**Goes:** after section `#integrate` (after the linked-chart question).
**The pupil can afterwards:** plan a summary presentation from a report as a storyboard, and build it from a Word outline and an Excel chart.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · PAT`, title "15 pages, *7 slides*"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:40).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. Thabo's report is 15
   pages. The teachers' meeting gives him ten minutes.
   > Board: a tall stack of pages beside a small stack of slides; Clicky with a stopwatch.
2. **A summary, not a copy (0:40-1:40).** The question, what you found, what
   it means, what to do - the detail stays in the report. How each board
   uses it (CAPS: presenting the findings; IEB: a third package).
3. **The storyboard (1:40-3:30).** Report part to slide: title page, the
   question, a slide per finding with its chart, conclusion,
   recommendations, sources. Six to ten slides.
   > Board: the table drawn row by row; highlighter on "one slide per finding".
4. **From Word (3:30-5:00).** PhonesReport.docx as an outline; new
   presentation; Slides from Outline; delete the empty first slide; Title
   Slide layout on slide 1; a design.
   > Screen: the whole path.
5. **From Excel (5:00-7:00).** Copy the chart; Ctrl+V on the Finding 1
   slide; Paste Options > Keep Source Formatting & Link Data; change 40 to
   42 in Excel and watch it follow. Hand in both files, in one folder.
   > Screen: Excel and PowerPoint side by side.
6. **Sign-off.**

## catpowerpoint-06.2 Finishing and checking (about 6 min)

**Goes:** after section `#check` (before the written question).
**The pupil can afterwards:** add slide numbers and a footer, link the sources, check the spelling, and go through the hand-in checklist.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · PAT`, title "Before you *hand it in*"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:30).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. One spelling
   mistake on a big screen is very big.
2. **Slide numbers and footer (0:30-2:00).** Insert > Header & Footer: Slide
   number, Footer, Don't show on title slide, Apply to All.
   > Screen: the box, step by step; slide 2 shows its number.
3. **Sources (2:00-3:00).** The sources slide; select the web source, Ctrl+K,
   the address, OK; pictures acknowledged.
4. **Proofing (3:00-4:20).** Review > Spelling (F7): "libary" > Change; a
   name > Ignore. Read every slide - "form" for "from". Run from the
   beginning.
   > Screen: the Spelling pane.
5. **The checklist (4:20-5:40).** Read through the Learn / Memorise
   checklist, ticking Thabo's presentation item by item.
   > Board: the checklist, a tick for each line; highlighter on "hand in the Excel file too".
6. **Sign-off.**
