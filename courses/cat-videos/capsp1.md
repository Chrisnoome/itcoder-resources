Lesson: `content/cattheory12/capsp1.php` (CAPS only - the whole lesson is a
CAPS board section). Two videos: how the memo marks, and a game plan for the
three hours. Board in the CAT marker style with Clicky
(brand/cat-art-style.md); yellow highlighter on any words on screen being

## cat12-25.1 Paper 1 - one tick per step (about 7 min)

**Goes:** after section `#marking` - the comment after `w25Formula`.
**The pupil can afterwards:** say how Paper 1 is built, and explain why a partly right formula still earns marks and a typed answer earns none.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · PAPER 1`, title "One *tick* per step"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:40).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. Lerato typed 184 500 into the total cell. The number is right. She got nothing. Why?
   > Board: a spreadsheet cell with 184 500; Clicky pointing at it; a red pen hovering with no tick.
2. **How the paper is built (0:40-1:50).** Three hours, 150 marks, seven questions, all on the computer. Word processing 45, spreadsheet 40, database 35, HTML 15, Question 7 15. One scenario for the whole paper; the tag sheet and input mask sheet; the information sheet filled in afterwards.
   > Board: the seven questions as a bar, block by block; highlighter on "45".
3. **The data files (1:50-3:00).** The password-protected .exe: double-click, Extract, Show Password, type it exactly, check the files, delete the .exe, rename the folder with your examination number. File names start with the question number.
   > Screen: a practice .exe made for the video (password `Practice1`), extracted step by step; the folder renamed to a made-up examination number.
4. **One tick per step (3:00-4:40).** The SUMIF example: SUMIF, the range to check, the criterion, the range to add - four ticks. Three right of four still earns 3.
   > Screen: `=SUMIF(D2:D60,"Full-time",F2:F60)` typed into a practice workbook; each part gets a yellow band as it is named.
   > Board: four ticks drawn one by one beside the parts.
5. **The rules above every spreadsheet question (4:40-6:00).** The formula, not the value. Your references may differ. Helper cells are allowed. A named range may replace a range. It must survive a data change - change a number and watch the typed total stay wrong.
   > Screen: change one fee; the formula total updates, the typed total does not.
6. **More than one right answer; pictures, HTML, queries (6:00-6:40).** OR alternatives (MAX or LARGE). One tick per visible difference in a picture. HTML marked from the code, plus one mark for closing and nesting tags. AND across a row, OR down the rows.
   > Board: the query grid with two criteria on one row (AND) and on two rows (OR).
7. **Sign-off.**

## cat12-25.2 Three hours, seven files - a Paper 1 game plan (about 6 min)

**Goes:** after section `#lost` - the comment after `mw25Slips`.
**The pupil can afterwards:** plan the three hours, save every answer where the marker will find it, and name the common ways marks are lost.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · PAPER 1`, title "Three hours, *seven* files"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:30).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. A perfect formula, saved as Document1 in Downloads. Marked as: not found.
   > Board: the lost-file drawing; highlighter on "Downloads".
2. **The budget (0:30-1:50).** About 1.2 minutes a mark. Five minutes to start; word processing 50, spreadsheet 45, database 40, HTML and Question 7 about 18 each; four minutes to check.
   > Board: a clock face divided into the blocks as they are named.
3. **Technique (1:50-3:20).** Read the scenario and skim the paper. Any order - strongest first. Examination number in the header first. Read every part to the end. Stuck for three minutes? Move on. Do the part you can.
   > Board: six numbered steps appear one by one; Clicky ticks each.
4. **Saving (3:20-4:40).** The exam folder, not Documents. The exact name the paper gives. Save often - Ctrl+S. No extra copies; never delete an original. Close every file at the end.
   > Screen: Save As in Word, the address bar showing the exam folder, the file name typed exactly; highlighter on the address bar and the name.
5. **How marks are lost (4:40-5:40).** Typed numbers; references that should be absolute; doing more than asked; the last line of a part; the notes under a picture; the wrong query row; HTML in Word or a tag left open; the wrong place or name; a blank part.
   > Board: the list as a strip of nine slips, each crossed out by Clicky.
6. **Sign-off.**
