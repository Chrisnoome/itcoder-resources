# catword lesson 21: Tracking changes - videos

Lesson: `content/catword/tracking.php` (Grade 12). Screen recordings in the
CAT VM (Word 365), starting from Farewell letter.docx (G:\My
Drive\CAT\Word): Thabo's letter about the matric farewell with Ms Naidoo's
and Mr Khumalo's tracked changes. Set Word's user name for the recording
(File > Options > General, and "Always use these values regardless of sign
in to Office" ticked) so that no account name shows on a change or a
comment; never show the title bar. The Word window wide enough (about 1900
px) for the Review tab's Tracking, Changes and Protect groups to show in
full. Board scenes in the CAT marker style with Clicky; yellow highlighter
on what is talked about. Compare and Restrict Editing are IEB Grade 12 - say
so once when they come up. Never "in the exam".

## catword-21.1 Track Changes and the markup (about 7 min)

**Goes:** after the written question `wWhyTrack`, before section `#finish`
(the comment `// VIDEO catword-21.1`).
**The pupil can afterwards:** turn tracking on and off, read the markup
(insertions, deletions, formatting, the change line), choose Simple, All,
No Markup or Original, and open the Reviewing Pane.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · WORD`, title "Who changed *my* letter?"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:40).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. Thabo sent his
   letter to two people and got back two changed copies - and spent half an
   hour finding what was different.
   > Board: two letters side by side, Clicky with a magnifying glass going
   > line by line, sweat drops.
2. **What Track Changes records (0:40-1:40).** What, who and when; the
   writer accepts or rejects later. Comments are for remarks about the words.
   > Board: a change as a sticky note with three labels: WHAT, WHO, WHEN;
   > yellow highlighter on WHO.
3. **Turning it on (1:40-2:40).** A clean copy of the letter: Review >
   Track Changes (the button stays shaded). Ctrl+Shift+E does the same.
   Change 22:00 to 23:00 - it shows crossed out and underlined.
   > Screen: the Review tab, the Tracking group, the edit.
4. **Reading the markup (2:40-4:20).** Farewell letter.docx in All Markup:
   underlined is added, crossed out is deleted, "Formatted: Font: Bold" in
   the margin, the line in the left margin; two colours for two reviewers;
   point to a change for the name and time.
   > Screen: the pointer resting on each kind of change in turn.
5. **Display for Review (4:20-5:40).** Simple Markup, All Markup, No Markup,
   Original - each one; "this is the screen only, the changes are still in
   the file". Show Markup > Specific People.
   > Board: a pair of sunglasses labelled No Markup over a page that is
   > still covered in red underneath.
6. **The Reviewing Pane (5:40-6:40).** Review > Reviewing Pane: "8
   revisions", each with its reviewer; click one to jump to it.
7. **Sign-off.**

## catword-21.2 Accepting and rejecting changes (about 7 min)

**Goes:** after the reveal `rLeftOver`, before the IEB section "Comparing
two versions" (the comment `// VIDEO catword-21.2`).
**The pupil can afterwards:** go through the changes with Next, accept and
reject them, use Accept All, finish a reviewed document (comments deleted,
tracking off, Inspect Document), and - IEB - compare two versions and
restrict editing to tracked changes.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · WORD`, title "Keep it or *bin* it"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:30).** Eight changes, two reviewers, and Thabo decides.
   > Board: Clicky as a judge with two stamps, ACCEPT and REJECT.
2. **Next, Accept, Reject (0:30-2:30).** Ctrl+Home; Review > Next selects
   the end-time change: Accept, Accept (the deletion and the insertion).
   The price change: Reject, Reject - R150 is back. Mr Khumalo's deletion of
   "or by EFT": Reject - it comes back. Word moves on by itself each time.
   > Screen: the Changes group, each click; yellow highlighter on the text
   > that stays.
3. **Accept means yes, reject means no (2:30-3:30).** The four cases:
   accept an insertion (stays), reject a deletion (stays), accept a deletion
   (goes), reject an insertion (goes). A replaced word is two changes.
   > Board: a two-by-two grid with arrows in and out of a page.
4. **All at once (3:30-4:10).** The Accept arrow: Accept This Change, Accept
   All Changes, Accept All Changes and Stop Tracking. Right-click a change.
5. **Before you send it (4:10-5:20).** Delete all comments, Track Changes
   off, File > Info > Check for Issues > Inspect Document. Lerato's CV with
   No Markup on - and the company saw it all.
   > Board: a CV arriving at an office, covered in red; Clicky facepalming.
6. **IEB: Compare (5:20-6:10).** Review > Compare > Compare...: original and
   revised, Label changes with, OK - a third document of tracked changes.
   Combine for several reviewers.
   > Screen: the Compare menu and the Compare Documents box.
7. **IEB: Restrict Editing (6:10-6:50).** Review > Restrict Editing:
   Allow only this type of editing > Tracked changes > Yes, Start
   Enforcing Protection, a password. Block Authors for a shared document.
   > Screen: the Restrict Editing pane.
8. **Sign-off.**
