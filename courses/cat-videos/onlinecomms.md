## cat10-28.1 Messages, social networks and your location (about 7 min)

**Goes:** after section `#location` - the comment after `s28LocationSafe`, before the CAPS section.
**The pupil can afterwards:** tell SMS, MMS and instant messaging apart and say what each needs; say what a social network is, with its building blocks and a good and a bad side; say what GPS and location sharing are and share a location safely.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · COMMUNICATION`, title "Gogo's phone got *smart*"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:40).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. A year ago Gogo's phone made calls and sent SMSes. Now: voice notes every morning, Thabo's concert on a video call, a baking page, and a dot on Lerato's map on taxi days.
   > Board: Gogo with her phone, four little bubbles around her; Clicky waving.
2. **SMS and MMS (0:40-1:50).** SMS: text over the cellphone network, any phone, no data, 160 characters, airtime (about 50c). MMS: a picture, sound or short video over the cellphone network, costs more. Why banks and schools still use SMS: it reaches everyone.
   > Board: an old phone with an SMS bubble; coins dropping (the sms-coins doodle).
3. **Instant messaging (1:50-3:20).** Messages over the Internet with an app - WhatsApp. Text, photos, voice notes, calls, groups, read ticks, location. Both need a smartphone or computer, the same app, and data or Wi-Fi. The SMS vs IM table.
   > Board: the Learn / Memorise table drawn row by row, highlighter on each row as it is said.
4. **Social networks (3:20-5:00).** A profile, friends or followers, a feed, likes, comments and shares, hashtags (#MatricResults). Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, X, LinkedIn, YouTube - one line each. Good: in touch, free advertising (Botha's Bakery's page), help and news. Bad: public and permanent, strangers, false stories, time, cruelty.
   > Board: a phone screen with a profile, a post with a heart and a comment; a two-column good/bad list.
5. **Your location (5:00-6:40).** GPS: about 30 satellites, the phone works out where it is to a few metres. Location sharing: once, or live for a set time. Gogo on the taxi. The danger: where you live, school, when you're away; quiet sharing in apps; photos carry the place. The four safe habits.
   > Board: satellites over a phone; a map with a moving dot; then a house with a pin and a red cross. Sticky note: the four habits.
6. **Sign-off.**

## cat10-28.2 Publishing, meeting online and choosing the right tool (about 7 min)

**Goes:** after section `#choosing` - the comment after `m28Choose`.
**The pupil can afterwards:** say what a blog, a vlog, a podcast and a wiki are; use a wiki as a starting point; (CAPS) join an online meeting and use its buttons, with an advantage and a disadvantage; choose the right tool for a job.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · COMMUNICATION`, title "Which tool for *which* job?"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:30).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. Gogo turns 80. Twenty relatives, a neighbour with an old phone, an uncle in London. One message won't reach them all.
   > Board: a birthday cake with lines to stick figures in three cities and London.
2. **Publishing (0:30-2:00).** Blog (weblog) - written; vlog - video; podcast - sound, followed in an app, new episodes arrive by themselves. Anyone can publish - nobody checks it first. A podcast's small files suit a taxi ride.
   > Board: three columns - a page, a camera, a microphone; a taxi with earphones.
3. **Wikis (2:00-3:00).** A website its readers build together; Wikipedia; history and footnotes. Use it as a starting point: the four steps (read, follow the footnotes, check, name the real sources).
   > Board: a wiki page with an Edit tab and a numbered footnote; the four steps numbered.
4. **CAPS: online meetings (3:00-5:00).** Teams, Zoom, Google Meet. Joining: the link, click, check camera and mic, Join. Mute, camera, share screen, chat, raise hand, leave. Good: no travel, from anywhere, share and record. Bad: data and connection, load shedding, harder to read faces, tiring. Manners: on time, quiet, mute, look at the camera.
   > Board: the identify-l30 meeting screen with the buttons along the bottom, each highlighted as named. Note: a "CAPS" flag in the corner for this scene.
5. **Choosing the right tool (5:00-6:40).** The three questions: who is it for, what do they have, how formal? The table: e-mail, SMS, IM, social network, blog/vlog/podcast, online meeting. Back to Gogo's birthday: SMS for the neighbour, a WhatsApp group for the family, a video call for London, live location for the taxi.
   > Board: the three questions on a sticky note; lines from each guest to the right tool.
6. **Sign-off.**
