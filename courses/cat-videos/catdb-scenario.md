## catdb-16.1 A whole database task, start to finish (about 9 min)

**Goes:** after the study block.
**The pupil can afterwards:** read a scenario and the database first; work in
the order design - form - queries - report; see each instruction as a mark;
check every answer; fix the common errors.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · DATABASES`, title "The whole *task*, in order"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:40).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. The fun run in three
   weeks, a half-done database, a list of jobs.
2. **Read first (0:40-1:50).** Tables, data, record count, empty values,
   "5 km" with its space.
   > Screen: tblRunners in Datasheet and Design View.
3. **Table design (1:50-3:30).** Surname 30 and Required; Gender 1 and >L; Age
   Between 7 And 90 with text; FinishMin one decimal; save and test the data.
4. **Queries (3:30-6:00).** qryJuniors (<18, Ascending); qryNoClub (Is Null,
   Show off); qryLong (the two-row grid); qryOwes (EarlyBird); qryPerDistance (totals).
5. **Form and report (6:00-7:20).** A quick form; a grouped report with =Count(*) and a label.
6. **Check and fix (7:20-8:40).** Counts (16 split into 5 km, 10 km, 21 km);
   spot-checks; the troubleshooting table.
7. **Sign-off.**
