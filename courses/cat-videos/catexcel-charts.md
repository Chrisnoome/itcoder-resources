## catexcel-07.1 Making and finishing a chart (about 8 min)

**Goes:** after the prose block "Making a column chart" (section `#make`),
before the simulation `simColumnChart`.
**The pupil can afterwards:** choose column, bar or pie (and line, CAPS) for
a job; make a chart from selected data; give it a proper title; add data
labels, axis titles and a legend; name the parts of a chart.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · SPREADSHEETS`, title "Numbers into a *chart*"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:40).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. 420, 260, 180, 150,
   90 - which item sells best? Now the same five numbers as bars. The tallest
   bar wins before you have read a number.
   > Board: Clicky holding a list of numbers in one hand and a bar chart in the other; the list crossed out.
2. **Which chart (0:40-2:10).** Column to compare a few groups; bar for long
   names or many items; pie for parts of one whole - one set of numbers, a
   few slices; line (CAPS) for change over time.
   > Board: four little charts drawn one after the other, each with its job written under it in highlighter.
3. **Making it (2:10-3:40).** Click in the table, Ctrl+A. Insert tab,
   Charts group, Insert Column or Bar Chart, Clustered Column. The Chart
   Design and Format tabs appear.
   > Screen: each click, slowly; highlighter on the selected A1:B6 and on the new tabs.
4. **The parts (3:40-5:00).** Chart title, horizontal axis (the groups),
   vertical axis (the numbers and scale), axis titles, legend, data labels,
   gridlines.
   > Screen: the finished chart (parts-1); highlighter on each part as it is named.
5. **Title and labels (5:00-6:40).** Click the title, type "Botha's Bakery:
   items sold this week", Enter. Chart Design > Add Chart Element > Data
   Labels > Outside End. The + button beside the chart does the same.
   Moving (drag the edge), sizing (a corner), Change Chart Type, Delete.
   > Screen: the typing appearing in the Formula Bar; the menu and its submenu.
6. **A pie, and reading it (6:40-7:40).** Insert > Insert Pie or Doughnut
   Chart > Pie. Bread is 38% - with muffins, just under half.
   > Screen: the pie with percentages; highlighter on the Bread and Muffins slices.
7. **Sign-off.**
