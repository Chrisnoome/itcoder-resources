## cat12-30.1 The IEB PAT, task by task (about 8 min)

**Goes:** after section `#phase2` - the comment after `r30Integration`.
**The pupil can afterwards:** describe the two phases and four tasks with their marks, and work out a package mark from the best two.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · THE PAT`, title "170 marks, *four* tasks"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:40).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. An exam hides its memo. The PAT hands you its rubric on day one - 170 marks of it.
   > Board: a rubric sheet; Clicky holding it up like a treasure map.
2. **What it is worth (0:40-1:30).** 100 of the final 400; the rubric's 170 converted to 100. You choose the topic; your teacher approves it, marks it; the IEB moderates.
   > Board: four blocks of 100; highlighter on the PAT block; "170 -> 100".
3. **The four tasks (1:30-2:30).** Phase 1: Task 1 (33), Task 2 (37). Phase 2: Task 1 packages (75), Task 2 report (25).
   > Board: the staircase drawing with four steps and their marks.
4. **Phase 1, Task 1 (2:30-3:50).** Task definition 5, main question 2, at least 15 questions of different types 10, grouped 2, questionnaire 2, three or more kinds of source 6, criteria 6.
   > Board: the line-up drawing; Naledi's eleven "How many" questions crossed out and replaced by a mix.
5. **Phase 1, Task 2 (3:50-5:00).** Information for all questions; three good sources; summarised and cross-referenced; quality evidence; the reference list (6); framework; a three-application plan; integration; spellcheck and time - the cheap 5.
   > Board: the rubric lines listed; highlighter on "spellcheck" and "due date".
6. **The packages (5:00-6:50).** Word processing 25, compulsory; the best two of spreadsheet, database and a third package - presentation, video or website. Naledi's 20, 22, 14, 19 = 61. What each rubric looks for; the database minimum; integration - a linked chart, not a pasted one.
   > Board: four package cards; the 14 card slides out; the sum written beneath.
7. **Sign-off.**

## cat12-30.2 Pre-checking your PAT against the rubric (about 6 min)

**Goes:** after section `#precheck` - the comment after `o30Precheck`.
**The pupil can afterwards:** describe the report rubric, explain how the PAT is moderated, and pre-check a task against its rubric before handing in.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · THE PAT`, title "Be your own *marker*"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:30).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. Naledi's report answers only half of her questions. The biggest line on the rubric is about to cost her.
   > Board: the report stack with half its question tags missing.
2. **The report rubric (0:30-2:20).** 35 converted to 25: title page, automatic contents, introduction, outline and styles, the body (10) - every Phase 1 question answered - conclusion, self-evaluation, bibliography, spellcheck, time.
   > Board: the rubric table; highlighter on "Body and content 10".
3. **Moderation and honesty (2:20-3:30).** The teacher marks, with a mark or comment on the work; the Regional Moderator samples 10%, at least five; national moderation can call every PAT. The declaration, signed by you and your teacher; AI only as allowed.
   > Board: a class of 30 PAT folders; five lifted out by a moderator's hand; the consent-box drawing.
4. **The pre-check (3:30-5:30).** Open the rubric; find the evidence for each line and note its page; count; mark done, weak or missing; fix the missing first - the cheap ones; check the thread; hand in on time.
   > Board: the red-pen drawing; a rubric with page numbers written beside each line and three coloured marks.
5. **Sign-off.**
