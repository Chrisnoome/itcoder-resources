## cathtml-03.1 Paragraphs, line breaks and lines (about 6 min)

**Goes:** after the markwords question in section `#hr` (the comment
`// VIDEO cathtml-03.1`).
**The pupil can afterwards:** say what the browser does with Enter and
spaces in the code, and use p, br and hr - knowing br and hr have no
closing tag.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · HTML`, title "Why *Enter* does nothing"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:40).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. Thabo types Mr
   Botha's address on three neat lines - and the browser puts it all on one.
   > Screen: the address on three lines in Notepad++, then on one line in Edge.
2. **What the browser ignores (0:40-1:40).** A new line becomes a space; many
   spaces one; empty lines vanish. Only tags decide where text goes - so you
   can lay out code for yourself.
   > Board: Clicky squashing a stack of spaces into one.
3. **Paragraphs (1:40-2:40).** p ... /p: a block with space above and below;
   each paragraph its own pair.
   > Screen: two paragraphs, save, refresh - the gap between them highlighted.
4. **Line breaks (2:40-4:00).** br: next line, no gap, no closing tag. The
   address with br. Also written br/ - never /br. Shift+Enter in Word. Not a
   row of br tags to make space.
   > Screen: add br to the address lines, save, refresh.
5. **Lines across the page (4:00-5:00).** hr: a horizontal rule; between
   parts, not inside a paragraph; no closing tag; width and size in lesson 5.
   > Screen: an hr under the heading.
6. **Sign-off.** In the lesson: fix the Find us page, and put Thabo's poem
   online.
