# catword lesson 6: Page layout - videos

Lesson: `content/catword/pagelayout.php` (Grade 10). Screen recordings in the
CAT VM (Word 365), starting from Newsletter.docx (G:\My Drive\CAT\Word) and a
new blank document for Mr Botha's sign. Never show the title bar's account.
Board scenes in the CAT marker style with Clicky; yellow highlighter on what
is talked about.

## catword-06.1 Margins, orientation and size (about 6 min)

**Goes:** after the typed question `t6Margin` in section `#pagesetup` (the
comment `// VIDEO catword-06.1`).
**The pupil can afterwards:** name margins, orientation and size, change them
from the Layout tab's Page Setup group, set an exact margin in the Page Setup
dialog box, and choose the right setting for a job.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · WORD`, title "The *shape* of the page"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:40).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. Mr Botha's sign,
   CLOSED ON MONDAY, does not fit across a tall page.
   > Board: a tall page with the words falling off its right edge; Clicky pushing the page over onto its side.
2. **The three settings (0:40-1:40).** Margins (the white edge), orientation
   (portrait, landscape), size (A4 is 21 x 29.7 cm; A5 is half of it).
   > Board: an A4 page with its four margins shaded and labelled; an A4 folded into two A5s.
3. **Landscape (1:40-2:40).** Layout tab, Page Setup group, Orientation,
   Landscape. The whole document turns.
   > Screen: the sign in Word at 50% zoom; click Layout, Orientation, Landscape.
4. **Margins (2:40-4:30).** The Margins menu: Normal, Narrow, Wide. The
   dialog box launcher: the Page Setup dialog box, the Margins tab, Top 1.5,
   Enter = OK. Custom Margins opens the same box. The Paper tab: A4.
   > Screen: highlighter on the tiny launcher arrow; type 1.5 in Top; Enter.
5. **Which one for the job? (4:30-5:30).** A seven-column timetable:
   landscape. Words too high on the sign: a smaller top margin.
   > Board: the timetable before and after.
6. **Sign-off.**

## catword-06.2 Columns, headers and the page background (about 8 min)

**Goes:** after the quiz `q6Hyphen` in section `#columns` (the comment
`// VIDEO catword-06.2`).
**The pupil can afterwards:** set selected text in two columns with a line
between, turn on hyphenation, use page and section breaks, add a header, a
footer and page numbers, and add a watermark, page colour and page border.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · WORD`, title "From page to *newsletter*"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:30).** Phumlani News looks like homework.
   > Board: a plain page beside a newspaper; Clicky cutting the page into two strips.
2. **Columns (0:30-2:30).** Select the stories (not the title); Layout,
   Columns, More Columns..., Two, Line between, OK. Show/Hide: the Continuous
   section break Word added.
   > Screen: exactly those clicks; highlighter on "Apply to".
3. **Hyphenation (2:30-3:30).** The gaps in justified narrow columns;
   Layout, Hyphenation, Automatic. Why never type hyphens yourself.
   > Screen: before and after, side by side.
4. **Page and section breaks (3:30-4:30).** Ctrl+Enter; Breaks > Next Page
   and Continuous; one landscape page in a portrait report.
   > Board: eight pages in a row, page 5 turned sideways, two section-break marks round it.
5. **Headers, footers, page numbers (4:30-6:30).** Double-click the top
   margin, type Phumlani News - Term 3, Close Header and Footer. Insert, Page
   Number, Bottom of Page, Plain Number 2; Page X of Y; Different First Page.
   > Screen: the newsletter; highlighter on the pale header.
6. **The page background (6:30-7:30).** Design tab: Watermark DRAFT, Page
   Color (shown, not printed by default), Page Borders: Box, OK.
   > Screen: the newsletter at 50%; then the print preview without the colour.
7. **Cover page and line numbers (7:30-8:00).** Insert > Cover Page (CAPS);
   Layout > Line Numbers > Continuous (IEB).
8. **Sign-off.**
