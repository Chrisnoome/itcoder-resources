# CAT 11 lesson 8: System requirements and a slow computer - videos

Lesson file: `AIPascalCourse/content/cattheory11/sysreqs.php`. Two videos.
Everything said or shown here is also in the lesson text (anchors in the
tables below). Screen recordings in the VM (Windows 11). PixelPal 6 and
FamilyTree Plus are made up - draw their requirement tables on the board,
never show a real product's page as if it were them.

## cat11-08.1 Will it run? Reading system requirements (about 7 min)

**Goes:** after the order question `o8About`, at the end of section
`#checking` (before `#slow`).
**The pupil can afterwards:** say what system requirements are, tell
minimum from recommended, read a requirements table against a computer's
specifications, say what the CPU, GPU, RAM and storage do for a program,
name the usual compatibility issues, and find their own computer's
specifications.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · SOFTWARE`, title "Will it *run* on my laptop?"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:40).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. Thabo saved three
   months for PixelPal 6. "Setup cannot continue - needs 10 GB free, there
   is 6 GB." He made space; it installed; every filter froze the laptop.
   > Board: the error box drawn as a callout; Clicky pushing a too-big box
   > into a too-small laptop.
2. **System requirements (0:40-1:50).** What a program needs: OS, CPU, RAM,
   free space, graphics, screen. Where to find them. Minimum: installs and
   runs at all, slowly. Recommended: runs well. Aim for recommended - other
   programs share the computer.
   > Board: two columns headed MINIMUM and RECOMMENDED; yellow band on
   > "runs well".
3. **Reading the table (1:50-3:30).** PixelPal 6 against Thabo's laptop, one
   line at a time: OS ok, CPU 2 cores vs 4 - below, RAM 8 = 8 ok, space 6 vs
   10 - below, graphics ok, screen ok. Any line below the minimum means
   trouble. Space can be made; a slow processor must be replaced.
   > Board: the table drawn; a tick or a cross added line by line; pink
   > highlighter on the two crosses.
4. **What each part does for the program (3:30-4:30).** CPU does the work;
   GPU draws the picture; RAM holds the program while it runs; storage keeps
   it installed. The impact of hardware on software.
   > Board: four boxes, each with a one-line job and a tiny drawing.
5. **Compatibility (4:30-5:40).** Wrong OS (an .exe on a tablet); an OS too
   old; hardware too weak; a driver not made for the new OS; a new Excel
   function showing #NAME? in an old Excel. Windows 11's own requirements;
   Windows 10 support ended 14 October 2025.
   > Board: five plugs that do not fit their sockets, one per issue.
6. **Checking your own computer (5:40-6:50).** Settings, System, About
   (Windows key + Pause): Processor, Installed RAM, Edition, Version. This
   PC: the drive bar (red when nearly full); Settings, System, Storage. Task
   Manager (Ctrl + Shift + Esc), Performance: CPU, Memory, Disk, GPU.
   > Screen: VM - exactly these three places; yellow highlighter on each
   > line read.
7. **Sign-off.**

## cat11-08.2 Speeding up a slow computer (about 8 min)

**Goes:** after the written question `w8FreeSpace`, at the end of section
`#fixes` (before the scenario).
**The pupil can afterwards:** read the signs of each cause of a slow
computer, explain why a full drive and malware slow it down, and use Disk
Clean-up, the Startup settings and a full anti-malware scan - free fixes
first.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · SOFTWARE`, title "Make it *fast* again - for free"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:30).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. Gogo's laptop was
   quick four years ago. Now: three minutes to start, a spinning pointer
   every time. Computers do not wear out like shoes - there is a cause.
   > Board: a laptop with cobwebs and a snail on the keyboard; Clicky with a
   > magnifying glass.
2. **Signs point to causes (0:30-2:30).** The table: too little RAM, a full
   drive, malware, start-up programs, an old hard drive, heat and dust, new
   software on old hardware - each with its signs.
   > Board: the Learn / Memorise table built row by row; a detective's
   > notebook of clues.
3. **Why a full drive slows it (2:30-3:10).** Windows needs room for
   temporary files and updates, and for parts of programs moved out of a
   full RAM. Keep a tenth free.
   > Board: a drive drawn as a cupboard, stuffed to the door.
4. **How malware slows it (3:10-3:50).** Adware, spyware, a cryptocurrency
   miner - working in the background with your processor, RAM and data.
   > Board: little gremlins pedalling inside the laptop.
5. **Disk Clean-up (3:50-5:00).** Type Disk Cleanup; drive C:; tick
   Temporary files, Temporary Internet Files, Thumbnails, Recycle Bin (not
   Downloads unchecked); Clean up system files; OK, Delete Files. Then
   uninstall, move photos, check Downloads. Storage Sense is Grade 12.
   > Screen: VM - exactly these steps; yellow highlighter on each tick.
6. **Start-up programs (5:00-6:00).** Settings, Apps, Startup: the
   switches and their impact; switch off from High down; keep the
   anti-malware and drivers; restart. Task Manager's Startup apps too.
   > Screen: VM - exactly these steps.
7. **A full scan (6:00-7:00).** Windows Security, Virus & threat protection,
   check for updates, Scan options, Full scan, Scan now; remove what it
   finds; uninstall unknown programs and extensions.
   > Screen: VM - start a full scan (cut while it runs).
8. **Then the ones that cost money (7:00-7:30).** More RAM (if not
   soldered), an SSD, a new computer.
   > Board: three price tags.
9. **Sign-off.**
