# catword lesson 8: Pictures, shapes and WordArt - videos

Lesson: `content/catword/illustrations.php` (Grade 10). Screen recordings in
the CAT VM (Word 365): Thabo's e-waste project, with e-waste.jpg in the VM's
Pictures folder (the course's own picture). Never show the title bar's
account. Board scenes in the CAT marker style with Clicky; yellow highlighter
on what is talked about.

## catword-08.1 Pictures and text wrapping (about 7 min)

**Goes:** after the match `g8Wrap` in section `#wrap` (the comment
`// VIDEO catword-08.1`).
**The pupil can afterwards:** insert a picture from the computer, resize it
without squashing it, rotate and crop it, and choose the right text wrapping
and position.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · WORD`, title "Make the text *wrap*"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:40).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. Three pages of
   words about e-waste; one photo says more.
   > Board: a wall of text, Clicky yawning; then the same page with a photo, Clicky leaning in.
2. **Insert a picture (0:40-1:50).** Insert, Pictures, This Device...,
   double-click e-waste. Stock Images; other people's pictures need
   permission and a source line.
   > Screen: exactly those clicks.
3. **Handles (1:50-3:20).** A corner handle (keeps the shape) vs a side
   handle (squashes it); the rotate handle; Crop and the black crop handles;
   Compress Pictures.
   > Board: a squashed face beside a happy one.
4. **Wrap Text (3:20-5:40).** In Line with Text (one big letter); Picture
   Format, Wrap Text, Square; Tight; Top and Bottom; Behind Text; In Front of
   Text; the Layout Options button; Position.
   > Screen: the photo moved round the page in each setting.
5. **Which wrap? (5:40-6:40).** Five quick jobs and their wraps.
6. **Sign-off.**

## catword-08.2 Shapes, WordArt and SmartArt (about 8 min)

**Goes:** after the written question `w8SmartArt` in section `#smartart`
(the comment `// VIDEO catword-08.2`).
**The pupil can afterwards:** draw shapes (with Shift), fill and outline
them, add icons, a text box and WordArt, build a SmartArt process, take a
screenshot or screen clipping - and (IEB) group, align and layer objects and
insert a chart.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · WORD`, title "Shapes, *WordArt*, SmartArt"

### Scenes

1. **Shapes (0:00-1:40).** Insert, Shapes, the Oval; drag; Shift for a
   circle; Shape Fill, Shape Outline; type in a shape. Icons; 3D Models.
2. **Text box and WordArt (1:40-3:20).** Draw Text Box; a "Did you know?"
   box. Insert, WordArt, the first style, type Say no to e-waste. One style
   per document.
3. **SmartArt (3:20-5:20).** Insert, SmartArt, Process, Basic Process, OK;
   the Text Pane: Collect, Sort, Recycle; Enter adds a box. List, Cycle,
   Hierarchy.
   > Board: the four kinds as tiny sketches.
4. **Screenshots (5:20-6:20).** Insert, Screenshot, Screen Clipping round
   the depot's opening times; Windows key + Shift + S and Ctrl+V. A source line.
5. **IEB: arrange and charts (6:20-7:40).** Ctrl+click three objects, Group;
   Align; Bring Forward and Send Backward; the Selection Pane; Rotate.
   Insert, Chart, Column, OK; type the numbers; Edit Data.
6. **Sign-off.**
