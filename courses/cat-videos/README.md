# CAT Theory videos - plans for the video chat

Chris, 6 October 2026: "plan videos for the lessons that the video chat can
create. aim for videos of - 10 minutes if possible. all video content must be
available in the text as well."

**206 videos for CAT Theory Grades 10-12** (Grade 10: 79 videos for 36 lessons, Grade 11: 61 videos for 28 lessons, Grade 12: 66 videos for 30 lessons), about 24 hours in all, each under 10 minutes. One file per lesson. Each video has its place in the
lesson (a `// VIDEO catNN-NN.n` comment in
`AIPascalCourse/content/cattheory1N/<lesson>.php`), scenes with draft narration
and `> Board` / `> Screen` directions, and a table showing where every point is
in the lesson text.

## Rules for these videos

- The method and voice of [../tutorial-videos.md](../tutorial-videos.md):
  Chris's cloned voice, narration first, "Hi, and welcome to BestLessons.",
  the BestLessons sign-off, title card and thumbnail (a `thumbs-cat.json` in
  `brand/youtube/source/`, CAT colours `#1b2233` with accent `#ff6a4d`),
  `.srt` captions.
- **Board scenes in the CAT art style** - black marker, highlighter swipes,
  sticky notes and Clicky the mouse pointer
  ([../../brand/cat-art-style.md](../../brand/cat-art-style.md)), not the IT
  blue pen. Yellow highlighter on any words on screen being talked about.
- **Screen recordings** (File Explorer, Settings, Edge, Excel) in the video
  VM, Windows 11 and Microsoft 365. Check each menu name in the VM before
  recording; the plans say what is clicked.
- **Nothing in a video that is not in the lesson text.** If a video needs a
  new point, add it to the lesson first.
- When a video is on YouTube, the lesson's `// VIDEO` comment becomes a
  `video` block with its id - never before (ids are never invented).
- **Finished videos go in the one flat 'for upload' folder** (Chris, 6
  October 2026), with their thumbnail, captions and YouTube text - not left
  in a folder tree. Chris moves each to 'uploaded' once it is on YouTube.
  The video chat owns the folder's path (tutorial-videos.md).
- The draft narration is a starting point. Make the video straight away;
  Chris corrects from the video (tutorial-videos.md, 5 October 2026).
