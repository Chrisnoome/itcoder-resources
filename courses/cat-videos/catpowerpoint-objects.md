# Presentations lesson 3: Tables, charts and pictures - videos

Lesson: `content/catpowerpoint/objects.php`. Screen recordings in the **CAT
VM `itcoder-cat`** (PowerPoint 365 and Excel 365), board scenes in the CAT
marker style with Clicky.

## catpowerpoint-03.1 Tables and charts on slides (about 7 min)

**Goes:** after section `#charts` (before `#excel`).
**The pupil can afterwards:** insert a table and a chart, choose the right chart, and bring a chart in from Excel - embedded, linked or as a picture.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · POWERPOINT`, title "Numbers at a *glance*"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:30).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. Mr Botha's TV in
   the bakery: nobody in a queue reads paragraphs.
   > Board: a queue at the counter, everyone glancing up at a TV.
2. **The six icons (0:30-1:10).** An empty content placeholder: table,
   chart, SmartArt, pictures, stock images, video.
3. **A table (1:10-2:30).** Insert Table: 2 columns, 4 rows; type the
   prices with Tab; Table Design and Layout tabs.
   > Screen: the icon, the box, typing; add a row with Tab in the last cell.
4. **A chart (2:30-4:00).** Insert Chart > Pie > OK; type over the sample
   numbers; close the sheet. Pie, column/bar, line - which for what. A clear
   title.
   > Screen: the Insert Chart box; the small sheet; the pie appears.
5. **From Excel (4:00-6:30).** Copy the chart in Excel, Ctrl+V on the slide,
   Paste Options: embed, link, picture; destination theme or source
   formatting. Change a number in Excel and watch the linked chart follow.
   Integration: each program does its job.
   > Screen: Excel and PowerPoint side by side; the Paste Options menu; the linked chart updating.
6. **Sign-off.**

## catpowerpoint-03.2 Pictures, shapes and SmartArt (about 6 min)

**Goes:** after the Video and sound board section (before the copyright reveal).
**The pupil can afterwards:** insert and resize a picture, draw a shape with words in it, make SmartArt, and add video and sound.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · POWERPOINT`, title "Show it, *don't* say it"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:30).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. New rusks - a
   photo says it better than any sentence.
2. **Pictures (0:30-2:00).** Insert > Pictures: This Device, Stock Images,
   Online Pictures (only what you may use - name the source). Resize from a
   corner; Picture Format. Icons.
   > Screen: insert a photo, drag a side handle (squashed), undo, drag a corner.
3. **Shapes (2:00-3:00).** Insert > Shapes > Star: 5 Points, drag, type
   "New!", Shape Format colour.
4. **SmartArt (3:00-4:30).** Insert > SmartArt > Process > Basic Process >
   OK; Mix the dough, Bake at 05:00, Sell it warm. List, Cycle, Hierarchy
   named.
5. **Video and sound (4:30-5:40).** Insert > Video; Audio > Record Audio;
   the Playback tab. Keep clips short. (CAPS names these; worth knowing.)
   > Screen: record three seconds of voice; the speaker icon; Playback > Start Automatically.
6. **Sign-off.**
