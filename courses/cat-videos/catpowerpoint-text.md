# Presentations lesson 2: Text and lists on slides - videos

Lesson: `content/catpowerpoint/text.php`. Screen recordings in the **CAT VM
`itcoder-cat`** (PowerPoint 365 and Word 365), board scenes in the CAT marker
style with Clicky.

## catpowerpoint-02.1 Typing, levels and formatting on slides (about 7 min)

**Goes:** after section `#levels` (before `#format`).
**The pupil can afterwards:** type into placeholders, set list levels with Tab and Shift+Tab, switch bullets and numbering, and format text and paragraphs.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · POWERPOINT`, title "Words that *fit* a slide"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:30).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. Slide 3 is empty,
   and some of Thabo's points belong under others.
   > Board: a list with two points drawn slightly in, Clicky pushing them.
2. **Typing in a placeholder (0:30-1:40).** Click "Click to add title", type
   "Mobile data or Wi-Fi?". Enter in a content placeholder starts a new
   point. Placeholder or text box - why placeholders. AutoFit shrinking text:
   cut words instead.
   > Screen: click the title placeholder, type; type three points; keep typing until AutoFit shrinks it, then undo.
3. **Levels (1:40-3:00).** Select "Costs money" and "Works anywhere", Tab:
   one level in. Shift+Tab back out. Increase / Decrease List Level buttons.
   Bullets and Numbering - numbers only when order matters.
   > Screen: Tab, Shift+Tab; the buttons; Numbering on and off.
4. **Formatting (3:00-5:30).** Select first. Font, size, Increase Font Size
   (Ctrl+Shift+>), bold, italic, underline, Font Color, Text Highlight Color
   (double-click HD, the marker pen). Alignment (Ctrl+E), Line Spacing and
   its options, indentation and the Paragraph box.
   > Screen: each button on the Home tab in turn; highlighter on each name.
5. **One thing stands out (5:30-6:30).** The design chose the fonts; a word
   in bold or highlighted is enough.
   > Board: a slide with everything bold, then one with one word highlighted.
6. **Sign-off.**

## catpowerpoint-02.2 Replace, and slides from a Word outline (about 6 min)

**Goes:** after section `#outline` (after the outline simulation).
**The pupil can afterwards:** cut, copy and paste text, use Find and Replace, set out a Word outline and make slides from it.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · POWERPOINT`, title "Never type it *twice*"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:30).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. "Cellphone" on three
   slides, and the app tips already typed in Word.
2. **Editing keys (0:30-1:20).** Ctrl+X, Ctrl+C, Ctrl+V, Ctrl+Z; Delete and
   Backspace.
   > Screen: move a point from slide 4 to slide 2 with cut and paste.
3. **Replace (1:20-2:50).** Ctrl+H: cellphone, phone, Replace All - how many
   it replaced. Ctrl+F to find. The "cat" in "CAT" trap: Match case and
   Find whole words only.
   > Screen: the Replace box, step by step; then tick the two options.
4. **A Word outline (2:50-4:20).** In Word: Heading 1 for each slide title,
   Heading 2 for each point; Normal text is left out. Save.
   > Screen: Word with MoreTips.docx, the Styles gallery, View > Outline to show the levels.
5. **Slides from Outline (4:20-5:30).** PowerPoint: click slide 4, New Slide
   arrow > Slides from Outline > MoreTips.docx > Insert. The new slides take
   the design. Copy-paste for a few lines: Paste Options > Keep Text Only.
   > Screen: the whole path; then paste one line from Word with Keep Text Only.
6. **Sign-off.**
