# CAT 12 lesson 19: The law and your rights - videos

Lesson: `content/cattheory12/lawrights.php`. Three videos: the POPI Act and
your rights (inside the CAPS section - say so in the description), copyright

## cat12-19.1 The POPI Act and your rights (about 8 min)

**Goes:** inside the CAPS section, after `#regulator` - the comment after `w19Breach`.
**The pupil can afterwards:** name the data subject, responsible party and processing, give the eight conditions with an example each, list a data subject's rights, and say what the Information Regulator does.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · THE LAW`, title "Who *sold* my number?"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:40).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. Lerato bought a kettle once. Now the shop sends her SMSes - and has passed her number to a loan company. May it?
   > Board: a phone buzzing with SMSes; a kettle; highlighter on "loan company".
2. **Who is who (0:40-1:40).** The data subject (Gogo), the responsible party (her bank), processing - anything done with the information, even deleting it.
   > Board: Gogo, a bank building and an arrow labelled "processing"; highlighter on each term.
3. **The eight conditions (1:40-4:30).** One at a time, each with what Gogo's bank must do: accountability, processing limitation, purpose specification, further processing limitation, information quality, openness, security safeguards, data subject participation.
   > Board: the eight-row table filled in line by line; highlighter on each condition's name as it is said.
4. **Three special rules (4:30-5:20).** Children need a parent's consent; special personal information is protected more; direct marketing needs opt-in except to customers, and always an opt-out. Lerato's shop: specials yes, selling her number no.
   > Board: three cards; an "Unsubscribe" link circled.
5. **Your rights (5:20-6:20).** Be told; be told of a breach; see; correct or delete; object and refuse marketing; no decision by a computer alone; complain.
   > Board: seven short lines with ticks.
6. **The Information Regulator (6:20-7:40).** Free complaints; enforcement notices; fines up to R10 million, prison up to ten years; the information officer. The first fine: the Department of Justice, R5 million, July 2023. Complaining step by step: write to the information officer, keep copies, the Regulator's form, the investigation.
   > Board: a referee with a whistle (the Regulator); four complaint steps; highlighter on "R10 million".
7. **Sign-off.**

## cat12-19.2 Copyright and fair use - whose is it, and when may I use it? (about 7 min)

**Goes:** after section `#copyright` - the comment after `mw19Reels`.
**The pupil can afterwards:** say how long copyright lasts, explain fair dealing (fair use) and the public domain, tell plagiarism from breaking copyright, and judge posts on social media.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · THE LAW`, title "I gave credit. *Isn't that enough?*"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:40).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. Lerato finds her own photo of Table Mountain in a tour company's advert. Nobody asked her.
   > Board: a photo frame inside an advert; Clicky pointing at it, cross.
2. **Copyright in brief (0:40-1:40).** Intellectual property; copyright is automatic; she may ask for it to be removed, ask to be paid, or go to a lawyer. It lasts until 50 years after the creator dies; then the public domain - Shakespeare.
   > Board: a timeline from "photo taken" to "creator dies + 50 years"; highlighter on "public domain".
3. **Fair dealing (1:40-3:30).** South Africa's name; fair use in America. A small part for research or study, criticism or review, the news. Three questions: how much, why, does it hurt the owner? Always give the source.
   > Board: three question marks becoming three checks; highlighter on "small part".
4. **Plagiarism or copyright? (3:30-5:00).** No credit = plagiarism, an honesty matter, fixed by credit. No permission = breaking copyright, a legal matter, not fixed by credit. One without the other: a Shakespeare speech passed off as yours; a photographer's picture on mugs, with her name.
   > Board: the three-row comparison table; highlighter on "Does credit fix it?".
5. **On social media (5:00-6:30).** Other people's photos and videos need permission - use the share button; the app's music library, not your own CD; take-downs and closed accounts; free to use: your own, public domain, Creative Commons as its licence says. Zodwa's cupcake week, post by post.
   > Board: seven small post cards, four with red crosses.
6. **Sign-off.**

## cat12-19.3 Identity theft - the warning signs and what to do (about 6 min)

**Goes:** after section `#identity` - the comment after `w19IdProtect`.
**The pupil can afterwards:** say where criminals get personal details, spot the warning signs (including a SIM swap), put the steps after identity theft in order, and recommend ways to protect an ID.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · THE LAW`, title "A loan Gogo *never* took"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:30).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. Gogo Dlamini gets a letter about a R25 000 loan she never took.
   > Board: an envelope with "R25 000" and a highlighter band; Gogo frowning.
2. **Where they get your details (0:30-1:40).** Phishing; leaks from hacked companies; ID copies left at shops or posted with a CV; social media; a stolen wallet or phone; a SIM swap.
   > Board: six arrows pointing at an ID card.
3. **Warning signs (1:40-2:50).** Accounts and contracts you never opened; money missing; "No service" for no reason - a SIM swap, OTPs going to the criminal; debt collectors; a loan refused.
   > Board: a phone showing "No service", highlighter on it; the OTP flying to a criminal's phone.
4. **What to do (2:50-4:40).** The bank's fraud line (and the network); change passwords from a clean device, two-step verification; police case number, new ID at Home Affairs; SAFPS protective registration, free; a free credit report every year from each bureau; keep records.
   > Board: six numbered steps; highlighter on "fraud line" and "SAFPS".
5. **Protecting yourself (4:40-5:40).** Never post your ID, bank card, boarding pass or matric certificate; ID number only when the law needs it; "copy for ... only, date" across copies; shred papers; two-step verification, never share an OTP.
   > Board: the shredder doodle; an ID copy with "copy for job application only" written across it.
6. **Sign-off.**
