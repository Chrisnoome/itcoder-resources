## catword-12.1 Changing and updating a style (about 8 min)

**Goes:** after the quiz `q12WhatChanges`, before section `#update` (the
comment `// VIDEO catword-12.1`).
**The pupil can afterwards:** change a style with Modify Style, update a
style to match a heading formatted by hand, and explain why a heading
formatted by hand does not follow the style.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · WORD`, title "Change *40 headings* in one go"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:40).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. Ms Naidoo's
   handbook has 40 headings, and the principal wants them all in the
   school's maroon, a little bigger. Forty changes - or one?
   > Board: a stack of pages, every heading circled, Clicky with one big
   > paint roller labelled "Heading 1".
2. **One place for the look (0:40-1:40).** Every paragraph in a style takes
   its look from the style - like a web page's CSS. Change the style, and
   every paragraph in it changes.
3. **Modify Style (1:40-4:00).** Right-click Heading 1 in the Styles group >
   Modify... The Formatting part (font, size 24, colour, alignment); the
   Format button (Paragraph, Border); Only in this document versus New
   documents based on this template; leave Automatically update off. OK -
   every Heading 1 is 24 pt; the Heading 2s did not change.
   > Screen: the clicks above; yellow highlighter on the size box and on
   > "Only in this document".
4. **Update to Match Selection (4:00-5:30).** Make Welcome maroon by hand
   (Font Color, Dark Red), select it, right-click Heading 1 > Update Heading
   1 to Match Selection. All 30 headings turn maroon.
5. **Direct formatting (5:30-7:30).** A heading made green by hand stays
   green - formatting by hand sits on top of the style and wins. Clear All
   Formatting (Home > Font) lets the style show through.
   > Board: a layer cake - style at the bottom, "by hand" icing on top,
   > Clicky scraping the icing off.
6. **Sign-off.**

## catword-12.2 New styles and the Design tab (about 8 min)

**Goes:** after the typed question `t12Default`, before the scenario (the
comment `// VIDEO catword-12.2`).
**The pupil can afterwards:** create a style of their own (Create a Style;
the Styles pane's New Style), choose its type and base style, and change the
whole document's look from the Design tab.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · WORD`, title "Your *own* style - and a new look in one click"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:30).** The handbook's "Remember:" notes need a look of
   their own, the same every time. None of Word's styles fits.
2. **Create a Style (0:30-2:30).** Make the first note italic and dark blue
   by hand; Styles gallery More arrow > Create a Style; name it Note (not
   Style1); OK. Apply Note to the other two notes.
   > Screen: the clicks above; the gallery's new Note tile highlighted.
3. **The Styles pane (2:30-4:30).** Ctrl+Alt+Shift+S (or the Styles group's
   launcher); New Style: Name, Style type (Paragraph or Character - Botha's
   Bakery in the middle of a sentence), Style based on Normal (Note follows
   Normal's font), Style for following paragraph.
   > Board: a family tree - Normal at the top, Note below it, an arrow
   > "font comes from here".
4. **The Design tab (4:30-7:00).** Styles use the theme's fonts and colours.
   Design > Themes, the Style Set gallery, Colors (school colours), Fonts
   (Corbel - every style changes, nothing selected), Paragraph Spacing,
   Effects, Set as Default. A font chosen by hand does not follow.
   > Screen: Design > Fonts > Corbel; yellow highlighter on the headings and
   > body text changing together.
5. **Sign-off.**
