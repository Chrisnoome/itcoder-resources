# catword lesson 11: Styles, a table of contents and references - videos

Lesson: `content/catword/references.php` (Grade 10). Screen recordings in
the CAT VM (Word 365), starting from Phones in class.docx (G:\My
Drive\CAT\Word). Never show the title bar's account. Board scenes in the CAT
marker style with Clicky; yellow highlighter on what is talked about. The
table of contents and Word's citation tools are CAPS Grade 10 and IEB Grade
11: say once, early in each video, "CAPS pupils learn this in Grade 10, IEB
pupils in Grade 11 - and every PAT report needs it". Never "in the exam".

## catword-11.1 Heading styles and a table of contents (about 8 min)

**Goes:** after the CAPS section "A table of contents", before section
`#sources` (the comment `// VIDEO catword-11.1`).
**The pupil can afterwards:** give a report's headings the right levels
(Heading 1, Heading 2), check them in the Navigation pane, insert a table of
contents from the References tab and update it after a change.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · WORD`, title "A contents page that *writes itself*"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:40).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. Lerato typed her
   contents page by hand, added a page in the middle - and every number
   after it was wrong.
   > Board: a contents page with page numbers crossed out in red, Clicky
   > holding an eraser.
2. **Levels (0:40-2:00).** Heading 1 for the main sections, Heading 2 for
   the parts inside them, like chapters and sections of a book; Title once,
   for the name of the document.
   > Board: a book's spine (Heading 1 chapters) with smaller tabs inside
   > (Heading 2); yellow highlighter on "Heading 2".
3. **Applying them (2:00-3:00).** Phones in class.docx: click in
   Introduction, Home > Styles > Heading 1; Ctrl+Alt+1 on What the survey
   found and Conclusion; Ctrl+Alt+2 on Phones for learning and Phones as
   distractions; Ctrl+Shift+N back to Normal.
   > Screen: the clicks above, slowly, the Styles gallery highlighted.
4. **The Navigation pane (3:00-4:20).** View > Navigation Pane (or Ctrl+F,
   Headings). Click Conclusion: Word jumps. The Heading 2s moved in; the
   arrow folds them. Drag Conclusion up and back: the whole section moves.
   A heading made big and bold by hand is missing from the pane.
5. **Insert the table (4:20-5:40).** Click the empty line under the title;
   References > Table of Contents > Automatic Table 1. Ctrl+click a line.
   "No table of contents entries found" - and why.
   > Board: the table of contents as a treasure map, Clicky pointing at
   > page 3.
6. **Update it (5:40-7:20).** Add a section, What teachers said (Heading
   1). References > Update Table > Update entire table > OK. Then add a
   photo so Conclusion moves a page: Update page numbers only. F9 does the
   same. Update last, before printing or a PDF.
7. **Sign-off.**

## catword-11.2 Citations and a bibliography (about 7 min)

**Goes:** after the CAPS section "Citations and a bibliography in Word",
before the scenario (the comment `// VIDEO catword-11.2`).
**The pupil can afterwards:** say why and where to acknowledge a source,
list the details of a reference, choose a reference style, add a source
and cite it with Insert Citation, insert a bibliography and update the
table of contents to include it.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · WORD`, title "Say *where* it came from"

### Scenes

1. **Why (0:00-1:10).** Ms Naidoo's survey: 84 of 120 pupils use their
   phones for schoolwork. The numbers are hers, so Thabo says so - not
   saying is plagiarism (CAT Theory lesson 33).
   > Board: Clicky standing on a giant's shoulders, with a sign "Naidoo,
   > 2026".
2. **Two parts (1:10-2:30).** The citation in the text, (Naidoo, 2026);
   the bibliography at the end, in alphabetical order. Who, when, what,
   where: author, year, title, city and publisher - or the web address and
   the date you looked. Harvard and APA; one style all the way through.
   Exact words in quotation marks too.
   > Board: a reference taken apart, each part highlighted a different
   > colour.
3. **Style and a new source (2:30-4:30).** References > Style: Harvard -
   Anglia. Click before the full stop of "In a survey..."; Insert Citation
   > Add New Source; Type of Source Report; Author Naidoo, P.; the title,
   2026, Soweto, Phumlani Secondary School; OK. (Naidoo, 2026) appears.
   > Screen: the Create Source box filled in slowly, each box highlighted.
4. **Again, and changing style (4:30-5:20).** Cite the same survey at the
   end of "Pupils look up words...": Insert Citation, click it in the list.
   Switch the Style to APA and back: every citation changes. Manage
   Sources.
5. **The bibliography (5:20-6:30).** The empty last line; References >
   Bibliography > Bibliography. Then References > Update Table > Update
   entire table: Bibliography is in the table of contents.
6. **Sign-off.**
