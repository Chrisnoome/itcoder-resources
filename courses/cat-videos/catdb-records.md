## catdb-03.1 Records: enter, delete, sort, filter and replace (about 8 min)

**Goes:** after the study block (first of two comments).
**The pupil can afterwards:** add, change and delete records; read the pencil;
sort; filter by selection and with the column filter; toggle and clear a filter;
Find and Replace with the right Match; (IEB) gridlines and alternate row colour.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · DATABASES`, title "Taming the *records*"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:40).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. Fourteen stalls, and
   every day something changes - a new stall, a fee paid, a cancellation, and
   "Craft" and "Crafts" both in the list.
   > Board: a market stall list with scribbles; Clicky with an eraser.
2. **A new record (0:40-2:00).** New (blank) record; skip the AutoNumber; Tab;
   the pencil; Shift+Enter; Esc.
   > Screen: the simulation: Lerato Khumalo's stall.
3. **Deleting (2:00-2:50).** The record selector, Delete, Yes - no Undo; the
   AutoNumber gap.
4. **Sorting (2:50-3:50).** Click in the column; Ascending, Descending, Remove Sort.
5. **Filters (3:50-5:30).** Selection > Equals "Food"; the column's arrow and
   its tick boxes; Text Filters; Filtered on the bar; Toggle Filter; Clear All
   Filters. A filter hides, never deletes.
   > Board: the sieve.
6. **Find and Replace (5:30-7:00).** Ctrl+H; Look In; Match: Whole Field vs Any
   Part ("Craftss"); Replace All.
7. **IEB: the datasheet's look (7:00-7:40).** Gridlines; Alternate Row Color;
   column widths; Hide and Freeze Fields.
8. **Sign-off.**

## catdb-03.2 Importing a CSV file into Access (about 4 min)

**Goes:** after catdb-03.1.
**The pupil can afterwards:** import a CSV (or a spreadsheet) as a new table.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · DATABASES`, title "Bring in a *CSV* file"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:30).** Three late stalls, in a file from the office. Nobody
   types them in again.
2. **What a CSV is (0:30-1:00).** One record a line, commas between; the names
   in the first line.
   > Screen: LateStalls.csv in Notepad.
3. **The Import Text Wizard (1:00-3:30).** External Data > New Data Source >
   From File > Text File; Browse; new table; Delimited; Comma; First Row
   Contains Field Names; data types (Phone stays Short Text); the key; the name
   tblLateStalls; Finish.
4. **Sign-off.**
