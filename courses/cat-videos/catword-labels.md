# catword lesson 18: Labels, e-mails and rules - videos

Lesson: `content/catword/labels.php` (Grade 11). Screen recordings in the
CAT VM (Word 365), starting from a new document and Grade 11 parents.xlsx
(G:\My Drive\CAT\Word). Never show the title bar's account; paint out the
Add-ins group. Board scenes in the CAT marker style with Clicky; yellow
highlighter on what is talked about. A Word table as the list is CAPS Grade
11 (IEB Grade 12); e-mail merges and rules are IEB - say each once.

## catword-18.1 Labels and fixing a merge (about 8 min)

**Goes:** after the CAPS section "A Word table as the list", before section
`#errors` (the comment `// VIDEO catword-18.1`).
**The pupil can afterwards:** merge a sheet of address labels, use a Word
table as the list, and find and fix the usual merge problems.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · WORD`, title "21 labels at *once*"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:30).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. The letters are
   printed; now 64 envelopes need addresses.
   > Board: a sheet of 21 labels, Clicky peeling one off.
2. **Label Options (0:30-2:30).** New blank document; Mailings > Start Mail
   Merge > Labels...: Avery A4/A5, L7160 (3 across, 7 down, 63.5 x 38.1 mm).
   Table Layout > View Gridlines.
   > Screen: the clicks above.
3. **First label, Update Labels (2:30-4:30).** Select Recipients; fields in
   the first label only; Update Labels («Next Record»); Preview; Finish &
   Merge. Print a test on plain paper. Envelopes, and Mailings > Envelopes
   for one.
4. **A Word table as the list - CAPS (4:30-5:30).** The table first in the
   document, headings in row 1, a row per person; Use an Existing List >
   the .docx.
5. **Check for Errors and fixes (5:30-7:30).** The three choices; the
   leading 0 of 0182 (Text column in Excel), the same person on every label
   (Update Labels), field codes (Alt+F9), missing spaces, Match Fields.
   > Board: a "symptom - cause - fix" chart, Clicky in a doctor's coat.
6. **Sign-off.**

## catword-18.2 E-mail merges and rules (about 6 min)

**Goes:** after the written question `w18Labels`, before the scenario (the
comment `// VIDEO catword-18.2`).
**The pupil can afterwards:** (IEB) send an e-mail merge and add a rule such
as If...Then...Else to a merge.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · WORD`, title "E-mail merges - and *rules*"

### Scenes

1. **E-mail merge (0:00-2:30).** Start Mail Merge > E-mail Messages; the
   list's Email column; Finish & Merge > Send Email Messages: To Email,
   Subject line, Mail format HTML; Outlook. Test with your own address first.
   Spam, as the quote warns.
   > Screen: the Merge to E-mail box.
2. **Rules (2:30-5:00).** Rules > If...Then...Else...: Paid Equal to No -
   "Please pay the camp fee by Friday."; otherwise "Thank you for paying".
   Skip Record If, Fill-in, Ask, Merge Record #, Next Record.
   > Board: a fork in the road labelled Paid = No / Paid = Yes, Clicky at
   > the signpost.
3. **Sign-off.**
