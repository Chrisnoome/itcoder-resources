## catexcel-09.1 Sorting a list (about 7 min)

**Goes:** after the prose block "Sorting A to Z" (section `#sort`).
**The pupil can afterwards:** keep a list sortable (one heading row, no
blank rows or columns); sort it with Sort A to Z and Sort Z to A after
clicking ONE cell; say why selecting one column is the mistake and what the
Sort Warning offers; sort with the Sort dialog box (My data has headers,
Sort by, Order) and on two levels.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · SPREADSHEETS`, title "Who *won* the fun run?"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:40).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. Ms Naidoo has the
   fun run times of 14 runners in the order they signed up. Who won? Who
   came last? Sort the list and the answer is in row 4.
   > Board: Clicky holding a stopwatch beside a jumbled queue of runners.
2. **A list Excel can sort (0:40-1:40).** One heading row, one runner per
   row, no empty rows or columns inside the list; a blank row between the
   title and the list.
   > Screen: the Results sheet; highlighter on row 3 (the header row) and the blank row 2.
3. **Sort A to Z (1:40-3:00).** Click one cell in the Surname column (A7);
   Data tab > Sort & Filter > Sort A to Z. Every row moves with its
   surname. Sort Z to A turns it round. Text A to Z, numbers smallest to
   largest, dates oldest to newest: ascending; the other way: descending.
   > Screen: the click on A7, the Data tab, Sort A to Z; highlighter on "Adams" in A4 and on "Chloe" next to it.
4. **The mistake (3:00-4:00).** Select column A only, then Sort A to Z: the
   Sort Warning. Expand the selection is safe; Continue with the current
   selection would sort the surnames and leave every other column where it
   was - Adams would get Sipho's time. Ctrl+Z undoes it.
   > Board: names sliding down a list while the times stay put, Clicky gasping.
5. **The Sort dialog box (4:00-5:40).** Click D6; Data > Sort. My data has
   headers ticked; Sort by Time (min), Cell Values, Smallest to Largest; OK.
   Mokoena, 18.4 minutes, at the top.
   > Screen: the dialog; highlighter on "My data has headers".
6. **Two levels (5:40-6:40).** Data > Sort; Sort by Class; Add Level; Then
   by Surname; OK - 10A first, A to Z inside each class.
7. **Sign-off.**

## catexcel-09.2 Filters and finishing touches (about 8 min)

**Goes:** after the prose block "Pictures, shapes and icons" (section
`#pictures`).
**The pupil can afterwards:** insert a picture, a shape with text and an
icon, and move and size them; (IEB) filter a list with the arrows and clear
the filter; change the theme; use the thesaurus and Translate on a cell;
add, show and delete a comment.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · SPREADSHEETS`, title "Only *10B*, please"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:30).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. Mr Mahlangu,
   10B's class teacher, wants only his class's runners - without anyone
   deleting the others.
2. **Filter (0:30-2:30)** (IEB). Click C6; Data > Filter (Ctrl+Shift+L):
   arrows on the headings. The Class arrow; untick (Select All); tick 10B;
   OK. Blue row numbers, the funnel on the arrow, "5 of 14 records found".
   Clear; Filter again to take the arrows away. Number Filters > Greater
   Than for times over 30 minutes.
   > Screen: the drop-down; highlighter on the blue row numbers.
   > Board: Clicky with a sieve; rows dropping through, the 10B ones staying.
3. **Pictures, shapes and icons (2:30-4:30).** Insert tab > Illustrations:
   Pictures > This Device; Shapes > a speech bubble, drag to draw it, type
   in it; Icons. They float above the cells: drag to move, corner handles to
   size; Shape Format tab. Keep them clear of the data.
   > Screen: the Shapes gallery; the bubble "Fastest runner" beside the list.
4. **Themes (4:30-5:40)** (IEB). Page Layout > Themes: the whole workbook's
   colours and fonts change at once - the heading row, the title, the
   shape. Colors and Fonts for one part only.
   > Screen: the Themes gallery; before and after side by side.
5. **The thesaurus and Translate (5:40-6:40)** (IEB). Click E3 (Money);
   Review > Thesaurus (Shift+F7); Translate opens the Translator pane.
6. **Comments (6:40-7:40)** (IEB). Click D12; Review > New Comment; type;
   Post (Ctrl+Enter). The purple corner; Show Comments; Delete.
   > Screen: the comment card; highlighter on the purple mark in D12.
7. **Sign-off.**
