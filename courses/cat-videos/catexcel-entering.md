## catexcel-02.1 Typing and fixing data (about 6 min)

**Goes:** after the study block (the comment sits there with 02.2).
**The pupil can afterwards:** say how Excel shows text, numbers, dates and
times; avoid the three traps (an R, a phone number's 0, a date the wrong way
round); tell contents from value; type over a cell and edit one (double-click,
F2, the Formula Bar); use Esc, Delete, Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Y, F7, Ctrl+F and Ctrl+H.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · SPREADSHEETS`, title "Why is my *R18* on the left?"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:40).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. Ms Naidoo's class
   list has a name spelt wrong, a pupil missing and no numbers. Today it gets
   fixed - but first, what Excel makes of what you type.
   > Board: a messy class list on a clipboard, Clicky with a red pen.
2. **Left or right (0:40-2:00).** Type Sipho, 64, 2026/03/12, 12:30, R18,
   0821234567 and '0821234567 down a column. Text left; numbers, dates and
   times right. R18 is text; the 0 goes; the apostrophe keeps it.
   > Screen: each typed in turn; highlighter on left/right; a SUM under the R amounts giving 0.
   > Board: a date 03/12/2026 with two speech bubbles: "3 December!" / "12 March!" - then 2026/03/12, no argument.
3. **Contents and value (2:00-2:40).** Click a cell: the Formula Bar shows
   what was typed. For a formula they differ (lesson 3).
   > Screen: highlighter on the Formula Bar.
4. **Fixing a cell (2:40-4:30).** Click C4, type Nomvula, Enter. Then the
   other way: double-click, F2, the Formula Bar. Esc while typing. Delete
   empties. Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Y. F7 spelling; Ctrl+F and Ctrl+H.
   > Screen: each in turn, with a key overlay.
5. **Sign-off.**

## catexcel-02.2 Rows, AutoFill and sheets (about 7 min)

**Goes:** after the study block, with 02.1.
**The pupil can afterwards:** insert, delete and hide a row or column (and
know the Delete key only empties); AutoFill a list and a number series with
the fill handle; copy, cut and paste; add, rename, colour, move, hide and
delete a sheet.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · SPREADSHEETS`, title "Let *Excel* carry on the list"

### Scenes

1. **A new pupil (0:00-1:40).** Right-click row 5's heading > Insert: a new
   row above, everyone below moves down. Type Mahlangu and Zanele. Delete on
   the menu removes a row; the Delete key only empties it. Hide a column; the
   letters skip; Unhide.
   > Screen: the right-click menu with highlighter on Insert; Home > Cells > Insert the same way; hide and unhide column D.
2. **AutoFill (1:40-3:40).** The fill handle and the thin black +. Type 1 and
   2, select both, double-click the handle: 1 to 7. Drag Term 1 across:
   Term 2, 3, 4. Mon, January, a date. One number copies; two carry on. The
   AutoFill Options button.
   > Screen: zoom on the fill handle; each fill.
   > Board: Clicky dragging a fill handle like a paint roller, numbers rolling out.
3. **Copy, cut, paste (3:40-5:00).** Select A1:E1, Ctrl+C, the dashed line.
   Sheet2, A1, Ctrl+V. Paste types over what is there. Esc clears the dashed
   line. Ctrl+X moves.
   > Screen: key overlays; highlighter on the status bar's "Select destination".
4. **Sheets (5:00-6:30).** + adds a sheet; double-click a tab to rename it
   10B; right-click > Tab Color, Hide, Unhide, Delete (no undo!); drag a tab
   to move it.
   > Screen: each in turn.
5. **Sign-off.**
