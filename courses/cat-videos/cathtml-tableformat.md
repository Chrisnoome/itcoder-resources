## cathtml-12.1 Formatting a table: border, padding, spacing and alignment (about 8 min)

**Goes:** the comment `// VIDEO cathtml-12.1`, after the IEB caption section.
**The pupil can afterwards:** set a table's border and width, its cell padding and spacing, line up words across and up and down a cell, and colour rows and cells.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · HTML`, title "Padding or *spacing*?"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:40).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. Thabo's platters table works - but it is cramped and crooked.
   > Board: a squashed table, Clicky pushing its walls apart.
2. **IEB: tables from the start (0:40-1:40).** Table, tr, td, th in one minute, for IEB pupils meeting tables now.
   > Screen: a two-row table built quickly.
3. **Border and width (1:40-2:40).** Border in pixels; width in pixels or a percentage.
   > Screen: border 0, 1, 4; width 50% and the window narrowed.
4. **Padding and spacing (2:40-4:30).** Cellpadding inside each cell, cellspacing between cells; 0 spacing makes single lines.
   > Board: the egg box - cups and gaps.
   > Screen: change each number and refresh.
5. **align and valign (4:30-6:30).** Align across: left, center, right; valign up and down: top, middle, bottom; on the tr or one td; prices on the right.
   > Screen: valign top on the platter rows; prices right-aligned.
6. **Colour and caption (6:30-7:40).** Bgcolor on table, row or cell - the closest wins. IEB: caption.
   > Screen: wheat on the heading row.
7. **Sign-off.**
