## catword-04.1 Marks, styles, alignment and spacing (about 8 min)

**Goes:** after the quiz `q4Gap` in section `#spacing` (the comment
`// VIDEO catword-04.1`).
**The pupil can afterwards:** show the formatting marks and find typing
mistakes with them, apply a quick style, align paragraphs, and set line and
paragraph spacing.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · WORD`, title "No more *grey block*"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:40).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. Ms Naidoo's lab
   rules: one grey block on the wall. The rule for this lesson: paragraph
   formatting works on the paragraph the insertion point is in.
2. **Show/Hide (0:40-2:00).** ¶, dots, arrows, Shift+Enter. Find the two
   spaces and the empty paragraph; fix them.
   > Screen: Show/Hide on; highlighter on the two dots and the lonely ¶.
3. **Quick styles (2:00-3:00).** Title, Heading 1 and Normal from the gallery.
4. **Alignment (3:00-4:30).** Left, centre, right, justify; Ctrl+L/E/R/J.
5. **Spacing (4:30-7:00).** The Line and Paragraph Spacing menu; Ctrl+1,
   Ctrl+5, Ctrl+2. Paragraph spacing Before and After on the Layout tab.
   Why not empty Enters: add a line above and watch the gap move.
   > Board: a stack of empty ¶ boxes sliding down a page.
6. **Sign-off.**
