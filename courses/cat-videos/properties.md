# CAT 10 lesson 20: File properties - videos

Lesson file: `AIPascalCourse/content/cattheory10/properties.php`. Two videos,
mostly screen recordings in File Explorer. Everything said or shown here is
also in the lesson text (anchors in the tables below).

**Screen recordings:** the VM, Windows 11, Microsoft 365. Before recording,
make `Documents\CAT\Notice.docx` (about 24 KB), `Documents\CAT\Market Day
poster.pptx`, a JPG photo, and a flash drive (VHD mounted as E:). Note the real sizes and dates the VM shows and say those -
the lesson's numbers are an example, not a script. A JPG photo with no GPS
data in it (never one of a real home).

## cat10-20.1 Reading a file's properties (about 7 min)

**Goes:** after the written question `w20Copied`, at the end of section
`#dates` (the comment `// VIDEO cat10-20.1`), before `#attributes`.
**The pupil can afterwards:** open a file's Properties window; read the
type of file, opens with, location, size, size on disk and the three dates;
convert KB to MB; say why a copy has a new Created date.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · FILES`, title "What your file *knows* about itself"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:40).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. "I finished my PAT
   on Friday, Ma'am." Ms Naidoo opens Properties: Modified, Monday, 06:40.
   One spelling mistake on the bus.
   > Board: Thabo, Ms Naidoo and a laptop; a sticky note "Modified: Mon
   > 06:40" with yellow highlighter.
2. **Opening Properties (0:40-1:30).** Right-click > Properties, or select
   and Alt + Enter. The tabs: General, Details, Security, Previous Versions.
   > Screen: VM, Documents\CAT. Right-click Notice.docx > Properties. Close.
   > Click it, press Alt + Enter (key-press overlay). Hover each tab name.
3. **The General tab, line by line (1:30-3:30).** Type of file; Opens with
   and Change; Location; Size and Size on disk; Created, Modified, Accessed;
   the attribute boxes. Location + name = the file specification.
   > Screen: VM, the General tab. A yellow highlighter band moves down each
   > line as it is named; zoom on Location, then a caption builds
   > C:\Users\Thabo\Documents\CAT\Notice.docx.
4. **Size (3:30-4:40).** Bytes; KB, MB, GB, TB, each 1 000 times the one
   before. Bigger unit: divide; smaller: multiply. 2 500 KB is 2.5 MB.
   Windows counts in 1 024s - nothing missing. Size on disk: whole 4 KB
   blocks; one sock, one box.
   > Board: the unit ladder drawn rung by rung; the 2 500 / 1 000 sum on a
   > sticky note; a sock alone in a moving box.
5. **The dates (4:40-5:50).** Created - made here; Modified - contents last
   changed and saved; Accessed - last opened, least reliable. Copy to the
   flash drive: Created is now, Modified stays.
   > Screen: VM. Copy Notice.docx to E: (Ctrl + C, Ctrl + V). On E:, Alt +
   > Enter: highlighter on Created (today, now) and Modified (the old date).
   > Board: the four-row "what changes each date" table filled in.
6. **Sign-off.**

## cat10-20.2 Read-only, hidden and extra columns (about 5 min)

**Goes:** after the quiz `q20Column`, at the end of section `#columns` (the
comment `// VIDEO cat10-20.2`), before the Good to Know.
**The pupil can afterwards:** say what read-only and hidden do and do not
do, set either one, show hidden items, and add a column in Details view.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · FILES`, title "Look, but don't *touch*"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:20).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. Two tick boxes at
   the bottom of every Properties window.
   > Board: the two boxes drawn large; Clicky about to tick one.
2. **Read-only (0:20-1:40).** Open and read, but not save over it. Word says
   Read-Only and asks for a new name. Good for a template or your final PAT.
   It does not stop deleting or renaming.
   > Screen: VM. Properties of Notice.docx > tick Read-only > OK. Open it:
   > zoom on "Read-Only" in Word's title. Type a word, Ctrl + S: Word asks for
   > a new name; Cancel; close without saving. Untick Read-only again.
3. **Hidden (1:40-2:50).** Properties > tick Hidden > OK: the file vanishes
   from the list. Still there, still takes space. View > Show > Hidden items:
   it comes back, faded. Not a way to keep a secret.
   > Screen: VM. Properties of Market Day poster.pptx > Hidden > OK. It
   > disappears. View > Show > Hidden items: it reappears with a faded icon
   > (zoom). Untick Hidden in its Properties; untick Hidden items.
4. **Columns and the Details pane (2:50-3:50).** Details view; right-click a
   heading; tick Date created; More for Length, Dimensions, Slides. Sort by
   Size, biggest first. Alt + Shift + P for the Details pane.
   > Screen: VM, Documents\CAT in Details view. Right-click the Name heading
   > > Date created. Right-click again > More... > tick Dimensions > OK.
   > Click the Size heading until the biggest is on top. Press Alt + Shift +
   > P; click a photo, then Notice.docx - the pane changes.
5. **Metadata, in one breath (3:50-4:30).** The Details tab: authors, photo
   size, sometimes where a photo was taken. Remove Properties and Personal
   Information.
   > Screen: VM. Properties of the photo > Details tab; highlighter on
   > Dimensions; scroll to the link at the bottom.
6. **Sign-off.**
