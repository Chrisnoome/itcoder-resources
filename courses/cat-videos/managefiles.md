# CAT 10 lesson 19: Managing files - videos

Lesson file: `AIPascalCourse/content/cattheory10/managefiles.php`. Three
videos, all mostly screen recordings in File Explorer - this lesson is the
practical skill itself. Everything said or shown here is also in the lesson
text (anchors in the tables below).

**Screen recordings:** the VM, Windows 11, File Explorer opening on This PC,
Details view off at the start of 19.2. Before recording, make Thabo's test
folder `Documents\Test` with 15 small files, mixed: four Word documents
(`Fun Day.docx`, `Notice.docx`, `Minutes.docx`, `Thank you letter.docx` - not
next to each other when sorted by name), three Excel workbooks, `Poster.pptx`,
`Old.txt`; a `Pictures` folder of
about 40 JPG photos and one big Excel file. A flash drive (VHD mounted as E:,
label USB Drive). Keys pressed appear as a key-press overlay throughout.

## cat10-19.1 Make, rename, copy and move (about 8 min)

**Goes:** at the end of section `#copymove` (the comment
`// VIDEO cat10-19.1`), before `g19CopyOrMove`.
**The pupil can afterwards:** select one, some or all files; make a folder;
rename with F2; copy and move with the keys and by dragging, and say which
one leaves two files.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · FILES`, title "Copy or *move*?"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:40).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. Thabo's first
   practical test: seven jobs, a folder of 15 files, 45 minutes. Each job is
   a few clicks - if you know which.
   > Board: the seven-job list on a sticky note; Clicky with a stopwatch.
2. **Selecting (0:40-1:50).** Click one; Ctrl + click several apart; Shift +
   click a run; Ctrl + A everything; click empty space to clear.
   > Screen: VM, Documents\Test, Details view. Click Notice.docx. Ctrl +
   > click Fun Day.docx and Minutes.docx. Click empty space. Click the 3rd
   > file, Shift + click the 8th. Ctrl + A. Click empty space.
3. **A new folder (1:50-2:40).** New > Folder (Ctrl + Shift + N); type Docs;
   Enter. The slip: clicking away leaves "New folder".
   > Screen: VM. Click New > Folder. Type Docs, Enter. Then press Ctrl +
   > Shift + N, click away on purpose - "New folder" stays - then F2, type
   > Spare, Enter.
4. **Renaming (2:40-3:40).** Select, F2, only the name part is selected, type,
   Enter. The extension warning. Many at once.
   > Screen: VM. Click Poster.pptx, F2 - highlighter on the selected part
   > "Poster" and NOT ".pptx". Type Fun Day Poster, Enter. Ctrl + Z to show
   > undo, then F2 and rename it again.
5. **Copy and move (3:40-5:20).** Copy: Ctrl + C, go there, Ctrl + V - two.
   Move: Ctrl + X (icon goes pale), go there, Ctrl + V - one. Paste into the
   same folder: "- Copy".
   > Board: the `copy-vs-move` drawing redrawn; "2" and "1" on pink
   > highlighter.
   > Screen: VM. Select Notice.docx, Ctrl + C, open E:, Ctrl + V. Back to
   > Test: Notice.docx still there. Select the four .docx files with Ctrl +
   > click, Ctrl + X (pale icons, zoom in), open Docs, Ctrl + V; back to Test:
   > gone.
6. **Dragging (5:20-6:50).** Same drive moves, other drive copies; the label
   says which; Ctrl copies, Shift moves; right-drag asks.
   > Screen: VM, two File Explorer windows side by side (Test and E:). Drag
   > Minutes.docx onto the Docs folder - zoom on the label "Move to Docs".
   > Drag Old.txt onto E: - label "Copy to USB Drive (E:)". Right-drag
   > Notice.docx onto Docs - the menu with Copy here / Move here / Create
   > shortcuts here; click Copy here.
7. **Same name (6:50-7:30).** Replace, Skip, or Compare. Replace cannot be
   undone.
   > Screen: VM. Copy Notice.docx to E: again: the Replace or Skip Files box;
   > highlighter on its three choices; click Skip this file.
8. **Sign-off.** (Tip before it: wait for a copy to finish; right-click the
   flash drive, Eject.)

## cat10-19.2 Delete, restore, sort and search (about 7 min)

**Goes:** after the IEB board section (the comment `// VIDEO cat10-19.2`),
before `#compress`.
**The pupil can afterwards:** delete and restore a file, say what does not
go to the Recycle Bin, switch to Details view, sort ascending and descending,
and search a folder (IEB: with * and ?).
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · FILES`, title "Deleted - or *gone*?"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:30).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. Delete is not
   always the end. And sometimes it is.
   > Board: Clicky dropping a file into a bin; the bin lid has a "back" arrow.
2. **Delete and restore (0:30-2:00).** Select, Delete - to the Recycle Bin.
   Open the bin, select, Restore the selected items - back to its folder.
   Ctrl + Z straight after. Empty the bin: gone for good.
   > Screen: VM. Select Old.txt in Documents\Test, press Delete. Minimise to
   > the desktop, double-click Recycle Bin, click Old.txt, click Restore the
   > selected items. Back in Test: Old.txt is back. (Do not empty the bin on
   > camera - point at Empty Recycle Bin with a highlighter band.)
3. **No bin (2:00-2:50).** Shift + Delete; flash drive; memory card; network
   drive; too big. Cloud drives have their own bin online.
   > Screen: VM. Select Old.txt on E:, press Delete: the "permanently delete"
   > question; click No. Highlighter on "permanently".
   > Board: the four no-bin cases as four crossed-out bins.
4. **Details view (2:50-3:40).** View > Details (Ctrl + Shift + 6): Name,
   Date modified, Type, Size.
   > Screen: VM, Test in Large icons. View > Details. Highlighter across the
   > four column headings.
5. **Sorting (3:40-5:00).** Ascending: A-Z, smallest, oldest. Descending:
   Z-A, biggest, newest. Sort > Date modified, Sort > Descending. Or click a
   heading; again to reverse. Group by Type.
   > Screen: VM. Sort > Date modified; Sort > Descending - newest at the top.
   > Click the Size heading twice - biggest at the top. Sort > Group by >
   > Type.
6. **Searching (5:00-6:00).** Open the folder (searches inside it and its
   sub-folders), Ctrl + E, type, Enter. Open file location.
   > Screen: VM. In Documents, Ctrl + E, type notice, Enter. Right-click the
   > result > Open file location.
7. **Wildcards - IEB (6:00-6:50).** * any number of characters; ? exactly
   one. *.docx finds every Word document.
   > Screen: VM. In Test, search *.docx; Ctrl + A on the results.
   > Board: Report?.docx against Report1, ReportA, Report12 - ticks and a
   > cross.
8. **Sign-off.**

## cat10-19.3 Zip, extract and shortcuts (about 6 min)

**Goes:** at the end of section `#shortcuts` (the comment
`// VIDEO cat10-19.3`), before `#keys`.
**The pupil can afterwards:** zip files or a folder, extract a zip file, say
what shrinks and what doesn't, and make a desktop shortcut three ways - and
tell a shortcut from a keyboard shortcut.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · FILES`, title "Squeeze it, *point* to it"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:30).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. Forty Market Day
   photos, one e-mail. Forty attachments? No.
   > Board: an e-mail with 40 paper clips; Clicky buried under them.
2. **What zipping is (0:30-1:30).** Many files into one smaller .zip file.
   Why: one attachment, smaller (e-mail limits about 20-25 MB), folders stay
   together.
   > Board: the `zip-lossless` drawing redrawn - 40 files into one, and back
   > out again unchanged.
3. **Zip it (1:30-2:30).** Select, right-click, Compress to > ZIP File, name,
   Enter. The originals are still there.
   > Screen: VM. Right-click the Pictures folder > Compress to > ZIP File
   > (on 22H2/23H2: Compress to ZIP file). Type Market Day photos, Enter.
   > Show both the folder and the zip, in Details view with Size.
4. **What shrinks (2:30-3:10).** The JPG photos hardly shrank; a big Excel
   file shrinks a lot.
   > Screen: VM. Zip the big Excel file; compare the two sizes in the Size
   > column; highlighter on both numbers.
5. **Extract it (3:10-4:00).** Right-click > Extract All, Browse if needed,
   Extract. Work on files only after extracting.
   > Screen: VM. Right-click Market Day photos.zip > Extract All > Extract.
   > The new folder opens.
6. **Shortcuts (4:00-5:30).** A pointer with a little arrow; deleting it
   leaves the file. Send to > Desktop; New > Shortcut; Alt + drag. Pin to
   taskbar for programs. Not a keyboard shortcut.
   > Screen: VM. Right-click Fun Day.docx > Show more options > Send to >
   > Desktop (create shortcut). Show the desktop: zoom on the arrow. Double-
   > click it - the document opens; close. Delete the shortcut - the file is
   > still in Docs.
   > Board: a signpost to a shop; the sign taken down, the shop still there.
7. **Sign-off.**
