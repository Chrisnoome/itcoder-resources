# Presentations lesson 1: Slides, layouts and designs - videos

Lesson: `content/catpowerpoint/start.php`. Screen recordings in the **CAT VM
`itcoder-cat`** (PowerPoint 365, an 1800-wide window so the ribbon does not
fold), with board scenes in the CAT marker style with Clicky
(brand/cat-art-style.md). The course's first video, catprac-00.1 (How to work
with software simulations, [simulations.md](simulations.md)), comes before
the practice simulation and is not repeated here.

## catpowerpoint-01.1 The PowerPoint window, views and new slides (about 7 min)

**Goes:** after section `#window` (before `#views`).
**The pupil can afterwards:** name the parts of the window, switch between Normal and Slide Sorter, add a slide with Ctrl+M, choose and change a layout.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · POWERPOINT`, title "Your *first* slides"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:40).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. Thabo has three
   minutes to talk to his class about saving data - and thirty faces staring
   at him. A few slides behind him will keep him on track.
   > Board: Thabo at the front of a class, Clicky pointing at a blank screen behind him.
2. **What a presentation is for (0:40-1:40).** Slides help a speaker; a few
   points and a picture, never a page to read. Uses: a talk, a lesson, Mr
   Botha's TV in the bakery, the PAT summary.
   > Board: four little scenes - class, lesson, bakery TV, meeting; yellow highlighter on "helps a speaker".
3. **The window (1:40-3:20).** Open PowerPoint, Blank Presentation. Point to
   the ribbon (tabs, groups), the Slides pane (thumbnails), the slide, the
   Notes pane, the status bar ("Slide 1 of 4", view buttons, zoom). Save As,
   then Ctrl+S; a .pptx. The Search box and the Help tab.
   > Screen: hover each part in turn; highlighter band on each name as it is said.
4. **Views (3:20-4:30).** View tab > Slide Sorter: every slide as a
   thumbnail; spot the copied slide. Back with Normal. The same buttons on
   the status bar. Notes Page, Reading View and Slide Show named.
   > Screen: View > Slide Sorter, then the status bar's Normal button.
5. **New slides (4:30-5:30).** Click slide 2, Ctrl+M: the new slide comes
   after the one you clicked. The arrow under New Slide to choose a layout.
   > Screen: click slide 2, Ctrl+M; show the new slide 3.
6. **Layouts (5:30-6:40).** Placeholders: "Click to add title". Home >
   Layout > Two Content. The seven layouts named, each with its job.
   > Screen: Home > Layout > Two Content; then open the gallery again and point at each layout.
7. **Sign-off.**

## catpowerpoint-01.2 Designs and the rules of a good slide (about 7 min)

**Goes:** after the Templates board section (before `#rules`).
**The pupil can afterwards:** delete, duplicate and move slides, apply a theme and a variant, set the slide size, and judge a slide against the rules.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · POWERPOINT`, title "Slides people *want* to read"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:30).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. Thabo's slides work
   - but they are plain white, and one is a copy made by mistake.
   > Board: two identical slides, Clicky scratching its head.
2. **Deleting and moving (0:30-1:50).** Right-click slide 5 > Delete Slide;
   the Delete key; Ctrl+Z. Duplicate Slide (Ctrl+D). Drag a thumbnail to
   move it; drag in Slide Sorter.
   > Screen: right-click > Delete Slide; Ctrl+D; drag slide 4 to the top and back.
3. **A theme (1:50-3:00).** Design tab > Themes > Archway: every slide at
   once. Variants for other colours. Keep one design.
   > Screen: Design > Archway, then point at Variants.
4. **Designer (3:00-3:40).** Home > Designer (Design Suggestions): ideas for
   this slide; needs the Internet; check it still reads well.
   > Screen: open Designer, click one idea, undo it.
5. **Slide size (3:40-4:40).** Design > Slide Size: Widescreen 16:9, Standard
   4:3, Custom (portrait for an A4 poster). Set it first.
   > Screen: Design > Slide Size, open the menu; Custom Slide Size box, Portrait.
6. **Templates (4:40-5:20).** File > New, search "school project", Create. A
   .potx. (The IEB names templates; worth knowing for everyone.)
   > Screen: File > New, search, Create.
7. **The rules of a good slide (5:20-7:00).** One idea per slide; key words;
   24 points or more; contrast; one design; pictures and charts; spelling;
   never read it out. Before and after: Thabo's twelve-sentence slide, and
   three points in 28-point text with the sentences moved to the notes.
   > Board: the wall-of-text slide, a yawning audience; then the short slide; highlighter on each rule as it is said.
8. **Sign-off.**
