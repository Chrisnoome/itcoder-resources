# CAT 12 lesson 7: Metadata, converting files and searching - videos

Lesson file: `AIPascalCourse/content/cattheory12/metadata.php`. Two videos,
mostly screen recordings in File Explorer and Word. Everything said or shown
here is also in the lesson text (anchors in the tables below).

**Screen recordings:** the VM, Windows 11, Microsoft 365. Before recording,
make `Documents\CAT\Bursary application.docx` with Authors "LibraryUser",
a phone photo **taken in the VM's own test location with made-up GPS data**
(never a real home - set the latitude and longitude with a tag editor to a
public place such as the Union Buildings), an MP3 with artist and album
filled in, and a folder `Pictures\Yearbook` with about 30 photos named like
`IMG_4471.jpg`, some with Date taken 12 September 2026, a few tagged
`sportsday`, and two videos over 500 MB. Say the numbers the VM shows - the
lesson's are an example, not a script.

## cat12-07.1 What your photo knows about you (about 8 min)

**Goes:** after the written question `wMetaGpsSchool`, at the end of section
`#gps` (the comment `// VIDEO cat12-07.1`), before `#converting`.
**The pupil can afterwards:** say what metadata is; read a document's and a
photo's metadata on the Details tab; change the Title and Authors; say why
GPS metadata is a risk and remove it.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · FILES`, title "What your *photo* knows about you"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:40).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. Lerato e-mails
   three photos of her old laptop to a buyer. "I see you're in Hatfield - I
   can collect tonight." She never told him. The photos did.
   > Board: Lerato's phone with the buyer's message; Clicky pointing at a
   > photo with a map pin floating out of it.
2. **Data about data (0:40-1:40).** The letter and the envelope. Metadata
   is data about data: the essay's text is the data; its author, word count
   and last-saved date are metadata.
   > Board: an envelope drawn with "From", "To", "Date"; yellow highlighter
   > on "data about data".
3. **Where to see it (1:40-3:00).** Right-click > Properties > Details;
   Alt + Enter; the Details pane (Alt + Shift + P); extra columns (Authors,
   Tags, Date taken).
   > Screen: VM. Properties of Bursary application.docx > Details; scroll
   > through Description, Origin, Content. Close; Alt + Shift + P; click
   > the file; right-click a column heading and tick Authors.
4. **Changing Title and Authors (3:00-4:20).** Details tab: type in Title
   and Authors, OK (not the X). Or File > Info in Word. Why: credit,
   finding it, looking professional, privacy. The exam may ask for your
   examination number in Authors.
   > Screen: VM. Change Authors from LibraryUser to Lerato Mokoena and Title
   > to "Bursary application 2027" on the Details tab; click OK; reopen to
   > show it stayed. Then the same in Word, File > Info.
5. **A photo's metadata (4:20-5:40).** EXIF. Image: dimensions (4000 x
   3000 = 12 MP); Origin: Date taken (not Date created); Camera; GPS:
   latitude and longitude. An MP3: contributing artists, album, year,
   length.
   > Screen: VM, the photo's Details tab; yellow highlighter on Dimensions,
   > Date taken, Camera model, Latitude, Longitude. Then the MP3's Media
   > section.
6. **Why GPS is a risk (5:40-6:50).** Home, routine, valuables, children.
   Which ways of sending keep it (e-mail, flash drive, WhatsApp as a
   document, cloud link) and which usually strip it (most social media,
   WhatsApp photos). POPI: a location tied to a person is personal
   information.
   > Board: Thabo's TV photo and "Off to Durban for two weeks!" - Clicky
   > drawing a line from the photo to a house; a burglar reading it.
7. **Removing it (6:50-7:50).** Switch off location tags in the camera
   app; Details tab > Remove Properties and Personal Information > Create
   a copy with all possible properties removed > OK; Word: File > Info >
   Check for Issues > Inspect Document > Remove All.
   > Screen: VM, the photo: Remove Properties and Personal Information >
   > the copy; open the copy's Details: no GPS section. Word: Inspect
   > Document.
8. **Sign-off.**

## cat12-07.2 Find any file in seconds (about 7 min)

**Goes:** after the written question `wMetaSearchVsName`, at the end of
section `#search` (the comment `// VIDEO cat12-07.2`), before the IEB box
`#wildcards`.
**The pupil can afterwards:** say why and how a file is converted and what
can be lost; search by kind, date, size, tags and author; combine filters;
tag files.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · FILES`, title "Find *any* file in seconds"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:30).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. 6 000 photos
   called IMG_something. Which ones are from the wedding on 14 September?
   > Board: a mountain of photo icons; Clicky with binoculars.
2. **Converting, and what is lost (0:30-2:30).** Why convert (others must
   open it, same look, a website wants .pdf or .jpg, another program needs
   the data, smaller). Four ways: Save As, Export, Print to PDF, open in
   another program. What is lost: .txt formatting, .csv other sheets and
   formulas, PDF easy editing, JPG detail. Keep the original; check the
   copy; beware online converters. Export out, import in.
   > Screen: VM. Word: File > Save As > Save as type list (highlighter on
   > PDF and Plain Text). Ctrl + P > Microsoft Print to PDF.
   > Board: the "what can be lost" table filled in row by row.
3. **Searching by properties (2:30-4:30).** kind:picture,
   datemodified:last week, datetaken:this month, size:>100MB, tags:,
   author:. Search options on the command bar.
   > Screen: VM, Pictures\Yearbook. Type each filter in the search box;
   > highlighter on the filter as it is typed; open Search options to show
   > the same choices as lists.
4. **Combining filters (4:30-5:40).** A space means all must match. Thabo's
   full laptop: This PC, kind:video size:>500MB datemodified:this year,
   sort by size.
   > Screen: VM, This PC; type the combined search; sort by Size.
5. **Tagging (5:40-6:40).** Select photos, Details pane or Properties >
   Details, Tags: sportsday (semicolons between tags), Save / OK. Then
   tags:sportsday finds them wherever they are. Not every type takes tags.
   > Screen: VM, select four photos with Ctrl + click, tag them in the
   > Details pane, Save; search tags:sportsday.
6. **Sign-off.**
