## cat12-29.1 The CAPS PAT in three phases (about 7 min)

**Goes:** after section `#phase3` - the comment after `g29Phase`.
**The pupil can afterwards:** say what the PAT is worth, and what each of the three phases needs and when it is due.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · THE PAT`, title "One project, *three* hand-ins"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:40).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. "The PAT is just a project," says Lerato. It is worth as much as Paper 1.
   > Board: four equal blocks - SBA, PAT, Paper 1, Paper 2 - each 25%; highlighter on the PAT block.
2. **What the PAT is (0:40-1:40).** An information management project: find and access, process, present. Documents in the applications; a report with an introduction, body, conclusion and recommendations. Your PAT document from your teacher is the final word.
   > Board: three arrows - find, process, present - under Thabo's water drop.
3. **Three phases (1:40-2:30).** Phase 1 by the end of Term 1, Phase 2 by the end of Term 2, Phase 3 before the prelim. Each builds on the one before.
   > Board: the staircase drawing, a phase on each step, a date beside each.
4. **Phase 1 (2:30-3:50).** The task definition; the main question and sub-questions; a questionnaire and at least two other sources; searching and judging; folders from day one.
   > Board: Thabo's main question written out; five numbered items appear.
5. **Phase 2 (3:50-5:10).** Gather with an online form; import into the spreadsheet and database; functions, charts, queries, reports; trends and patterns; sift and combine.
   > Board: a form's answers flowing into a spreadsheet grid, then a chart; highlighter on "import".
6. **Phase 3 (5:10-6:30).** The report: title page, automatic contents, introduction, body with charts and tables, conclusions, recommendations, references. Quality control: the thread from task to conclusion.
   > Board: the report as a stack of pages, each labelled; a red thread drawn from the task on page 1 to the conclusion.
7. **Sign-off.**

## cat12-29.2 Planning the PAT year (about 6 min)

**Goes:** after section `#year` - the comment after `mw29Plan`.
**The pupil can afterwards:** use the assessment tool as a checklist, keep evidence and work honestly, and plan the year backwards from the deadlines.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · THE PAT`, title "Count the days in *January*"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:30).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. Thabo's plan: send the questionnaire out in the last week of Term 2. Phase 2 is due that Friday.
   > Board: the countdown-cal drawing showing 5 days; Clicky gasping.
2. **The assessment tool (0:30-2:00).** A rubric set outside the school; teacher marks, subject advisor samples, province moderates. Read it before each phase; turn each line into a to-do; tick it off against your draft; ask in time; read the feedback.
   > Board: a rubric page becoming a to-do list; ticks appear.
3. **Evidence and honesty (2:00-3:30).** Evidence of the process: keep every draft, the raw data; back up in two places. The declaration of authenticity. AI only as your teacher allows - the conclusions must be your own.
   > Board: the final-final drawing, then a row of numbered drafts; the consent-box drawing for the declaration.
4. **The year (3:30-5:20).** Term 1: task, tool, folders, questions, sources - hand in Phase 1. Term 2: questionnaire out early, process before the mid-year exams - hand in Phase 2. Term 3: the report - hand in Phase 3 before the prelim. Habits: a fixed hour a week, reference as you go, save in the right folder, use the feedback. Paper 2's Question 6 asks about the PAT.
   > Board: a year line with three flags; tasks pinned between them.
5. **Sign-off.**
