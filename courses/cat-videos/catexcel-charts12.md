## catexcel-26.1 Stacked columns, the axis and the labels (about 8 min)

**Goes:** after the prose block "The scale on the axis" (section `#axes`).
**The pupil can afterwards:** choose a chart for a scenario, make a stacked
column chart, set an axis's minimum, maximum and major unit, add axis titles
and re-label the axis.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · SPREADSHEETS`, title "The *right* chart"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:40).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. The same six months
   of recycling, drawn three ways - one tells the story, two do not.
   > Board: Clicky judging three charts.
2. **Which chart (0:40-1:40).** Compare, over time, shares, parts and totals.
   > Board: the table of jobs and charts.
3. **A stacked column (1:40-3:00).** Select A3:D9; Insert > Insert Column or
   Bar Chart > Stacked Column. 100% stacked shows shares.
   > Screen: s-1, s-2 (the gallery), s-3.
4. **The axis (3:00-5:00).** Double-click the axis; Bounds Minimum and
   Maximum; Units Major. Start a column chart at 0 - a chart that starts at
   150 lies.
   > Screen: a-1, a-2. Board: the same two bars with the axis starting at 0 and at 150.
5. **Titles and labels (5:00-7:20).** Add Chart Element > Axis Titles; Select
   Data > Horizontal (Category) Axis Labels > Edit.
   > Screen: a-3, a-4, a-5, a-6.
6. **Sign-off (7:20-7:50).**

## catexcel-26.2 A chart made of pictures (about 4 min)

**Goes:** after the prose block "A chart made of pictures" (section
`#pictograph`).
**The pupil can afterwards:** fill a series with a picture, stacked and
scaled to a number of units per picture.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · SPREADSHEETS`, title "One bottle = *100 kg*"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:30).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. The back row of the
   assembly cannot read numbers. They can count bottles.
2. **Picture fill (0:30-2:20).** Double-click the columns; Fill & Line >
   Fill > Picture or texture fill > Insert > From a file.
   > Screen: p-2.
3. **Stack and Scale (2:20-3:30).** Stretch against Stack against Stack and
   Scale with 100 units per picture; say what a picture is worth.
   > Screen: p-1. Board: a stretched bottle beside a stack of bottles.
4. **Sign-off (3:30-4:00).**

| picture fill, Stack and Scale, Units/Picture | `#pictograph` |
