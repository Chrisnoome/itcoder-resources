# catword lesson 3: Formatting characters - videos

Lesson: `content/catword/fonts.php` (Grade 10). Screen recordings in the
CAT VM (Word 365), starting from Specials.docx (G:\My Drive\CAT\Word).
Never show the title bar's account. Board scenes in the CAT marker style
with Clicky; yellow highlighter on what is talked about.

## catword-03.1 Fonts, sizes, colours and effects (about 9 min)

**Goes:** after the match `m3Effects` in section `#effects` (the comment
`// VIDEO catword-03.1`).
**The pupil can afterwards:** change the font, size, style and colour of
selected text, highlight it, use strikethrough, subscript and superscript,
and use every effect in the Font dialog box.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · WORD`, title "Make the *letters* work"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:40).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. Mr Botha's
   specials: all the same font, size and black. Nobody stops.
   > Board: a plain flyer on the bakery door; people walking past.
2. **Select first (0:40-1:10).** Character formatting changes only what is selected.
3. **Fonts (1:10-2:40).** Serif and sans serif; one or two fonts per
   document. The Font box: type Arial Black, Enter.
   > Board: a serif letter with its "feet" highlighted.
4. **Size (2:40-3:30).** Points; 72 pt is about 2.5 cm; type 28; Grow Font
   and Shrink Font.
5. **Bold, italic, underline, colour, highlight (3:30-5:00).** Ctrl+B/I/U;
   the Underline arrow; Font Color and Text Highlight Color - the button
   uses the colour on its bar, the arrow chooses another; Automatic and No Color.
6. **Effects (5:00-7:30).** Strikethrough on R60; superscript th in 25th;
   subscript in H2O; Text Effects for a poster. The Font dialog box
   (launcher, Ctrl+D): each of the seven effects ticked in turn and watched
   in the Preview - strikethrough, double strikethrough, superscript,
   subscript, small caps, all caps, hidden. OK and Cancel.
7. **Sign-off.**

## catword-03.2 Change Case and the Format Painter (about 5 min)

**Goes:** after the simulation `simPainter` in section `#painter` (the
comment `// VIDEO catword-03.2`).
**The pupil can afterwards:** fix capitals with Change Case or Shift+F3,
copy formatting with the Format Painter (once or many times), and clear
formatting.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · WORD`, title "Copy the *look*"

### Scenes

1. **Change Case (0:00-2:00).** PLEASE ORDER BY THURSDAY. - shouting.
   Aa > Sentence case., then the capital T of Thursday back. lowercase,
   UPPERCASE, Capitalize Each Word, tOGGLE cASE. Shift+F3.
   > Board: the caps-shout doodle.
2. **Format Painter (2:00-4:00).** Click in Chelsea buns, click the brush,
   click Koeksisters. Double-click the brush, paint three places, Esc.
3. **Clear All Formatting (4:00-4:40).**
4. **Sign-off.**
