Lesson file: `AIPascalCourse/content/cattheory10/reports.php`. Board drawn in

## cat10-36.1 From answers to a chart (about 8 min)

**Goes:** after section `#charts` (the comment `// VIDEO cat10-36.1`).
**The pupil can afterwards:** lay out survey answers in a spreadsheet, answer the five data questions, and choose and label the right chart.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · PAT`, title "120 rows. *One* chart."

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:40).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. 120 pupils answered
   Thabo's questionnaire. Nobody will read 120 rows.
2. **One row per person (0:40-2:00).** Online forms fill the spreadsheet for
   you. One row per person, one column per question, a heading row. No names.
   Type every answer the same way - "WA" is a different app. Garbage in,
   garbage out.
   > Screen: in the VM, Excel with the six-response table from the lesson; highlight row 2, then column C.
3. **Five data questions (2:00-4:30).** Worked on the six responses: how many
   have a smartphone (5); most popular app (WhatsApp, 3); least common
   (Google Classroom and YouTube, 1 each); how many more WhatsApp than YouTube
   (2); average hours (24 / 6 = 4).
   > Screen: type =COUNTIF(B2:B7,"Yes") and =AVERAGE(D2:D7) in the VM; the answers 5 and 4 appear. (Functions named only - the Excel course teaches them.)
4. **Spreadsheet or database (4:30-5:00).** Spreadsheet: calculate and chart.
   Database: keep and find very many records.
5. **Which chart (5:00-6:40).** Column or bar: compare groups. Pie: parts of
   one whole. Line: change over time.
   > Board: three small charts drawn side by side; Clicky points at each.
6. **Every chart needs (6:40-7:30).** Title, axis titles, labels, legend when
   needed - shown on Thabo's column chart (WhatsApp 48, Google Classroom 36,
   YouTube 24, Other 12).
   > Screen: Insert > Column chart on the 120-pupil summary in the VM; add the title, axis titles and data labels.
7. **Sign-off.**

## cat10-36.2 The parts of a report (about 6 min)

**Goes:** after section `#present` (the comment `// VIDEO cat10-36.2`, after `s36Present`).
**The pupil can afterwards:** tell a finding from a conclusion and a recommendation, list a report's parts in order, and write a Harvard citation.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · PAT`, title "Findings, conclusions, *what now?*"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:30).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. Ms Naidoo's question:
   should the school change its phone rule? The chart is not the answer yet.
2. **Finding, conclusion, recommendation (0:30-2:00).** Thabo's three, from
   the lesson. Every recommendation must come from a finding.
   > Board: three stairs; the lightbulb drawing on the top step.
3. **The six parts (2:00-3:40).** Title page, table of contents,
   introduction, body (grouped findings with charts), conclusion, references.
   > Board: six stacked pages, labelled one at a time.
4. **Referencing (3:40-5:00).** Citation in the text: (Pillay, 2025).
   Reference list at the end, alphabetical; the book and web page patterns;
   pictures need sources too.
   > Board: the reference-anatomy drawing; highlighter on surname and year.
5. **Presenting it (5:00-5:40).** A slide per finding with its chart, a few
   short points, the recommendations.
6. **Sign-off.**
