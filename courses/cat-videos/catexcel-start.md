## catexcel-01.1 Your first look at Excel (about 7 min)

**Goes:** in the study block's place - after the section `#sheets` and its
simulation (the comment sits after the study block).
**The pupil can afterwards:** say what a spreadsheet is for and why it beats
a calculator; name the ribbon (tabs, groups, buttons), the Name Box, the
Formula Bar, the column and row headings, the active cell, the sheet tabs,
the status bar and the zoom slider; give a cell's address; select a cell, a
column, a row and the whole sheet; jump to a cell with the Name Box; switch
sheets; use the View tab's views, Gridlines and Zoom.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · SPREADSHEETS`, title "Your first look at *Excel*"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:50).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. Every evening Mr
   Botha adds up his bakery's sales on a calculator. One wrong key and the
   total is wrong - and when bread goes up, every sum starts again. A
   spreadsheet remembers the numbers, does the sums, and does them again by
   itself when a number changes.
   > Board: Clicky on a calculator, paper strip curling to the floor; then a grid where one number changes and the total flips by itself.
2. **What it is for (0:50-1:40).** Prices and takings, a class's marks, a
   budget, a survey for the PAT. Three reasons: it remembers, it
   recalculates, it shows (tables, charts).
   > Board: three sticky notes: remembers / recalculates / shows.
3. **The window, top to bottom (1:40-3:40).** The ribbon: tabs, groups (name
   underneath), buttons - Home > Font > Bold. The Name Box (A1), the Formula
   Bar (Item). Column letters, row numbers, the grid. The sheet tabs and +.
   The status bar: Ready, the three view buttons, the zoom slider. The
   Search box at the top finds any button; F1 for Help.
   > Screen: highlighter on each part in turn as it is named; click Home, then View, then Home.
4. **Addresses and the active cell (3:40-4:40).** Column letter, then row
   number: brown bread's price is B3. Click B3 - the Name Box says B3, the
   Formula Bar 17. Arrow keys, Enter (down), Tab (right), Ctrl+Home (A1).
   > Screen: click B3; highlighter on the Name Box and the Formula Bar; arrows, Enter, Tab, Ctrl+Home with a key overlay.
5. **Selecting (4:40-5:40).** Click the letter C - the whole column. Click
   row number 3. Drag over B2:D6. The corner square, or Ctrl+A, for all. Click
   in the Name Box, type D20, Enter.
   > Screen: each selection in turn; the Name Box jump.
6. **Sheets and views (5:40-6:40).** Click Specials. View tab: Normal, Page
   Layout, Page Break Preview; Show - untick Gridlines, Formula Bar,
   Headings and tick them again; Zoom and the slider. Nothing in the cells
   changes.
   > Screen: Specials tab; View tab; Page Layout and back; Gridlines off and on; the zoom slider dragged.
7. **Sign-off.**

## catexcel-01.2 Saving a workbook in Google Drive (about 5 min)

**Goes:** after the section `#save` (the comment sits after the study block,
with 01.1).
**The pupil can afterwards:** say the difference between Save and Save As;
use Ctrl+N, Ctrl+O, Ctrl+S, F12 and Ctrl+W; give reasons to keep CAT files in
the cloud; choose a good file name; save a workbook into a CAT folder in
Google Drive (and say where OneDrive is instead).
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · SPREADSHEETS`, title "Save it in the *cloud*"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:40).** Load shedding at 18:00. Thabo's budget was never
   saved - it was only in memory. Gone.
   > Board: a laptop screen going dark, Clicky with a candle; "Book1 - not saved" on a sticky note.
2. **Save or Save As (0:40-1:40).** Save (Ctrl+S): same name, same place.
   Save As (F12): a copy with a new name or place; the old file stays. New
   (Ctrl+N), Open (Ctrl+O), Close (Ctrl+W).
   > Board: two folders; Save As drawn as a photocopier making a second file.
3. **Why the cloud (1:40-2:30).** Google Drive or OneDrive: a stolen laptop
   or a dead drive does not take the work; open it anywhere; share with a
   link. Google Drive for desktop shows up as Google Drive (G:) with My
   Drive; OneDrive is in the same list, and AutoSave saves every change.
   > Board: a cloud above a school computer, a phone and a home laptop, arrows to all three.
4. **A good name (2:30-3:10).** Says what is in it: Botha prices, Budget
   October 2026. Never Book1, asdf, final FINAL 2. Excel adds .xlsx.
   > Board: a row of bad names crossed out, a good one ticked.
5. **Doing it (3:10-4:30).** Press F12. The box opens in My Drive (or click
   Google Drive (G:) on the left, then My Drive). Double-click CAT. Type
   Botha prices. Save. Then change a price and press Ctrl+S.
   > Screen: F12 (key overlay); highlighter on the address bar; double-click CAT; type the name; Save; change B2, Ctrl+S. Cover the OneDrive entry with the VM account's name.
6. **Sign-off.**
