## catdb-08.1 AND, OR and NOT in the design grid (about 8 min)

**Goes:** after the study block.
**The pupil can afterwards:** join criteria with AND (one row) and OR (rows);
write ranges with Between and >= And <=; use Or and In in one box; apply the row
rule; use NOT; sort on two fields; (CAPS) make a calculated field.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · DATABASES`, title "AND across, OR *down*"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:40).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. The coach never asks a
   simple question. Ask a muddled one, get a muddled answer.
2. **AND (0:40-2:10).** "F" and 11 on one row; every AND shortens the list;
   Between 12 And 13.5.
   > Board: a fine sieve. Screen: qryGirls11 - six records.
3. **OR (2:10-3:40).** "Red" on Criteria, "Blue" on or; "Red" Or "Blue"; In;
   the "Red" And "Blue" mistake - nobody.
   > Screen: qryWrongOr's empty answer, then the fixed one.
4. **AND and OR together (3:40-5:20).** The row rule: AND across, OR down; what
   must always be true goes on every row.
   > Board: the two-row grid, with the second "F" highlighted.
5. **NOT (5:20-6:00).** Not "Relay"; <>"Relay".
6. **Two sorts (6:00-6:40).** Left to right; moving a column.
7. **CAPS: a calculated field (6:40-7:40).** Balance: 2500-[Deposit]; brackets;
   Format Currency.
8. **Sign-off.**
