## catpowerpoint-04.1 Transitions and animation - and when to leave them out (about 8 min)

**Goes:** after section `#timing` (before `#helps`).
**The pupil can afterwards:** add a transition to one slide or all, set its duration and advance time, add an entrance, emphasis or exit animation, set when it starts, and judge when movement helps.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · POWERPOINT`, title "Move it - *or not*"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:40).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. Something moves, and
   every eye goes to it. That is a tool - and a trap.
   > Board: a slide with spinning words; Clicky with its eyes spinning too.
2. **Transitions (0:40-2:30).** Transitions tab > Fade; it plays once; Apply
   To All; the small star next to each thumbnail. Duration; Advance Slide -
   On Mouse Click, After 00:08.00 for Mr Botha's TV.
   > Screen: the whole path; highlighter on Apply To All and After.
3. **Animations (2:30-4:30).** Select the star; Animations > Fly In; the number
   1. The four kinds by colour: entrance, emphasis, exit, motion path.
   Effect Options; a list one point at a time; Add Animation.
   > Screen: animate the star, then a list with Fly In, Effect Options > From Left.
4. **The order (4:30-5:20).** The Animation Pane; drag to reorder; Move
   Earlier and Move Later.
5. **When it starts (5:20-6:30).** Start: On Click, With Previous, After
   Previous. Nobody clicks the TV - After Previous. Duration and Delay.
   > Screen: the Start list; play the slide to show the difference.
6. **When it helps (6:30-7:40).** Helps: one point at a time, steps, the one
   thing to notice. Gets in the way: a new effect every time, slow effects,
   sounds. One transition, animation only where it helps understanding.
   > Board: two columns, a green tick and a red cross; highlighter on "only where it helps".
7. **Sign-off.**
