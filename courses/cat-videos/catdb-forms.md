## catdb-06.1 Making and using a form (about 9 min)

**Goes:** after the study block.
**The pupil can afterwards:** make a form with Form, the Form Wizard and Form
Design; switch between Form, Layout and Design View; name the three sections;
add fields, a title, a logo, the date, a picture, a background image and a
button with the Command Button Wizard; format fonts and numbers; move fields;
enter records; (IEB) set the tab order.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · DATABASES`, title "A *form* Gogo can use"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:40).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. Gogo does not want a
   seven-column datasheet - one member at a time, big and clear.
   > Board: Gogo squinting at a datasheet; then smiling at a neat form.
2. **What a form is (0:40-1:30).** A window onto a table; the data stays in
   tblMembers; a good form is designed for its user.
3. **Three ways to make one (1:30-3:30).** Create > Form (one click); the Form
   Wizard (fields in order, Columnar, title); Form Design.
   > Screen: Form on tblMembers; then the Form Wizard all the way to Finish.
4. **Views and sections (3:30-4:40).** Form, Layout, Design; Form Header,
   Detail, Form Footer.
5. **Controls (4:40-6:40).** Label vs text box; Add Existing Fields; Title, Logo,
   Date and Time; Insert Image; Format > Background Image; a button with the
   Command Button Wizard (Record Operations > Add New Record); the Property Sheet.
6. **Formatting and order (6:40-7:30).** Format tab; number formats; dragging
   fields in the layout.
7. **Entering records (7:30-8:20).** New (blank) record; Tab; saving.
8. **IEB: the tab order (8:20-8:50).** Form Design > Tab Order; Auto Order.
9. **Sign-off.**
