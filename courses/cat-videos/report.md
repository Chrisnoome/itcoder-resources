# CAT 12 lesson 23: Writing a professional report - videos

Lesson: `content/cattheory12/report.php`. Two videos: from findings to
recommendations, and a professional report in Word. Board in the CAT marker
style with Clicky (brand/cat-art-style.md); yellow highlighter on any words
on screen being talked about. The second video is mostly a screen recording
in Word in the VM, on a short sample report (Thabo's water report - made-up
text, about four pages, headings typed in bold at the start). Everything
below is in the lesson text.

## cat12-23.1 From findings to recommendations (about 7 min)

**Goes:** after section `#fcr` - the comment after `r23Overreach`.
**The pupil can afterwards:** write findings, conclusions and recommendations that hold up, and spot a conclusion that claims too much or a recommendation nobody can act on.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · REPORTS`, title "So what *should* we do?"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:40).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. Thabo's report is going to the School Governing Body. If they believe it, they spend real money. What makes them believe it?
   > Board: a boardroom table, a report on it; Clicky in a tie.
2. **Formal and objective (0:40-1:40).** Facts, not feelings; numbers instead of "lots" and "everyone knows". Two sentences side by side.
   > Board: "I was shocked by the toilets!" crossed out; "70% of pupils said..." with a tick.
3. **A finding that holds up (1:40-2:20).** A fact, usually a number, with its source.
   > Board: "4 000 litres a night" with an arrow to the meter.
4. **A conclusion that holds up (2:20-4:00).** Answers the main question; rests on more than one finding; claims no more than the data shows (one school, not all schools); together is not cause (bottled water and taps running dry).
   > Board: the data-crystal-ball doodle beside "all South African schools", crossed out.
5. **A recommendation that holds up (4:00-5:30).** Comes from a conclusion; specific - what, who, by when; practical - affordable. "The school must save water" against the plumber recommendation.
   > Board: Thabo's chain table built row by row, highlighter on "R2 400" and "06:00 every Monday".
6. **The thread (5:30-6:30).** Problem statement, sub-questions, body sections, conclusions, recommendations - pull any one and follow it back. Nothing new in the conclusion (the Wi-Fi example).
   > Board: a thread through five boxes.
7. **Sign-off.**

## cat12-23.2 A professional report in Word (about 9 min)

**Goes:** after section `#look` - the comment after `w23Styles`.
**The pupil can afterwards:** use heading styles, an automatic table of contents, captions, page numbers, citations and a linked chart, and check a report before handing it in.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · REPORTS`, title "Make it look *professional*"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:40).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. Five fonts, bold headings, no page numbers. Readers judge a report before they read a word.
   > Screen: the sample report scrolled quickly in Word.
2. **Heading styles (0:40-2:00).** Click each bold heading, apply Heading 1 or 2; open the navigation pane.
   > Screen: Home, Styles, Heading 1; View, Navigation Pane. Highlighter on the Styles gallery.
3. **Automatic contents (2:00-3:00).** References, Table of Contents; add a heading, right-click, Update Field.
   > Screen: exactly those clicks.
4. **Captions and a linked chart (3:00-4:40).** References, Insert Caption under a chart: "Figure 2: Water used each night". Copy a chart in Excel, Paste Special, Paste link; change a number in Excel and watch the chart update. Source line under it.
   > Screen: Excel and Word side by side.
5. **Page numbers and breaks (4:40-5:30).** Insert, Page Number, bottom of page; title in the header; a page break before each main part.
   > Screen: those clicks.
6. **Citations (5:30-7:00).** References, Insert Citation, Add New Source (a web page); Manage Sources; Bibliography; Update Citations and Bibliography. The et al. rule and "cited in" said aloud.
   > Screen: the Create Source dialog filled in for Johannesburg Water (2026a).
7. **The final check (7:00-8:20).** Spelling and grammar set to English (South Africa); read it through ("form" for "from"); someone else reads it; update everything; save a PDF.
   > Screen: Review, Spelling and Grammar; File, Save As, PDF.
8. **Sign-off.**
