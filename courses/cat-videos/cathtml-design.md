# Web Design (HTML) lesson 5: Planning and building a page - video

Lesson: `AIPascalCourse/content/cathtml/design.php`. Screen recording in the
**CAT VM `itcoder-cat`** (Notepad++ and Edge). Board scenes in the CAT marker

## cathtml-05.1 Colour, fonts and lines: attributes (about 8 min)

**Goes:** after the quiz in section `#attributes` (the comment
`// VIDEO cathtml-05.1`).
**The pupil can afterwards:** plan a page on paper, follow the main rules of
good design, and use bgcolor, font (color, face, size) and hr (width, size).
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · HTML`, title "Plan it, *colour* it"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:40).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. Mr Botha wants his
   home page to look like a bakery, not a school report. First a plan, then
   the colours.
   > Board: Clicky with a paper sketch of the page: boxes and lines.
2. **Plan (0:40-2:00).** Purpose, readers, content, a sketch, the tags next to
   each part. Build in order: skeleton, then parts, then looks; save and
   refresh after each step.
   > Board: the sketch with h1, p, hr, h2 written in its margin.
3. **Good design (2:00-3:30).** Most important first; headings; short
   paragraphs; few colours with contrast; bold for a few words; no underline;
   one or two fonts. Gogo's red-on-dark-blue page next to a fixed one.
   > Board: the two versions side by side; a tick and a cross.
4. **Attributes (3:30-6:30).** name="value" in the opening tag. body
   bgcolor="lightyellow"; font color, face, size 1-7; hr width (percent or
   pixels) and size. Several in one tag. American spelling: type colour and
   watch nothing happen - no error, no colour. Quotes; attributes never in
   the closing tag.
   > Screen: add each attribute in Notepad++, save, refresh; then the colour spelling mistake.
5. **Building the page (6:30-7:30).** A quick time-lapse of Mr Botha's whole
   home page from the plan - the final task in the lesson.
   > Screen: the page built in steps, Edge refreshed after each.
6. **Sign-off.**
