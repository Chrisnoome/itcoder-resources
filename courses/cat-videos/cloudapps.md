# CAT 11 lesson 6: Cloud applications and working together - videos

Lesson file: `AIPascalCourse/content/cattheory11/cloudapps.php`. Two videos.
Everything said or shown here is also in the lesson text (anchors in the
tables below). Screen recordings in the VM (Windows 11, Edge, a test
Microsoft account with Word for the web; a second test account in a second
browser window for the co-authoring shots).

## cat11-06.1 Cloud applications - Word in a browser (about 6 min)

**Goes:** after the reveal `r6Offline`, at the end of section `#suites`
(before `#sharing`).
**The pupil can afterwards:** say what a cloud application is, compare it
with an installed application, name the two online office suites (with their
old names), open Word for the web, and give advantages and disadvantages
(and a limitation).
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · SOFTWARE`, title "Word with *nothing* installed"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:40).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. Thabo's group: one
   report, four pupils, seven e-mailed copies, and nobody knows which one
   is right. Lerato: "Put one copy online, share it, and all four of you
   work in it."
   > Board: seven document icons named report, report_v2, report_FINAL...
   > piling up; Clicky buried under them.
2. **What a cloud application is (0:40-1:50).** Grade 10: the cloud is
   someone else's computers; a web app runs in a browser. A cloud
   application runs on the company's servers, used over the Internet in a
   browser or an app, work saved online. Thabo starts at school, finishes on
   Lerato's laptop. Paid by subscription: software as a service.
   > Board: a laptop and a phone, both with arrows to a data centre drawn as
   > a building with fans; yellow band on "runs on the company's servers".
3. **Installed or cloud (1:50-2:50).** The six rows: where it runs, where
   work is saved, getting it, without the Internet, updates, an example.
   > Board: the Learn / Memorise table, two columns filled row by row.
4. **The suites (2:50-3:40).** Microsoft 365 (once Office 365) and Google
   Workspace (once G Suite) - the renaming in 2020. Free web versions;
   school plans.
   > Board: two boxes of app icons; the old names crossed out in marker.
5. **Opening Word for the web (3:40-4:40).** microsoft365.com, sign in,
   Word, Blank document, type - AutoSave to OneDrive; rename at the top.
   > Screen: VM - exactly these steps; yellow highlighter on "Saved" at the
   > top as AutoSave works.
6. **Good and bad (4:40-5:50).** Any device; nothing to install; saved as
   you type; easy to share and work together; a cheap laptop is enough. No
   Internet (or a power cut) - no program; data costs; files on someone
   else's servers; one stolen password; the subscription never ends. A
   limitation is built in (needs the Internet, fewer features); a
   disadvantage is a cost or risk.
   > Board: a see-saw with advantages and disadvantages; the router going
   > dark in a power cut (cloud-no-signal).
7. **Sign-off.**

## cat11-06.2 Sharing, comments and version history (about 7 min)

**Goes:** after the dragwords `dw6Together`, at the end of section
`#together` (before the Good to Know `#google`).
**The pupil can afterwards:** share a document with the right permission,
explain the risk of "anyone with the link", and use co-authoring, comments
and version history; say what collaboration software does.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · SOFTWARE`, title "One document, *four* people"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:30).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. Sending a copy
   versus sharing one copy: the e-mailed copies multiply; the shared one
   stays one.
   > Board: the email-copies-vs-shared drawing, built left then right.
2. **Why share a link (0:30-1:20).** One up-to-date copy; everyone's
   changes in one file; big files too; sharing can be taken away. When a
   copy is still right: a signed letter, no Internet.
   > Board: a list ticked off one line at a time.
3. **Sharing, step by step (1:20-2:30).** Share; the e-mail addresses; Can
   edit or Can view; Send - or Copy link.
   > Screen: VM - Word for the web, Share, add a test address, the
   > permission drop-down, Send; then Copy link.
4. **Permissions and links (2:30-3:40).** Viewer, Commenter, Editor, Owner
   with who gets which. Only the people you choose vs anyone with the link -
   links get forwarded. The least access needed.
   > Board: four badges; the anyone-link drawing, the link escaping into a
   > WhatsApp group.
5. **Co-authoring (3:40-4:30).** Two windows side by side: names at the
   top, coloured markers, changes appearing within seconds.
   > Screen: VM - the two test accounts typing in one document.
6. **Comments (4:30-5:30).** Select; Review, New Comment; @name sends an
   e-mail; Post; Reply; Resolve.
   > Screen: VM - exactly these steps; yellow highlighter on the @mention.
7. **Version history (5:30-6:30).** Someone deleted Thandi's page. File,
   Info, Version History; versions newest first with names and times; open
   one; Restore (or copy the page across).
   > Screen: VM - delete a paragraph, then bring it back through Version
   > History. Board: the who-changed-it drawing.
8. **Collaboration software (6:30-6:50).** Teams, Google Classroom: shared
   files, chat, meetings, tasks. Ms Naidoo's class Team.
   > Board: one box holding four icons.
9. **Sign-off.**
