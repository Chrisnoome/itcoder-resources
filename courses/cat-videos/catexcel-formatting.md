## catexcel-04.1 Making a sheet easy to read (about 6 min)

**Goes:** after the study block (the comment sits there with 04.2).
**The pupil can afterwards:** select, then format; use the Font group (font,
size, bold, italic, underline, colours); the Alignment group (left, centre,
right, top, middle, bottom, Wrap Text, Orientation, Merge & Center and
Unmerge); borders and fill; Cell Styles and Format as Table.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · SPREADSHEETS`, title "Same numbers, *better* look"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:40).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. Mr Botha's new
   price list has the right numbers - and 0.083333 for an increase, 46082 for
   a date. Formatting changes how they look, never what they are.
   > Board: two price lists side by side, a scruffy one and a neat one, the same numbers circled in both.
2. **Select, then format (0:40-1:20).** Formula Bar check: the number does
   not change. Ctrl+1 opens Format Cells.
   > Screen: click a cell, highlighter on the Formula Bar.
3. **The title (1:20-2:40).** Select A1:E1, Merge & Center, Ctrl+B, Increase
   Font Size. Unmerge Cells on the button's arrow. Merge only for titles.
   > Screen: each button, highlighter on it first.
4. **Headings (2:40-4:00).** Fill Color (the paint tin), Borders (the
   square), the arrows for more choices; borders print, gridlines do not.
   Wrap Text for a long heading; Orientation turns it.
   > Screen: each in turn; File > Print preview showing a border and no gridlines.
5. **Styles (4:00-5:20).** Cell Styles: Title, Heading 1, Total, Good, Bad.
   Format as Table: banded rows and filter buttons in one go.
   > Screen: the gallery with live preview; Format as Table on the data.
   > Board: Clicky with a rubber stamp marked "Heading 1".
6. **Sign-off.**

## catexcel-04.2 Number formats and ###### (about 6 min)

**Goes:** after the study block, with 04.1.
**The pupil can afterwards:** choose General, Number, Currency, Accounting,
Percentage, Short and Long Date, Time and Text; use Accounting, Percent
Style, Comma Style and Increase/Decrease Decimal; say why fewer decimals
only change what shows; fix ###### with AutoFit.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · SPREADSHEETS`, title "Why does my cell say *######*?"

### Scenes

1. **Rands (0:00-1:30).** Select B4:C8, Accounting Number Format: R and two
   decimals. Currency in the Number Format box puts the R next to the number;
   Accounting lines the Rs up (IEB pupils: know the difference).
   > Screen: both formats side by side, highlighter on the Rs.
2. **Percentages and decimals (1:30-3:00).** D4:D8: Percent Style, then
   Increase Decimal: 8.3%. The cell still holds 0.083333 - the Formula Bar
   proves it; formulas use the whole number.
   > Screen: the buttons; the Formula Bar highlighted.
   > Board: an iceberg - "8.3%" above the water, "0.0833333..." below.
3. **Dates (3:00-4:30).** E4:E8, Ctrl+1, Date, 14 March 2012, OK: ######.
   Not an error - the column is too narrow. Double-click the line on the
   right of E's heading: AutoFit. Or drag; or Format > Column Width.
   > Screen: the dialog; the ######; the double-click (zoomed).
4. **Sign-off.**
