## cathtml-11.1 One website, not four pages: design and colour (about 8 min)

**Goes:** the comment `// VIDEO cathtml-11.1`, after the written question in `#checklist`.
**The pupil can afterwards:** plan a website, keep its pages consistent, choose colours with contrast, and make text, pictures and links easy to use.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · HTML`, title "Make it look like *one* website"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:40).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. Mr Botha's four pages look like four different bakeries.
   > Board: four mismatched pages side by side, Clicky scratching his head.
2. **Plan the site (0:40-2:00).** Purpose and readers, pages, a site map, index.html and short names.
   > Board: the site map drawn as boxes and lines.
3. **Consistency (2:00-3:30).** The same logo, menu, colours and fonts on every page; a title that names site and page.
   > Screen: the cakes and pies pages in two Edge tabs, the same top.
4. **Colour (3:30-5:30).** Two or three colours; contrast; colours that fit; never colour alone; colour codes #RRGGBB. IEB: body text and hr color.
   > Board: yellow on white crossed out; brown on cream ticked; the code split into RR GG BB.
5. **Easy to read (5:30-7:00).** Lists, paragraphs on the left, no underlining but links; pictures that earn their place; link text, not click here. CAPS: cite.
   > Board: a screen reader's list of links: click here, click here, click here.
6. **The checklist (7:00-7:40).** Titles, the same top, every link, every picture, spelling, closed tags, a second browser.
   > Board: the checklist ticked off.
7. **Sign-off.**
