## cathtml-04.1 Bold, italic, underline - and finding the mistakes (about 8 min)

**Goes:** after the typed question in section `#bugs` (the comment
`// VIDEO cathtml-04.1`).
**The pupil can afterwards:** use b, i and u on exactly the right words, nest
bold and italic properly, and find mistakes by reading the code with a
checklist.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · HTML`, title "Find the *mistakes*"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:40).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. Mr Botha's specials
   page looks fine in the browser. Under the hood, it has four mistakes.
   > Board: Clicky lifting the bonnet of a car with a web page painted on it.
2. **b, i, u (0:40-2:00).** Bold, italic, underline; tags round only the
   words to change, inside the paragraph; underlined words look like links -
   use u rarely.
   > Screen: `<b>Koeksisters</b> - R8 each` typed, saved, refreshed.
3. **Nesting (2:00-3:30).** Bold and italic: the last opened closes first.
   Brackets in maths: ( [ ] ), never ( [ ) ]. Either one may go outside.
   Formatting inside the paragraph, not round it.
   > Board: two pairs of brackets nested, then crossed and crossed out.
4. **The checklist (3:30-6:30).** Spelt right; both angle brackets; closed;
   forward slash and same name; nested properly; in the right place. Walk
   the broken page line by line: titel, the crossed tags, the b never closed
   (the bold running on in the browser), /bodi. Click a tag - Notepad++ lights
   up its partner, or nothing.
   > Screen: the page from the lesson's figure, each mistake highlighted and fixed, save, refresh after each.
5. **Sign-off.** In the lesson: fix the specials, and format the market day
   page - only the words asked for.
