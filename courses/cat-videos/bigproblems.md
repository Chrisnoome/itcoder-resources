# CAT 12 lesson 20: Technology and the world's big problems - videos

Lesson: `content/cattheory12/bigproblems.php`. Two videos: computers against
disease and disaster, and the planet and the gap (green computing and the
digital divide). Board in the CAT marker style with Clicky
(brand/cat-art-style.md); yellow highlighter on any words on screen being
talked about. The KZN floods are spoken of calmly - people died. Everything
below is in the lesson text.

## cat12-20.1 Computers against disease and disaster (about 8 min)

**Goes:** after section `#disasters` - the comment after `q20Reach`.
**The pupil can afterwards:** name the five things computers bring to a big problem, explain telemedicine and how Omicron was found, describe an early warning system, and say why a warning can still fail.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · SOCIETY`, title "A warning is only the *first* link"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:40).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. April 2022: days of rain on KwaZulu-Natal, more than 400 people dead. Computers saw the rain coming. So why did so many people not get out?
   > Board: rain clouds over hills and a river; a computer screen with a forecast; Clicky looking worried.
2. **Five things computers bring (0:40-1:50).** Collect, share, find patterns, predict, reach.
   > Board: five verbs in a column, each with a tiny icon; highlighter on each as it is said.
3. **Health (1:50-4:00).** Telemedicine: a clinic in Limpopo, a doctor in Johannesburg; photos of X-rays; monitors that send readings; SMS reminders - MomConnect. Electronic health records. AI screening X-rays for TB. Omicron: codes read and compared by computer, shared with the WHO on 24 November 2021. The limits: signal and power, no touch, privacy, AI trained elsewhere.
   > Board: a nurse and a doctor on two screens joined by a line; then a long string of letters with one block changing colour (the variant); highlighter on "24 November 2021".
4. **Before a disaster (4:00-5:50).** An early warning system: sensors, computers, a way to reach people. Colour-coded weather warnings; satellite fire alerts; earthquake warnings that race ahead of the shaking; tsunami sensors since 2004.
   > Board: the bigp-quake-phone doodle; three boxes SENSE - WORK OUT - WARN; yellow, orange and red cards.
5. **During and after (5:50-6:50).** Drones, maps and location sharing, WhatsApp and social media; satellite photos, databases, crowdfunding.
   > Board: a drone over a flooded road; a map with pins.
6. **Where it goes wrong (6:50-7:50).** The warning must reach people who can act; towers and power fail; fake news; people without a phone or the language.
   > Board: a chain of links with the last one broken - "people can act"; highlighter on it.
7. **Sign-off.**

## cat12-20.2 The planet and the gap - green computing and the digital divide (about 8 min)

**Goes:** after section `#divide` - the comment after `w20Divide`.
**The pupil can afterwards:** explain the harm a device does over its life and where its carbon footprint lies, say why data centres matter, explain planned obsolescence and the right to repair, recommend green measures in order, and explain the digital divide between and inside countries and who can close it.
**Thumbnail:** tag `CAT · SOCIETY`, title "The cloud is *thirsty*"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:30).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. Thabo says saving photos in the cloud is green. Is it?
   > Board: the green-cloud doodle; highlighter on "green?".
2. **The whole life of a device (0:30-1:50).** Mining, making, shipping, using, throwing away. The carbon footprint; most of it from making - the greenest phone is the one you have.
   > Board: the green-lifecycle drawing, stage by stage; highlighter on "making".
3. **Data centres (1:50-2:50).** Buildings full of servers; about 1.5% of the world's electricity in 2024, growing with AI; water for cooling; South Africa's coal.
   > Board: a windowless building with fans on the roof, a water drop and a coal heap beside it.
4. **Built to be replaced (2:50-3:50).** Planned obsolescence - glued batteries, special screws, updates that stop, a new model every year. The right to repair. E-waste: about 62 million tonnes in 2022, under a quarter recycled.
   > Board: the green-glue doodle; the green-drawer doodle; highlighter on "right to repair".
5. **What helps (3:50-5:00).** Computers help the planet: satellites, climate models, smart meters, less travel and paper. For an organisation, biggest wins first: keep and repair, refurbished (about R4 000), efficient, the cloud, solar, green disposal.
   > Board: a numbered list growing down the board, the first item circled.
6. **The digital divide (5:00-7:20).** Three levels (Grade 10). Between countries: about 38% online in Africa, about 91% in Europe (2024). Inside the country: rural, poor, old, disability, language. Services that go online only - the SRD grant, applications, jobs, bank branches. Who can close it: government, networks, businesses and NGOs, schools, you.
   > Board: the divide-gap drawing; two bars for Africa and Europe; a "online only" door with people left outside; the who-does-what table.
7. **Sign-off.**
