# Safety lesson 8: Case 08 - The big case - videos

Lesson file: `AIPascalCourse/content/safety/bigcase.php`. Two videos. The second works through
exhibits like the final assessment's but **not** the assessment's own four exhibits - don't
give away w8FinalAssessment.

## safety-08.1 The full findings form (about 5 min)

**Goes:** after the match `m8WhichCase` (end of section `#fullForm`).
**The pupil can afterwards:** use all four checks on any message, app or person.
**Thumbnail:** tag `SAFETY · CASE 08`, title "The *fourth* check"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:30).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. Seven cases closed. Now The Phisher
   tries everything at once.
   > Board: the case-wall figure - four exhibits pinned, strings running to the form.
2. **The four checks (0:30-3:30).** 1. Sender or who - short code or cell number, real
   profile or new; a right sender proves nothing. 2. Tone - fear, deadline, greed, flattery,
   secrecy, pressure. 3. Link or address - the domain name before the first single slash.
   4. What does it ask for? A password, PIN, code, card, money, a photo, a secret, a
   permission it doesn't need. The check that catches almost everything.
   > Board: the findings form, each row written in as it is explained; the fourth row
   > highlighted with a red ring.
3. **Then act (3:30-4:30).** Don't click, reply or send codes; check another way; change and
   lock; report and block; tell a trusted adult.
4. **Sign-off.**

## safety-08.2 The big case, solved (about 7 min)

**Goes:** after the order question `o8Strength` (end of section `#review`).
**The pupil can afterwards:** work a mixed set of exhibits end to end, and recall the seven
habits.
**Thumbnail:** tag `SAFETY · CASE 08`, title "Case *closed*, detective"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:30).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. Three exhibits from the case load.
   Let's solve them together, out loud.
2. **Exhibit 1 (0:30-2:00).** An SMS showing KasiBank: "Unusual login. If this wasn't you, log
   in at kasibank.co.za.web-secure.net". Sender - looks right, proves nothing. Tone - fear.
   Link - the owner is web-secure.net. Asks for - your login. Verdict: scam; open the bank's
   app yourself.
   > Board: the exhibit as a Polaroid; the form filled in row by row; SCAM stamp.
3. **Exhibit 2 (2:00-3:10).** A DM from kayla_14_xx, joined this week: "u seem so mature...
   chat somewhere private... don't tell anyone". New profile; flattery and secrecy; asks for
   trust and secrecy. Verdict: don't reply; screenshot, block, report, tell an adult.
4. **Exhibit 3 (3:10-4:10).** An order confirmation from ShopNow, for an order you made, no
   link, asks for nothing. Verdict: CLEARED. A detective checks - but doesn't suspect
   everyone.
   > Board: Sniff with the CLEARED stamp.
5. **The seven habits (4:10-6:20).** One line each: check every message; long passphrases,
   different per site, or a manager; the second lock, email first, never share a code; after a
   leak, change everywhere, lock, expect phishing; only needed permissions, free apps may cost
   your data; what you send can't be unsent, ask before sharing; secrets are the biggest red
   flag, tell early.
   > Board: seven case folders stacked, each stamped CLOSED as it is named.
6. **Ending (6:20-6:50).** The Phisher will keep trying - but now you'll see the bait for what
   it is. Case closed, detective.
   > Board: The Phisher behind bars - "case closed".
7. **Sign-off.**
