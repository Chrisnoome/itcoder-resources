# Safety lesson 6: Case 06 - The photo that travelled - videos

Lesson file: `AIPascalCourse/content/safety/footprint.php`. Two videos. **Awareness level**
(Chris, 9 October 2026): the law in plain words and who to tell; nothing explicit. Calm tone.

## safety-06.1 The photo that travelled (about 6 min)

**Goes:** after the quiz `q6Delete` (end of section `#travel`).
**The pupil can afterwards:** explain the digital footprint and why a sent photo can't be
unsent.
**Thumbnail:** tag `SAFETY · CASE 06`, title "Deleted. Still *everywhere*."

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:40).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. Friday: Musa sends a silly photo of
   Kabelo - mid-sneeze, spaghetti on his chin - to one friend. Monday: the whole grade has seen
   it, it's a sticker, Kabelo won't come to school. Musa deleted it on Saturday.
2. **Your digital footprint (0:40-2:10).** Every post, photo, comment, like, tag and profile -
   some yours, some others' about you. Unlike sand: it lasts, it spreads, it gets searched -
   schools, bursaries, coaches, bosses. A good footprint helps you too; leave the one you'd
   choose.
   > Board: the footprints doodle - like, post, tag, pic, comment... forever.
3. **How a photo travels (2:10-4:00).** One friend. A screenshot - two copies, one of them
   Musa's. A group of 40. Then nobody knows. Deleting reaches only his copy. Disappearing
   stories: a screenshot takes seconds.
   > Board: the photo-travel figure built hop by hop, the counter of copies rising; the
   > CAN'T UNSEND stamp.
4. **The test (4:00-5:00).** Before you send: would I be fine if my gran, my principal and a
   future boss saw this - for ever?
   > Board: three portraits pinned on the folder - Gran, the principal, a boss in a tie.
5. **Sign-off.**

## safety-06.2 What your photo knows (about 5 min)

**Goes:** after the written question `w6MusaAdvice` (end of section `#law`).
**The pupil can afterwards:** spot what a photo gives away, ask consent, and know the law and
where to get help.
**Thumbnail:** tag `SAFETY · CASE 06`, title "Your photo knows where you *live*"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:30).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. A photo is more than its picture.
2. **Metadata (0:30-1:40).** Hidden in the file: date, time, phone, often the exact place.
   Many apps strip location when you post - not all, and a photo sent as a file or by email
   usually keeps it. Switch off location for the camera in Settings.
   > Board: a Polaroid flipped over, its back covered in typed data: DATE, PHONE, GPS.
3. **What the picture gives away (1:40-2:40).** A school badge, a house number, a number
   plate, a view anyone local knows, "home alone this weekend!".
   > Board: Nandi's photo described as a drawing - dog, gate with "14", blazer badge - with
   > red rings round the giveaways.
4. **Other people's pictures (2:40-3:40).** Every person in a photo has a say: consent. Ask
   before posting or tagging; take it down if asked; don't forward an embarrassing photo -
   every forward is another copy. Laughing at someone with a photo is bullying.
5. **The law and who to tell (3:40-4:40).** In plain words: sharing someone's private,
   intimate photo without consent is a crime (the Cybercrimes Act); sexual pictures of anyone
   under 18, even of yourself, are a crime - if anyone asks, it is never OK and never your
   fault; threats can be crimes too. If it's happening: keep the evidence (screenshots), tell
   a trusted adult, report and block, phone Childline on 116.
   > Board: plain cards only, no illustration of the photos themselves. End on the help card:
   > TRUSTED ADULT · REPORT · CHILDLINE 116.
6. **Sign-off.**
