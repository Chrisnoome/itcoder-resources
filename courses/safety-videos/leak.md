# Safety lesson 4: Case 04 - The leak - videos

Lesson file: `AIPascalCourse/content/safety/leak.php`. Two videos.

## safety-04.1 One leak, every door (about 6 min)

**Goes:** after the reveal `r4OnlyEmails` (end of section `#theyKnowYou`).
**The pupil can afterwards:** explain a data breach, credential stuffing and spear phishing.
**Thumbnail:** tag `SAFETY · CASE 04`, title "She did *nothing* wrong today"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:40).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. Lerato didn't click anything today.
   But three years ago she joined GameZone. Last night GameZone was broken into. This morning,
   someone logged into her email.
   > Board: the newspaper doodle - Sniff reading "2 MILLION PASSWORDS LEAKED!".
2. **What a leak is (0:40-1:50).** Companies keep your email, password (scrambled, if done
   properly), name, phone, birthday. Criminals break in and copy it: a data breach. The list is
   sold or posted. Not your fault, and you can't stop it - you can keep the damage small. And
   POPIA says the company must tell you.
   > Board: the leaky bucket doodle, sticky notes spilling out.
3. **One key, every door (1:50-3:40).** Lerato's GameZone password was lerato2012 - and she
   used it for her email and school login. Scammers feed the stolen list to a program that
   tries every email and password on thousands of sites, all night: credential stuffing. Email
   - open. School - open. Bank app, different password - shut.
   > Board: the leak-list figure built up - the stolen list, Lerato's row ringed, then each
   > door card with its string and OPEN / SHUT.
4. **Scams that know your name (3:40-5:20).** "Dear customer" versus "Hi Lerato, about your
   GameZone account, phone ending 4471, because of the recent breach..." The second knows real
   things - all from the leak. Spear phishing: a spear aimed at one fish. Knowing your details
   is not proof. The checks still decide, and no company ever asks for your password.
   > Board: a fishing net over many fish, then a single spear at one fish labelled "Lerato".
5. **Sign-off.**

## safety-04.2 Were you in a leak? What to do next (about 5 min)

**Goes:** after the activity `scamSpotter` (end of section `#afterLeak`).
**The pupil can afterwards:** check for leaks safely and follow the five steps after one.
**Thumbnail:** tag `SAFETY · CASE 04`, title "Have I Been *Pwned*?"

### Scenes

1. **Hook (0:00-0:30).** Hi, and welcome to BestLessons. You can find out whether your email
   was in a leak - safely.
2. **Have I Been Pwned (0:30-2:00).** A free site run by security researcher Troy Hunt, with
   the lists from known breaches - billions of accounts. Type your email; it lists the leaks
   and what spilled. Pwned is gamer slang for owned. Three rules: with an adult the first time;
   only ever your email - never a password - in any "check if you were hacked" site; the real
   address, haveibeenpwned.com.
   > Board: a magnifying glass over an email address; three rule cards. Do not record the real
   > site with a real email; if a screen is wanted, show only the address bar and the empty
   > search box.
3. **The fake checker (2:00-2:40).** A pop-up that wants your email and password "to check" is
   the leak itself. Close it.
4. **After a leak, in order (2:40-4:30).** 1. Change that password, in the app or by typing
   the address - never from a link in an email about the leak. 2. Change it everywhere you used
   it - email first. 3. Switch on the second lock. 4. Expect phishing about that company for
   months. 5. Tell an adult - at once if a card or ID number leaked.
   > Board: five numbered cards pinned down the folder, a tick appearing on each.
5. **Sign-off.**
